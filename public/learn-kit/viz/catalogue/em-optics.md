# Electricity, magnetism and optics

Figures for electrostatics, circuits, magnetism, induction, AC, waves, optics and plasma physics (group `em-`). Class 12, JAM, GATE and NET lessons use them; NCERT pages can reuse any of them.

## Electrostatics and matter
- `@em-field-lines`: draggable point charges with field lines, equipotentials and arrows, and a pointer readout of V and E. Used in: engineering-entrance/electric-charges-fields, iit-jam-physics/electrostatics-gauss
- `@em-gauss-surface`: Gauss's law with a resizable sphere and draggable charges; flux summed numerically and compared with Q_enc/ε₀. Used in: engineering-entrance/electric-charges-fields, iit-jam-physics/electrostatics-gauss
- `@em-charged-bodies`: on-axis field of a ring, rod and disc against distance, with the point-charge and infinite line or sheet limits. Used in: engineering-entrance/electric-charges-fields, iit-jam-physics/electrostatics-gauss
- `@em-laplace-relax`: animated relaxation solving Laplace's equation for four electrode layouts, with an average-of-neighbours check. Used in: iit-jam-physics/conductors-images, gate-physics/boundary-value-problems
- `@em-image-steps`: step player for the method of images (conducting plane, image charge, equipotential check, induced charge and force). Used in: iit-jam-physics/conductors-images, gate-physics/method-of-images
- `@em-dielectric-polarise`: molecules in a dielectric slab (stretching or turning), bound surface charge, E = E₀/κ. Used in: engineering-entrance/potential-capacitance, iit-jam-physics/dielectrics, gate-physics/fields-in-matter
- `@em-cap-network`: two capacitors in series or parallel with charge, voltage and energy bars. Used in: engineering-entrance/potential-capacitance, iit-jam-physics/dielectrics
- `@em-multipole-angles`: exact potential of charges on an axis against the truncated multipole series, with l and r sliders. Used in: gate-physics/multipoles, gate-physics/boundary-value-problems

## Circuits
- `@em-rc-flow`: animated RC charging and discharging with moving charge carriers and the V_C and I curves. Used in: engineering-entrance/potential-capacitance, iit-jam-physics/circuits
- `@em-drift`: animated random motion plus slow drift of electrons in a wire, with the real drift speed of copper. Used in: engineering-entrance/current-electricity
- `@em-kirchhoff`: walker round a chosen loop of a two-battery circuit with the potential graph and the loop sum. Used in: engineering-entrance/current-electricity
- `@em-bridge`: Wheatstone bridge and meter bridge with a galvanometer needle and node potentials. Used in: engineering-entrance/current-electricity
- `@em-lc-swing`: LC (and damped LCR) oscillation with energy passing between capacitor and inductor. Used in: engineering-entrance/alternating-current, iit-jam-physics/circuits
- `@em-phasor-rlc`: turning phasors and waveforms for R, L, C and series LCR with phase and impedance. Used in: engineering-entrance/alternating-current
- `@em-transformer`: transformer with turns, load and efficiency sliders, animated core flux and primary and secondary waveforms. Used in: engineering-entrance/alternating-current, iit-jam-physics/circuits

## Magnetism and induction
- `@em-helix-b`: charged particle in a uniform B in 3D (drag to turn), circle to helix with the angle, proton or electron. Used in: engineering-entrance/moving-charges-magnetism, iit-jam-physics/magnetostatics
- `@em-crossed-eb`: cycloid, E×B drift and velocity selector in crossed fields for either sign of charge. Used in: engineering-entrance/moving-charges-magnetism, iit-jam-physics/magnetostatics
- `@em-cyclotron`: animated cyclotron spiral with frequency, final energy and number of crossings. Used in: engineering-entrance/moving-charges-magnetism
- `@em-biot-savart`: compass-needle field maps of a wire, a loop and a solenoid computed by Biot-Savart summation; tap to read B. Used in: engineering-entrance/moving-charges-magnetism, iit-jam-physics/magnetostatics
- `@em-loop-torque`: 3D current loop in B, slip rings against split-ring commutator, with the torque curve. Used in: engineering-entrance/moving-charges-magnetism
- `@em-earth-dip`: Earth as a dipole with a dip needle, latitude slider, B_H, B_V and tan δ = 2 tan λ. Used in: engineering-entrance/magnetism-matter
- `@em-mag-types`: atomic moments in dia-, para- and ferromagnets with B and T sliders and the magnetisation. Used in: engineering-entrance/magnetism-matter, iit-jam-physics/magnetic-materials
- `@em-lenz-steps`: step player for Lenz's law with a magnet through a coil (flux, induced current, poles, force). Used in: engineering-entrance/electromagnetic-induction, iit-jam-physics/induction
- `@em-lenz-fall`: animated magnet drop through a closed or open coil with the EMF graph and fall time. Used in: engineering-entrance/electromagnetic-induction, iit-jam-physics/induction
- `@em-generator`: 3D rotating coil generator with flux and EMF graphs (drag to turn). Used in: engineering-entrance/electromagnetic-induction, engineering-entrance/alternating-current, iit-jam-physics/induction

## Waves, radiation and relativity
- `@em-displacement`: charging capacitor with an Ampère loop and flat or bag surface, B against loop radius. Used in: engineering-entrance/em-waves, iit-jam-physics/maxwell-em-waves
- `@em-wave-3d`: electromagnetic wave in 3D with linear or circular polarisation (drag to turn). Used in: engineering-entrance/em-waves, iit-jam-physics/maxwell-em-waves
- `@em-poynting-flow`: animated Poynting vector flowing into a current-carrying wire and a charging capacitor. Used in: engineering-entrance/em-waves, iit-jam-physics/maxwell-em-waves
- `@em-skin-depth`: decaying wave in a conductor with a metal choice and frequency from 1 Hz to 10 GHz. Used in: iit-jam-physics/maxwell-em-waves, gate-physics/em-waves-media
- `@em-radiation-kink`: step player and time slider for the kink in field lines that is the radiation field of a pushed charge. Used in: gate-physics/potentials-gauges, gate-physics/radiation, csir-net-physics/retarded-potentials
- `@em-beaming`: polar plot of radiated power against speed for acceleration along or across the velocity. Used in: gate-physics/radiation, csir-net-physics/retarded-potentials
- `@em-magnetism-relativity`: wire in the lab frame and in the test charge's frame; length contraction turns a magnetic force into an electric one. Used in: gate-physics/covariant-electrodynamics

## Optics
- `@em-mirror-rays`: concave and convex mirror ray diagrams with a movable object and the mirror formula. Used in: engineering-entrance/ray-optics
- `@em-snell-tir`: Snell's law, critical angle and Fresnel shares, plus an optical fibre with its acceptance angle. Used in: engineering-entrance/ray-optics, iit-jam-physics/geometrical-optics, gate-physics/fresnel-equations, gate-physics/waveguides
- `@em-prism-dispersion`: prism with seven traced colours, apex and incidence sliders and a minimum-deviation button. Used in: engineering-entrance/ray-optics, iit-jam-physics/geometrical-optics
- `@em-rainbow-drop`: step player through a raindrop (refract, reflect, refract, deviation curve, rainbow angle per colour). Used in: engineering-entrance/ray-optics, iit-jam-physics/geometrical-optics
- `@em-scope-rays`: paraxial ray paths through a compound microscope and an astronomical telescope. Used in: engineering-entrance/ray-optics, iit-jam-physics/geometrical-optics
- `@em-huygens`: animated Huygens wavelets building a refracted wavefront. Used in: engineering-entrance/wave-optics, iit-jam-physics/diffraction
- `@em-thin-film`: reflected strength against wavelength for a soap film or a coating on glass. Used in: engineering-entrance/wave-optics, iit-jam-physics/interference
- `@em-single-slit`: single-slit and finite-width double-slit intensity patterns with the screen strip. Used in: engineering-entrance/wave-optics, iit-jam-physics/diffraction
- `@em-brewster`: reflection polarisation at the Brewster angle and a Malus-law stack of polaroids. Used in: engineering-entrance/wave-optics, iit-jam-physics/polarisation, gate-physics/fresnel-equations
- `@em-evanescent`: evanescent wave in total internal reflection and frustrated total reflection across a gap. Used in: gate-physics/fresnel-equations
- `@em-guide-zigzag`: bouncing plane-wave picture of a waveguide mode with v_g, v_p and cutoff. Used in: gate-physics/waveguides

- `@em-plasma-slab`: oscillating electron slab and the plasma frequency, with a reflect or transmit test frequency. Used in: gate-physics/em-waves-media, csir-net-physics/plasma-dispersion
- `@em-debye-screen`: Debye screening cloud and the screened against the bare Coulomb potential. Used in: csir-net-physics/plasma-dispersion
- `@em-debye-relax`: dipoles lagging an alternating field with the Debye ε′ and ε″ curves. Used in: csir-net-physics/dielectric-optical
