# Solids and electronics

- `@sx-cell-3d`: six crystal structures (sc, bcc, fcc, hcp, diamond, rock salt) in drag-to-turn 3D, small-atom or touching view; atoms per cell, coordination number and packing fraction.
  Used in: csir-net-physics/condensed-roadmap, gate-physics/bonding, gate-physics/reciprocal-lattice, iit-jam-physics/crystal-structure.
- `@sx-packing-layers`: step player: close-packed layers A, B and then C or A seen from above; ABAB is hcp, ABCABC is fcc.
  Used in: iit-jam-physics/crystal-structure.
- `@sx-xrd-powder`: powder X-ray pattern of sc, bcc or fcc built from Bragg and the structure-factor rules; allowed peaks solid, forbidden ones dashed; a and lambda sliders.
  Used in: gate-physics/structure-factor, iit-jam-physics/x-ray-diffraction.
- `@sx-ewald`: 2D Ewald circle with a square, rectangular or hexagonal reciprocal lattice; rotate the crystal (or play) and lit reflections with their 2 theta.
  Used in: gate-physics/reciprocal-lattice, gate-physics/structure-factor, iit-jam-physics/x-ray-diffraction.
- `@sx-fermi-sea`: animated Fermi disc in k-space shifting under an electric field; only the crescent at the Fermi surface changes.
  Used in: gate-physics/free-electron-model.
- `@sx-nfe-gap`: nearly-free-electron bands with a gap at the zone boundary and the standing-wave probability densities of the lower and upper state.
  Used in: gate-physics/band-theory.
- `@sx-band-fill`: metal, semiconductor, insulator: gap and temperature sliders, thermal electrons and holes (log scale), photon absorption.
  Used in: csir-net-physics/condensed-roadmap, engineering-entrance/semiconductors, gate-physics/band-theory, gate-physics/semiconductors-hall, iit-jam-physics/semiconductors.
- `@sx-pn-junction`: step player: p-n junction forming (diffusion, depletion, field, built-in barrier, bias) with live depletion width and barrier.
  Used in: engineering-entrance/semiconductors, gate-physics/semiconductor-devices, iit-jam-physics/diodes.
- `@sx-zener-regulator`: animated Zener regulator: input ripple, load and the operating point on the Zener I-V; regulation fails when the Zener current runs out.
  Used in: engineering-entrance/semiconductors, iit-jam-physics/diodes.
- `@sx-led-gap`: animated recombination in an LED: band gap sets photon wavelength and colour (lambda = 1240/Eg), with material presets.
  Used in: engineering-entrance/semiconductors, gate-physics/semiconductor-devices, iit-jam-physics/diodes.
- `@sx-bjt-carriers`: animated electrons crossing the emitter, thin base and collector of an NPN; I_C = I_S exp(V_BE/V_T), I_B = I_C/beta.
  Used in: gate-physics/semiconductor-devices, iit-jam-physics/transistors.
- `@sx-amp-clip`: common-emitter amplifier: load line, Q-point and swinging point beside input and output waveforms; saturation and cut-off clipping.
  Used in: gate-physics/amplifiers-feedback, iit-jam-physics/transistors.
- `@sx-opamp-waves`: inverting, non-inverting, integrator and differentiator with sine, square or triangle input and rail clipping, live waveforms.
  Used in: csir-net-physics/electronics-roadmap, gate-physics/op-amp-circuits, iit-jam-physics/op-amps.
- `@sx-feedback-distortion`: negative feedback on a soft-saturating amplifier: closed-loop gain and distortion against open loop at equal output size.
  Used in: gate-physics/amplifiers-feedback, iit-jam-physics/op-amps.
- `@sx-lc-tank`: oscillator start-up: LC tank with loop gain G and Q, trace growing from a kick to saturation, energy shared by C and L along the trace.
  Used in: gate-physics/oscillators.
- `@sx-adders`: half adder, full adder and 4-bit ripple-carry adder at gate level, tap inputs, wires light up, carry ripples stage by stage.
  Used in: engineering-entrance/semiconductors, gate-physics/combinational-logic, iit-jam-physics/digital-logic.
- `@sx-flipflop`: D, T, JK and clocked SR flip-flops with a clock button or auto clock and a timing diagram; also a step player.
  Used in: csir-net-physics/electronics-roadmap, gate-physics/sequential-logic, iit-jam-physics/digital-logic.
- `@sx-dac-ladder`: 4-bit R-2R ladder DAC: tap bits, contributions V_ref/2..V_ref/16, staircase of all 16 outputs, count-up animation.
  Used in: gate-physics/adc-dac.
- `@sx-sar-adc`: step player: successive-approximation ADC as a binary search (3 to 6 bits), trial levels against V_in.
  Used in: gate-physics/adc-dac.
- `@sx-hall-bar`: animated Hall effect: electrons or holes deflected to one edge, edge charge builds up, V_H = IB/(nqt) with B, I and n sliders.
  Used in: gate-physics/semiconductors-hall, iit-jam-physics/semiconductors.
- `@sx-ising`: animated 2D Ising model (ferromagnet or antiferromagnet) with temperature and field sliders, critical temperature 2.27 J/k and M against time.
  Used in: gate-physics/magnetism.
- `@sx-sc-resistance`: electrons scattering in a vibrating lattice versus Cooper pairs gliding below T_c and B_c(T), with R against T for Hg, Nb, Pb and YBCO.
  Used in: gate-physics/superconductivity.
- `@sx-bond-well`: animated Lennard-Jones well: the lopsided swing makes the mean separation grow with energy (thermal expansion); harmonic parabola dashed.
  Used in: gate-physics/bonding, gate-physics/phonons.
- `@sx-defects-2d`: point defects in 2D: vacancy (with diffusion hops), interstitial, big and small substitutional, Schottky and Frenkel pairs, with lattice relaxation.
  Used in: csir-net-physics/defects-dislocations.
- `@sx-signal-average`: animated averaging of noisy sweeps: average settles on the peak, measured SNR against sqrt(N).
  Used in: csir-net-physics/noise-lock-in, engineering-entrance/experimental-physics.
- `@sx-wheatstone-strain`: strain gauge on a bending cantilever feeding a Wheatstone bridge: quarter, half and full bridge output against load.
  Used in: csir-net-physics/transducers-detectors, engineering-entrance/experimental-physics.
