# Business, BI and storytelling

- `@biz-breakeven`: Break-even chart: price, cost per unit and fixed cost sliders; revenue and cost lines cross at break-even; profit or loss and margin of safety.
  Used in: startup/break-even-runway, startup/online-vs-offline, startup/pricing-unit-economics, startup/true-cost-of-hire.
- `@biz-ltv-cac`: Cumulative gross profit per customer against CAC, with LTV = margin ÷ churn, payback month and LTV:CAC ratio.
  Used in: startup/pricing-unit-economics.
- `@biz-runway-burn`: Animated cash burn-down with cash, costs, sales and growth sliders; shows the month cash hits zero.
  Used in: startup/break-even-runway, startup/loans-grants, startup/paying-yourself, startup/raising-investment.
- `@biz-cohort-retention`: Retention curve (first-month drop and long-run floor) plus a six-cohort heat grid; leaky bucket vs flattening.
  Used in: startup/mvp.
- `@biz-funnel`: Four-stage sales funnel with conversion sliders and a "add 5 points to this step" comparison.
  Used in: startup/launch-plan, startup/mvp.
- `@biz-mrr-churn`: Animated monthly recurring revenue with churn and growing new sales; ceiling = new ÷ churn.
  Used in: startup/raising-investment, startup/what-is-a-startup.
- `@biz-dilution-steps`: Step player: founders, option pool, seed and Series A cap table bars with price per share and the founder stake value.
  Used in: startup/cap-table-dilution, startup/raising-investment.
- `@biz-elasticity`: Demand with price sensitivity: buyers, revenue or profit against price; best profit price = cost·e/(e-1).
  Used in: startup/pricing-unit-economics.
- `@biz-tam-sam-som`: Nested TAM, SAM and SOM circles, top-down or bottom-up (sales calls and close rate).
  Used in: startup/one-page-plan, startup/problem-worth-solving.
- `@biz-vesting`: Vesting staircase with vesting period, cliff and months worked (or Play); shares vested if someone leaves.
  Used in: startup/co-founder-agreement, startup/disputes-exits, startup/employee-stock-options, startup/splitting-equity, startup/vesting-cliffs.
- `@biz-gst-chain`: Step player: GST/VAT along grower, maker, shop; tax sent at each hop adds up to rate × final price.
  Used in: startup/canada-gst-hst, startup/how-gst-works, startup/india-gst.
- `@biz-loan-schedule`: Equal-instalment loan: interest vs principal in each payment, or balance; animated month marker.
  Used in: startup/loans-grants.
- `@biz-option-payoff`: Stock option payoff against share price with strike, number granted and vested share.
  Used in: startup/employee-stock-options.
- `@biz-filter-steps`: Step player: Store and Category slicers filter a Sales table; the measure adds the surviving rows.
  Used in: power-bi/filter-context, power-bi/relationships, power-bi/slicers-interactions, power-bi/star-schema.
- `@biz-row-vs-filter`: Calculated column (row by row, animated) against a measure (filtered rows) on the same Profit formula.
  Used in: power-bi/calculated-columns, power-bi/context-transition, power-bi/filter-context, power-bi/first-measures, power-bi/iterators.
- `@biz-calculate-modes`: Same measure with plain, CALCULATE override, KEEPFILTERS and ALL under two slicers; rows counted and total.
  Used in: power-bi/all-and-percent-of-total, power-bi/calculate, power-bi/filter-function.
- `@biz-time-intel`: 24-month calendar: YTD, QTD, same period last year, YTD last year, rolling 3 months, previous month selected on a month slider.
  Used in: power-bi/date-table, power-bi/last-year-growth, power-bi/pace-and-targets, power-bi/rolling-windows, power-bi/ytd-mtd.
- `@biz-ratio-of-sums`: Average of store margins against total profit ÷ total sales, with sliders that pull them apart.
  Used in: power-bi/divide-and-ratios, power-bi/first-measures, power-bi/modelling-mistakes.
- `@biz-rls-roles`: Row-level security: view the Sales table as head office, one store, one category or both roles.
  Used in: power-bi/row-level-security.
- `@biz-unpivot-anim`: Animated wide-to-long unpivot: cells move from a grid into a Store, Month, Sales list.
  Used in: power-bi/unpivot-group.
- `@biz-rank-topn`: Eight ranked products with ties: RANKX skip vs dense, and TOPN N slider returning tied rows.
  Used in: power-bi/ranking-topn.
- `@biz-five-charts`: Same data as bars, lines, pie, stacked bars and table, judged against three questions.
  Used in: data-storytelling/bars-and-lines, data-storytelling/choosing-a-chart.
- `@biz-highlight-colour`: Six lines coloured every-colour vs grey plus one highlight; choose which line is the story.
  Used in: data-storytelling/colour-with-purpose, data-storytelling/declutter.
- `@biz-pie-vs-bar`: Pie and bars side by side with a B-versus-C gap slider and hidden or shown numbers.
  Used in: data-storytelling/pies-and-parts.
- `@biz-small-multiples`: Eight lines in one chart or small panels, with a shared or separate scale.
  Used in: data-storytelling/explore-vs-explain, data-storytelling/tables-and-cards.
- `@biz-annotation-steps`: Step player adding a message title, direct labels, event marker and shaded gap to a line chart.
  Used in: data-storytelling/action-titles, data-storytelling/dashboard-to-slides, data-storytelling/labels-and-notes.
- `@biz-dashboard-arc`: Step player lighting up a dashboard sketch: headline, trend, breakdown, action.
  Used in: data-storytelling/dashboard-layout, data-storytelling/presenting, data-storytelling/story-structure.
- `@biz-sampling-error`: Thirty simulated surveys with 95% error bars; sample size slider and re-run button.
  Used in: data-storytelling/uncertainty.
- `@biz-baseline-toggle`: One sales number judged against nothing, target, last year, last month or industry average.
  Used in: data-storytelling/compared-to-what, data-storytelling/kpi-cards, data-storytelling/what-makes-a-kpi.
- `@biz-dual-axis`: Two unrelated series on one axis, two axes (slide the right axis range) or indexed to 100.
  Used in: data-storytelling/bars-and-lines, data-storytelling/honest-charts.
