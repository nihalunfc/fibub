# Test prep

- `@tp-ledger-balance`: Double entry one transaction at a time: debit and credit move two accounts and the assets stack stays equal to liabilities plus capital.
  Used in: cuet/accountancy-shares, cuet/accountancy-statements, cuet/accountancy-debentures.
- `@tp-depreciation`: Straight-line against written-down value depreciation: sliders for cost, scrap, life and rate, both book-value curves and yearly charges.
  Used in: cuet/accountancy-statements.
- `@tp-cashflow-sort`: Tap-to-sort game: ten items into operating, investing and financing activities, checked with a reason and net totals per box.
  Used in: cuet/accountancy-cash-flow.
- `@tp-profit-share`: Partnership profit splits: interest on capital, salary and ratio; new-partner sacrificing ratio; retirement gaining ratio (data-mode share, admit or retire).
  Used in: cuet/accountancy-dissolution, cuet/accountancy-reconstitution, cuet/accountancy-partnership, cuet/accountancy-retirement.
- `@tp-ratio-gauges`: Current, quick and debt-equity ratios as gauges with comfortable zones, driven by sliders on a small balance sheet.
  Used in: cuet/accountancy-ratios.
- `@tp-goodwill-methods`: Goodwill by average profit, super profit and capitalisation from the same sliders, with the super-profit bar.
  Used in: cuet/accountancy-goodwill.
- `@tp-demand-supply`: Supply and demand with shifting curves and price ceiling or floor; goods, rupee-dollar and money-market versions (data-market).
  Used in: cuet/economics-money-banking, cuet/economics-open-economy.
- `@tp-circular-flow`: Animated circular flow of income that adds saving and investment, government and the rest of the world; leakages against injections.
  Used in: cuet/economics-national-income.
- `@tp-income-expenditure`: Keynesian cross: AD = C0 + cY + I + G against the 45 degree line, equilibrium, multiplier and gap to full employment.
  Used in: cuet/economics-employment-environment, cuet/economics-income-determination.
- `@tp-bank-chain`: Animated chain of banks creating deposits from a first deposit, with a reserve-ratio slider and the 1 over LRR limit.
  Used in: cuet/economics-money-banking.
- `@tp-budget-deficit`: Budget receipts and spending as stacked bars with revenue, fiscal and primary deficits worked out live.
  Used in: cuet/economics-budget.
- `@tp-bop`: Balance of payments bars: trade, services, transfers, FDI and portfolio flows rolled up to current, capital and overall balance.
  Used in: cuet/economics-open-economy.
- `@tp-timeline`: Tappable timeline with Play: Indian economy since 1951, freedom struggle, making of the Constitution (data-set econ, freedom, constitution).
  Used in: cuet/economics-indian-economy-1950-90, cuet/ga-polity, cuet/ga-history-economy, cuet/economics-reforms-1991.
- `@tp-percent-chain`: Three successive percent changes as bars with multipliers; shows why the net change is not the sum, and order does not matter.
  Used in: gmat/percents, gmat/quant-review, gmat/interest-profit, sat/ratios-rates-percent, gre/gre-arithmetic, cuet/percentages, cuet/interest, cuet/profit-loss.
- `@tp-mixture-vessel`: Animated pouring of two solutions into one: weighted average, alligation ratio and a Hit a target button.
  Used in: gmat/ratios-mixtures, sat/ratios-rates-percent, gre/gre-quant-review, cuet/averages-mixtures, cuet/chemistry-solutions.
- `@tp-prob-tree`: Two-stage probability tree with width-scaled branches; light up A and B, total B, or A given B (Bayes).
  Used in: gmat/counting, gmat/probability, sat/probability, gre/gre-counting-probability, cuet/maths-probability.
- `@tp-logic-board`: Seating and ordering board for five people with rules that tick live and a live count of the orders that remain possible.
  Used in: lsat/conditional-logic, cuet/seating-arrangement.
- `@tp-passage-map`: Tap-to-label reading passage map (background, claim, evidence, objection, response) drawn as a coloured bar.
  Used in: gmat/rc-inference, gmat/main-idea-detail, gmat/reading-method, gmat/structure-tone, lsat/rc-main-point, lsat/rc-author-attitude, lsat/rc-structure-function, lsat/rc-comparative, lsat/rc-detail-inference, lsat/rc-passage-mapping, sat/central-ideas, sat/cross-text, sat/text-structure-purpose, gre/gre-reading, gre/gre-reading-questions, cuet/reading-comprehension.
- `@tp-pacing-clock`: Section pacing clock for SAT, GMAT, GRE, LSAT and CUET sections: even-pace ring, checkpoints, and your own pace (data-test).
  Used in: gmat/study-plan, gmat/practice-test, gmat/what-is-the-gmat, gmat/pacing, lsat/lsat-practice-test-1, lsat/how-the-lsat-works, lsat/lsat-study-plan, lsat/lsat-timing, lsat/lsat-practice-test-2, sat/sat-practice-test-1, sat/how-the-digital-sat-works, sat/sat-practice-test-2, sat/sat-study-plan, gre/gre-study-plan, gre/gre-pacing, gre/gre-practice-test, cuet/mock-general-test, cuet/study-plan, cuet/marking-strategy.
- `@tp-argument-build`: Tap sentences into premise, conclusion or background, then see the argument and its hidden assumption gap (data-set 0 to 2).
  Used in: gmat/evaluate-flaw, gmat/assumptions, gmat/boldface-paradox, gmat/critical-reasoning, gmat/strengthen-weaken, lsat/argument-anatomy, lsat/evaluate, lsat/weaken, lsat/paradox, lsat/point-at-issue, lsat/flaw, lsat/method-of-reasoning, lsat/main-conclusion, lsat/necessary-assumption, lsat/strengthen, lsat/parallel-reasoning, lsat/role-of-statement, sat/quantitative-evidence, sat/inferences, sat/textual-evidence, gre/gre-arguments.
- `@tp-contrapositive`: Conditional logic machine: pick an if-then and a fact; the picture and answer show what follows and why converse and inverse fail.
  Used in: gmat/inference, lsat/sufficient-assumption, lsat/conditional-logic, lsat/most-strongly-supported, lsat/quantifiers, lsat/inference-must-be-true, lsat/common-flaws, lsat/principle, cuet/syllogisms.
- `@tp-ineq-line`: Number-line solver for x > a, a < x < b, |x - c| < d and |x - c| > d with open or closed ends and a test point.
  Used in: gmat/inequalities-absolute-value, sat/linear-inequalities, gre/qc-strategies, gre/gre-algebra.
- `@tp-coord-points`: Drag two points on a grid: distance, midpoint, slope, line equation and perpendicular bisector.
  Used in: gmat/linear-equations, sat/linear-functions, sat/linear-equations, gre/coordinate-geometry, gre/lines-angles.
