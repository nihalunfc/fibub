**Module 1 · about 14 minutes.** By the end you will be able to turn your CELPIP levels into CLB levels, check whether they meet your programme's minimum, and see how many Express Entry points each level is worth.

<div class="bigidea">CELPIP gives you a level from 1 to 12 for each skill. For immigration, those levels match the Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB) one to one, and each ability must reach your programme's minimum on its own.</div>

## 1. How CELPIP reports your score

You get one level for each of the four skills, from **1** (lowest) to **12** (highest), plus two special marks: **M** (minimal, not enough English to place on the scale) and **NA** (not enough of an answer to rate). There is no single overall score that immigration uses; each skill counts separately.

What the levels feel like, in our own words:

| CELPIP level | Roughly means |
|---|---|
| 10-12 | Uses English with ease and precision in almost any everyday or work situation |
| 9 | Handles complex situations well; occasional small errors |
| 7-8 | Handles most everyday and work situations; some errors, but the message is clear |
| 5-6 | Manages familiar situations; needs effort with less familiar topics |
| 4 | Manages simple, predictable situations |
| 1-3, M | Not yet enough for most immigration purposes |

## 2. CELPIP levels and CLB

The **Canadian Language Benchmarks (CLB)** are the national scale the Government of Canada uses. CELPIP was designed so that its levels match CLB levels directly:

<div class="steps">
<div class="step">

**CELPIP 4 to 9 = CLB 4 to 9.** A CELPIP level 7 in writing is CLB 7 in writing. No conversion table needed.

</div>
<div class="step">

**CELPIP 10, 11 and 12 = CLB 10.** Immigration counts anything from 10 up as "CLB 10 or more". A 12 gives the same points as a 10.

</div>
<div class="step">

**Below CELPIP 4 = below CLB 4.** That is under the minimum of almost every programme, including citizenship.

</div>
<div class="step">

**Each ability stands alone.** A programme that needs CLB 7 needs 7 in listening, 7 in reading, 7 in writing and 7 in speaking. An average does not count.

</div>
</div>

## 3. What different goals need

| Goal | Minimum | Abilities |
|---|---|---|
| Canadian citizenship (ages 18-54) | CLB 4 | Listening and speaking |
| Express Entry, Canadian Experience Class, TEER 2 or 3 job | CLB 5 | All four |
| Express Entry, Canadian Experience Class, TEER 0 or 1 job | CLB 7 | All four |
| Express Entry, Federal Skilled Worker | CLB 7 | All four |
| Express Entry, Federal Skilled Trades | CLB 5 speaking and listening, CLB 4 reading and writing | All four |

Provincial programmes set their own minimums, often CLB 4 to 7. Always check the current rule on canada.ca or your province's site before you book.

## 4. Express Entry points

Being eligible is only the first step. In Express Entry, candidates are ranked by the **Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS)**, and your first official language gives up to **136 points** (single) or **128** (with a spouse or partner coming). Points are given **per ability**:

| CLB per ability | Single | With spouse |
|---|---|---|
| below 4 | 0 | 0 |
| 4 or 5 | 6 | 6 |
| 6 | 9 | 8 |
| 7 | 17 | 16 |
| 8 | 23 | 22 |
| 9 | 31 | 29 |
| 10 or more | 34 | 32 |

<div class="meaning">
<dl>
<dt>CLB 7 → 9</dt><dd>14 more points per ability (17 → 31), or 56 across all four.</dd>
<dt>CLB 9 → 10</dt><dd>Only 3 more points per ability (31 → 34).</dd>
<dt>Physical meaning</dt><dd>The biggest jumps are from 6 to 7 and from 8 to 9. If you are at 8, pushing to 9 is worth far more than pushing a 10 to a 12. Language at CLB 9 also unlocks extra "skill transferability" points when combined with education or foreign work experience.</dd>
</dl>
</div>

<div class="myth">

**"My strong skills will make up for my weak one."** Minimums are per ability, and CRS points are also counted per ability. One weak skill can block eligibility or cost you dozens of points. That is why the study planner in lesson 1.5 gives your weakest skill a double share of time.

<div class="keypoints">

- CELPIP gives a level from 1 to 12 for each skill; immigration uses each skill separately.
- CELPIP 4-9 equals CLB 4-9; CELPIP 10, 11 and 12 all count as CLB 10.
- Citizenship needs CLB 4 in listening and speaking; most Express Entry streams need CLB 5 or 7 in all four.
- CRS gives up to 34 points per ability (single); the jumps from CLB 6 to 7 and 8 to 9 are the biggest.
- Rules change: check canada.ca before booking or applying.
