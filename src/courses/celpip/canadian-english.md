**Module 1 · about 14 minutes.** By the end you will recognise the Canadian words and situations that appear in CELPIP and use Canadian spelling naturally in your writing.

<div class="bigidea">CELPIP is set in everyday Canada. Knowing the words Canadians use for homes, work, money, health and school makes the listening and reading easier and your writing sound natural.</div>

## 1. Canadian spelling in one minute

Canadian spelling is a mix: mostly British for some word families, mostly American for others. CELPIP raters accept any spelling used **consistently**, but these are the common Canadian choices:

| Pattern | Canadian | Also seen |
|---|---|---|
| -our | colour, neighbour, favourite, behaviour | color (US) |
| -re | centre, theatre, metre | center (US) |
| -ize | organize, realize, apologize | organise (UK) |
| -ll- | travelled, cancelled, counsellor | traveled (US) |
| words | cheque, licence (noun), defence | check, license (US) |
| words | tire, curb, program, jewellery | tyre, kerb, programme (UK) |

<div class="myth">

**"I will lose marks for American spelling."** You will not, as long as you are consistent. Mixing "color" and "colour" in one email looks careless; choose one style and stick to it.

</div>

## 2. Words by situation

<div class="steps">
<div class="step">

**Home.** *Apartment* or *condo* (owned apartment), *basement suite*, *landlord*, *lease*, *first and last month's rent*, *hydro* (electricity), *superintendent* or *super*, *strata* or *condo fees*, *snow removal*.

</div>
<div class="step">

**Work.** *Coworker*, *shift*, *overtime*, *pay stub*, *T4* (yearly tax slip from an employer), *EI* (Employment Insurance), *two weeks' notice*, *stat holiday* (statutory holiday).

</div>
<div class="step">

**Money.** *Loonie* ($1 coin), *toonie* ($2 coin), *debit* (paying straight from your bank account), *e-transfer*, *GST/HST* (sales taxes), *RRSP* and *TFSA* (savings accounts with tax benefits).

</div>
<div class="step">

**Health.** *Health card*, *family doctor*, *walk-in clinic*, *pharmacist*, *prescription*, *ER* (emergency room), *dental plan*, *benefits*.

</div>
<div class="step">

**School and community.** *Grade 10* (not "Year 10"), *elementary*, *high school*, *daycare*, *community centre*, *recreation program*, *city council*, *by-law* (local rule).

</div>
<div class="step">

**Everyday.** *Washroom* (toilet in a public place), *line-up* (queue), *toque* (winter hat), *double-double*, *cottage* (summer house by a lake), *postal code*, *parkade* (parking garage, in the west).

</div>
</div>

## 3. Situations CELPIP loves

The test returns again and again to a few kinds of everyday problems. Knowing the typical vocabulary helps you predict what you will hear and read.

| Situation | Words to know |
|---|---|
| A problem with a landlord or neighbour | repairs, notice, lease, noise complaint, deposit, maintenance request |
| A change at work | schedule, shift swap, training, deadline, policy, supervisor |
| Community planning | proposal, council meeting, residents, funding, by-law, petition |
| Shopping and services | refund, receipt, warranty, exchange, appointment, booking |
| Health and family | appointment, referral, daycare, pick-up, after-school program |
| Weather and travel | storm warning, road closure, delays, detour, transit pass |

<div class="phrases">

- *I'm writing about...* / *I'm calling to ask whether...*
- *Would it be possible to...?* / *I was wondering if...*
- *Sorry for the short notice, but...*
- *I'd appreciate it if you could...*
- *Let me know what works for you.*

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Canadian spelling: -our and -re (colour, centre), but -ize (organize); cheque, licence, tire.
- Any spelling style is accepted if you use it consistently.
- Learn words by situation: home, work, money, health, school and community.
- Hydro = electricity, washroom = public toilet, line-up = queue, Grade 10 = school year 10.
- CELPIP situations repeat: landlords, work changes, community plans, services, family and weather.
