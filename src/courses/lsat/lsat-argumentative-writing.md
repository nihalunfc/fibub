**Module 7 · about 25 minutes, plus 50 minutes per practice essay · read first: [2.1 Argument anatomy](/learn/courses/lsat/argument-anatomy/).** By the end you will have a plan you can apply to any prompt, and two practice essays done.

<div class="bigidea">Argumentative Writing asks you to do what the rest of the LSAT tests you on: build an argument. Take a side, give reasons, answer the best objection, and conclude. Clear beats clever.</div>

## 1. The format (checked 22 November 2026)

- Taken online, at home, separately from the multiple-choice test; it is not scored, but law schools receive it, and LSAC releases your score only once one is on file.
- You see a key question and several perspectives on it.
- Prewriting: 15 minutes to think and plan with guided prompts (you may move on after 5).
- Writing: 35 minutes.

## 2. A four-paragraph plan

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><b>Position:</b> one sentence that answers the key question, with any condition you need ("yes, but only if...").</div>
<div class="step"><b>Reason 1:</b> your strongest reason, supported by a perspective or an example.</div>
<div class="step"><b>The best objection, answered:</b> state the strongest opposing point fairly, then show why it does not defeat your position (or how your position handles it).</div>
<div class="step"><b>Conclusion:</b> tie the reasons back to the key question in two or three sentences.</div>
</div>

<div class="meaning">

<dl>
<dt>Perspective</dt><dd>a view given in the prompt; use them as material, not as a list to summarise</dd>
<dt>Concession</dt><dd>admitting what is right in an opposing view; it makes your argument more credible</dd>
<dt>Physical meaning</dt><dd>This is the shape of a legal brief: the rule you want, why, the other side's best argument, and why it fails.</dd>
</dl>

</div>

<div class="myth">

**"It's unscored, so it doesn't matter."** Admissions readers can and do read it, especially when they compare it with your personal statement. A clear, careful essay is worth an hour of practice.

</div>
