**Module 4 · about 16 minutes · read first: [2.2](/learn/courses/lsat/main-conclusion/).** By the end you will pin down exactly what two speakers disagree (or agree) about.

<div class="bigidea">Two people can talk past each other. The point at issue is a statement where both speakers have clearly committed to a view, and their views are opposite.</div>

## 1. The opinion test

For each answer, fill in a small table:

| Answer | Speaker 1 says | Speaker 2 says |
|---|---|---|
| must be: | true (or false) | the opposite |

If either speaker has no clear view, cross the answer out.

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><b>Ravi:</b> "The city should build a new library downtown. The old one is too small for the number of people who use it."</div>
<div class="step"><b>Jo:</b> "The old library isn't too small. Most of its users come only for the computers, and adding computers to the old building would fix the crowding."</div>
<div class="step">Ravi says the old library is too small (true). Jo says it is not (false). <b>Opposite views: that is the point at issue.</b></div>
<div class="step">"Libraries should have computers": Ravi says nothing about computers. No view, so cross it out even though Jo mentions them.</div>
</div>

<div class="meaning">

<dl>
<dt>Point at issue</dt><dd>a statement one speaker accepts and the other rejects</dd>
<dt>Point of agreement</dt><dd>a statement both accept (some questions ask for this instead)</dd>
<dt>Physical meaning</dt><dd>Disagreement needs two commitments. Silence is not disagreement, and agreeing on the facts while drawing different conclusions is still a disagreement about the conclusion.</dd>
</dl>

</div>

<details class="try">
<summary>Try it: a full question</summary>

"Amira: Schools should start later in the morning. Teenagers' body clocks make them sleepy early, so they learn less in early classes.

Ben: Teenagers' body clocks do shift later, but a later start would just push sports and part-time jobs later, so students would get no extra sleep."

Amira and Ben disagree about whether

(A) teenagers' body clocks shift later.
(B) a later school start would lead to students getting more sleep.
(C) sports should be cancelled.
(D) early classes are harder for teachers.
(E) part-time jobs are good for students.

**Answer: (B).** Amira's argument depends on later starts helping students (she implies they would be less sleepy); Ben says they would get no extra sleep. (A) is a point of <i>agreement</i>: Ben accepts it. (C), (D) and (E): at least one speaker has no view.

</details>

<div class="myth">

**"If one speaker brings up a topic, the other must disagree with it."** Speaker 2 often adds new points the first speaker never addressed. Without a view from both, it is not a point at issue.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- The right answer is a statement one speaker accepts and the other rejects.
- Use the opinion test on every answer; cross out any where a speaker has no stated view.
- Speakers often agree on facts and disagree on the conclusion drawn.
- Some questions ask for agreement instead: then both must accept the statement.
