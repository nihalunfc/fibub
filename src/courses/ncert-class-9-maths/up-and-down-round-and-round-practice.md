**Practice · Class 9 Maths · Chapter 5.** Read the lesson, [I’m Up and Down, and Round and Round](/learn/courses/ncert-class-9-maths/up-and-down-round-and-round/), first. Try each question on paper before you open the answer.

<div class="bigidea">Three habits win marks here: measure every hand or arm from the same start line, remember the hour hand creeps 0.5° each minute, and always give the smaller angle between clock hands unless the question says otherwise.</div>

## How to set out answers

1. Say what you are measuring from (the 12, or the start line).
2. Write each position in degrees with its working, for example 4 × 30 + 20 × 0.5.
3. Subtract, and if the answer is over 180°, give 360° minus it.
4. For periodic motion, write period = time ÷ cycles and include the unit.

## 1-mark questions

1. How many degrees are there in three quarters of a turn?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>270°.</details>

2. How many degrees does the minute hand turn in 5 minutes?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>5 × 6 = 30°.</details>

3. Name the time taken for one complete cycle of a repeating motion.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The period.</details>

4. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 6:00?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>180°: the hands make a straight line.</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

5. A wheel has 20 equally spaced spokes. Find the angle between two neighbouring spokes. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>360° ÷ 20 = 18°.</details>

6. Write an angle between 0° and 360° that ends in the same position as −100°. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>−100° + 360° = 260°.</details>

7. Find the angle between the hands of a clock at 8:00. Give the smaller angle. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The hour hand is at 8 × 30 = 240° and the minute hand at 0°. The difference is 240°, which is more than 180°, so the smaller angle is 360° − 240° = 120°.</details>

8. A swing makes 15 complete swings in 45 seconds. What is its period, and how many swings does it make in 2 minutes? *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Period = 45 ÷ 15 = 3 s. In 2 minutes (120 s) it makes 120 ÷ 3 = 40 swings.</details>

9. At what time between 3 and 4 o’clock is the angle between the hands exactly 0°? Give your answer as minutes past 3, to one decimal place. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

At m minutes past 3 the hour hand is at 90 + 0.5m degrees and the minute hand is at 6m degrees. Set them equal: 90 + 0.5m = 6m, so 5.5m = 90 and m = 90 ÷ 5.5 ≈ 16.4. The hands overlap at about 16.4 minutes past 3, that is about 3:16 and 22 seconds.

</details>

Fresh numbers every time. Try until you get three right in a row.

## 5-mark questions

10. Find the angle between the hands of a clock at (a) 2:40 and (b) 10:10. Say which is bigger and by how much. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) At 2:40: minute hand = 40 × 6 = 240°. Hour hand = 2 × 30 + 40 × 0.5 = 80°. Difference = 160°, which is less than 180°, so the angle is 160°.

(b) At 10:10: minute hand = 60°. Hour hand = 300 + 5 = 305°. Difference = 245°, so the smaller angle is 360° − 245° = 115°.

The angle in (a) is bigger by 160° − 115° = 45°.

</details>

11. A Ferris wheel of radius 18 m has its lowest point at ground level. It takes 12 seconds for a turn. A rider boards at the bottom. (a) How high is the rider after 3 s, 6 s and 9 s? (b) State the highest and lowest height and the period. (c) Describe the graph of height against time. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

3 s is a quarter turn (90°), 6 s is a half turn (180°) and 9 s is three quarters (270°). The centre is 18 m high.

(a) After 3 s the rider is level with the centre: 18 m. After 6 s the rider is at the top: 36 m. After 9 s the rider is level with the centre again: 18 m.

(b) The highest height is 36 m, the lowest is 0 m, and the period is 12 s.

(c) The graph starts at 0, rises to 18 m at 3 s, 36 m at 6 s, falls to 18 m at 9 s and 0 m at 12 s, and then repeats every 12 s. It is a smooth wave with its middle line at 18 m.

</details>

## Case-based question

Arjun’s grandfather has a wall clock with a pendulum in Kochi. The pendulum takes 1 second to go from the far left to the far right. A tourist boat at the harbour has a big wheel on its deck that turns once every 20 seconds.

12. (a) What is the period of the pendulum? (b) How many full swings in one minute? (c) Through how many degrees does the deck wheel turn each second? (d) Through how many degrees does it turn in 5 seconds?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) One second goes from left to right, which is only half a swing. A full swing is out and back, so the period is 2 s. (b) 60 ÷ 2 = 30 full swings. (c) 360° ÷ 20 = 18° per second. (d) 5 × 18 = 90°: a quarter turn.

</details>

## Assertion and reason

13. **Assertion (A):** The angle between the hands of a clock at 3:30 is 75°. **Reason (R):** At 3:30 the hour hand is exactly on the 3.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

A is true: minute hand 180°, hour hand 90 + 15 = 105°, difference 75°. R is false, because by 3:30 the hour hand has moved halfway to the 4. So A is true and R is false.

</details>

14. **Assertion (A):** The angles 30° and −330° leave an arm in the same position. **Reason (R):** The angles differ by 360°.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Both are true and R explains A: 30 − (−330) = 360, a whole turn, so the arm ends up at the same place.</details>

15. **Assertion (A):** The height graph of a rider on a Ferris wheel repeats. **Reason (R):** After each full turn the rider is back at the same height moving the same way.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Both are true and R explains A.</details>
