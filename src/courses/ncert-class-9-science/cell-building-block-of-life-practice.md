## How to set out your answers

Write the definition or the main fact first, then the reason or an example. For a calculation, write the formula, put the numbers in with units, and box the answer. For a diagram question, label every part with a ruled line. For a long answer, use short numbered points.

## 1-mark questions

**1.** Name the scientist who first described cells in a slice of cork.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>Robert Hooke (1665).</details>

**2.** Name the organelle called the power house of the cell.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>Mitochondrion.</details>

**3.** What is the outer layer of a plant cell made of?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>Cellulose (the cell wall).</details>

**4.** What name is given to a plant cell that is firm because it is full of water?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>Turgid.</details>

## 2- and 3-mark questions

**5.** Give two differences between a prokaryotic and a eukaryotic cell.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>A prokaryotic cell has no true nucleus (DNA lies free in the nucleoid) and no membrane-bound organelles; a eukaryotic cell has a nucleus with a membrane and membrane-bound organelles. Prokaryotic cells are also smaller (1 to 5 µm against 10 to 100 µm).</details>

**6.** A cell looks 3 mm long in a drawing made at 500×. Find its true length in µm.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

True length = image ÷ magnification = 3 mm ÷ 500 = 0.006 mm = 6 µm.

</details>

**7.** Why do lysosomes get called “suicide bags”?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>They contain strong digestive enzymes. If the cell is damaged and the lysosomes burst, the enzymes digest the cell’s own parts and the cell dies.</details>

**8.** Explain why salt is added to pickles.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>The strong salt solution outside makes water leave the cells of microbes by osmosis, so they shrivel and cannot grow. This keeps the pickle from spoiling.</details>

**9.** What is the difference between diffusion and osmosis?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>Diffusion is the movement of any particles from high to low concentration. Osmosis is the diffusion of water only, through a selectively permeable membrane, towards the stronger solution.</details>

## 5-mark questions

**10.** Describe how you would prepare and observe a temporary slide of onion peel. What do you see?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

1. Peel a thin transparent layer from the inner side of an onion scale leaf.
2. Place it flat in a drop of water on a clean slide.
3. Add a drop of safranin (or iodine) to stain the cell parts.
4. Lower a cover slip slowly with a needle so no air bubbles are trapped.
5. Look first under low power, then high power.

You see rectangular cells packed like bricks, each with a cell wall, a darker nucleus and a large vacuole. There are no chloroplasts in onion peel.

</details>

**11.** Compare a plant cell and an animal cell in a table, with at least four differences.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

| Feature | Plant cell | Animal cell |
|---|---|---|
| Cell wall | present (cellulose) | absent |
| Plastids | present (chloroplasts in green parts) | absent |
| Vacuole | one large central | none or small |
| Shape | fixed | changeable |

</details>

**12.** A cube-shaped cell has a side of 2 µm. It grows to a side of 4 µm. Find the ratio of surface area to volume before and after, and say what it means.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Before: SA = 6 × 2² = 24 µm², V = 2³ = 8 µm³, ratio = 3.

After: SA = 6 × 4² = 96 µm², V = 4³ = 64 µm³, ratio = 1.5.

The ratio halves: there is less surface to supply each unit of volume, so a bigger cell finds it harder to take in food and oxygen. This limits cell size, and cells divide.

</details>

## Case-based question

Meera put three identical potato strips into three beakers: pure water, a 1% salt solution and a 10% salt solution. After an hour, strip A was longer and stiff, strip B was almost unchanged and strip C was shorter and limp.

**13a.** Which beaker held which strip?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>A: pure water. B: 1% salt (close to the strength of the cell sap). C: 10% salt.</details>

**13b.** Name the process that made strip A longer.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>Osmosis: water entered the cells from the weaker solution outside.</details>

**13c.** Why did strip B hardly change?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>Its solution was nearly isotonic, so water entered and left at the same rate.</details>

**13d.** What would you see if you looked at strip C’s cells under a microscope?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>Plasmolysed cells: the cytoplasm and membrane pulled away from the cell wall.</details>

## Assertion and reason

Choose: (a) both true, R explains A; (b) both true, R does not explain A; (c) A true, R false; (d) A false, R true.

**14.** A: A plant cell does not burst in pure water. R: The cell wall resists the pressure of water entering the cell.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>(a). Both are true and the rigid cellulose wall is the reason the cell becomes turgid, not burst.</details>

**15.** A: Mitochondria are found in plant cells as well as animal cells. R: Plant cells also need to release energy from food by respiration.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>(a). Both true: plants respire too, so they have mitochondria besides their chloroplasts.</details>

**16.** A: Smooth ER helps remove poisons in liver cells. R: Smooth ER has ribosomes on it.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>(c). A is true; R is false: smooth ER has no ribosomes (rough ER has them).</details>

<div class="keypoints">

- Magnification: total = eyepiece × objective; true size = image size ÷ magnification.
- Know each organelle with one job: nucleus (control), mitochondria (energy), ribosomes (proteins), Golgi (packing), lysosomes (digestion), plastids and vacuoles.
- Osmosis moves water toward the stronger solution; hypotonic gives turgid cells, hypertonic gives plasmolysis.
- SA/V = 6/s for a cube, so it falls as size grows.
