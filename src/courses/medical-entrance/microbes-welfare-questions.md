**Biology · Class 12 · about 20 minutes.** Read [Microbes in human welfare](/learn/courses/medical-entrance/microbes-welfare/) first. Here you meet every question type NEET builds from this chapter, then practise.

<div class="bigidea">Nine questions out of ten here are "which microbe makes which product" or "which step happens where". Learn the pairs with their kind (bacterium, fungus, yeast, virus) and the order of sewage treatment, and the rest follows.</div>

## The question types

<div class="steps" data-ms="8000">
<div class="step"><b>1. Microbe to product.</b> <i>Method:</i> learn in sets: acids (<i>Aspergillus</i> citric, <i>Acetobacter</i> acetic, <i>Clostridium</i> butyric, <i>Lactobacillus</i> lactic); medicines (<i>Streptococcus</i> streptokinase, <i>Trichoderma</i> cyclosporin A, <i>Monascus</i> statins, <i>Penicillium</i> penicillin). <i>Trap:</i> <i>Trichoderma</i> appears twice: cyclosporin A and biocontrol.</div>
<div class="step"><b>2. Kind of microbe.</b> <i>Aspergillus</i>, <i>Trichoderma</i>, <i>Penicillium</i>, <i>Glomus</i> are fungi; <i>Monascus</i> and <i>Saccharomyces</i> are yeasts; Nucleopolyhedrovirus is a virus.</div>
<div class="step"><b>3. Household uses.</b> Curd and B₁₂ (LAB), dough (CO₂), Swiss cheese holes (<i>Propionibacterium sharmanii</i>), Roquefort (fungi), toddy (palm sap).</div>
<div class="step"><b>4. Beverages.</b> Distilled: whisky, brandy, rum. Not distilled: wine, beer. <i>Trap:</i> both use <i>Saccharomyces cerevisiae</i>.</div>
<div class="step"><b>5. Sewage steps in order.</b> Filtration, sedimentation (primary sludge) → aeration with flocs (BOD falls) → settling tank (activated sludge) → part back as inoculum, rest to anaerobic digesters (biogas). <i>Trap:</i> primary is physical, secondary biological.</div>
<div class="step"><b>6. BOD reading.</b> High BOD = more organic pollution; treatment lowers BOD.</div>
<div class="step"><b>7. Biogas.</b> Methanogens, anaerobic, cellulose, cattle rumen and dung; gas = methane + CO₂ + H₂. IARI and KVIC.</div>
<div class="step"><b>8. Biocontrol and biofertilisers.</b> Ladybird (aphids), dragonfly (mosquitoes), Bt (caterpillars), baculoviruses (species-specific, IPM). Symbiotic <i>Rhizobium</i>; free-living <i>Azotobacter</i>, <i>Azospirillum</i>; mycorrhiza (<i>Glomus</i>, phosphorus); cyanobacteria in paddy.</div>
</div>

## Worked examples

**Example 1 (level 4, match).** List I: (a) streptokinase, (b) cyclosporin A, (c) statins, (d) citric acid. List II: (p) *Monascus purpureus*, (q) *Aspergillus niger*, (r) *Streptococcus*, (s) *Trichoderma polysporum*. *Solution.* a-r, b-s, c-p, d-q.

**Example 2 (level 4, how many correct).** How many are fungi: *Glomus*, *Rhizobium*, *Trichoderma*, *Anabaena*, *Penicillium*, *Azotobacter*? *Solution.* *Glomus*, *Trichoderma*, *Penicillium*. **Three.**

**Example 3 (level 3, assertion-reason).** A: Baculoviruses are suitable for IPM in ecologically sensitive areas. R: They are species-specific and have a narrow spectrum. *Solution.* Both true; R explains A. **Option 1.**

**Example 4 (level 5, trap).** Which statement is wrong? (a) Activated sludge is partly returned to the aeration tank; (b) Anaerobic digesters release methane, H₂S and CO₂; (c) Primary treatment reduces BOD mainly through flocs; (d) Methanogens live in the rumen of cattle. *Solution.* **(c).** Flocs work in secondary treatment; primary treatment is physical.

<div class="myth">

**"Biocontrol means killing all pests."** The aim is to keep pests at a manageable level, because many insects are useful in the ecosystem.

</div>

## Practise

<div class="qbank" data-bank="entrance-biology" data-topics="microbes-welfare" data-mode="practice" data-exam="neet"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Learn microbe, kind and product together.
- Distilled: whisky, brandy, rum; not distilled: wine, beer.
- Sewage: primary physical, secondary biological (flocs), activated sludge, digesters make biogas.
- High BOD means more organic pollution.
- Free-living nitrogen fixers: <i>Azotobacter</i>, <i>Azospirillum</i>; symbiotic: <i>Rhizobium</i>; <i>Glomus</i> brings phosphorus.

</div>
