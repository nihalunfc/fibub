**Biology · Class 12 · about 22 minutes.** Read [Human reproduction](/learn/courses/medical-entrance/human-reproduction/) first. Here you meet every question type NEET builds from this chapter, then practise.

<div class="bigidea">Human reproduction questions test order (ducts, stages, months), counts (cells, chromosomes, DNA) and sources (which cell or gland makes which hormone). Most wrong answers swap two neighbours in a sequence or two cells that sit side by side.</div>

## The question types

<div class="steps" data-ms="8000">
<div class="step"><b>1. Duct or pathway order.</b> Rete testis → vasa efferentia → epididymis → vas deferens → ejaculatory duct → urethra. Egg: infundibulum → ampulla → isthmus → uterus. Milk: alveoli → tubules → duct → ampulla → lactiferous duct. <i>Trap:</i> vasa efferentia and vas deferens swapped.</div>
<div class="step"><b>2. Cell or part to function.</b> Sertoli (nourish), Leydig (testosterone), acrosome (enzymes to enter the egg), middle piece (mitochondria), myometrium (contractions), fimbriae (catch the egg). <i>Trap:</i> Sertoli and Leydig.</div>
<div class="step"><b>3. Gametogenesis counts.</b> <i>Method:</i> primary spermatocyte → 4 sperm, secondary → 2, spermatid → 1; primary oocyte → 1 ovum. <i>Trap:</i> counting polar bodies as eggs.</div>
<div class="step"><b>4. Ploidy and DNA content.</b> Spermatogonium and primary spermatocyte 2n; secondary spermatocyte, spermatid, sperm n. DNA: primary spermatocyte 4C, secondary 2C, spermatid 1C. <i>Trap:</i> a secondary spermatocyte is n but still 2C.</div>
<div class="step"><b>5. Hormone and source.</b> GnRH (hypothalamus), FSH and LH (anterior pituitary), oxytocin (released from posterior pituitary), estrogen (growing follicle), progesterone (corpus luteum), hCG, hPL, relaxin (placenta).</div>
<div class="step"><b>6. Cycle day to phase.</b> <i>Method:</i> ovulation = cycle length − 14; before it follicular, after it luteal; days 1 to 5 menstrual. <i>Trap:</i> assuming day 14 for every cycle.</div>
<div class="step"><b>7. Arrest stages.</b> Primary oocyte waits in prophase I (from before birth to puberty); secondary oocyte waits in metaphase II until a sperm enters.</div>
<div class="step"><b>8. Early embryo and pregnancy landmarks.</b> Zygote → cleavage → morula (8 to 16 cells) → blastocyst → implantation. Heart at 1 month, limbs and digits by the end of 2 months, organs by 12 weeks, first movements in the 5th month.</div>
<div class="step"><b>9. Statements and assertion-reason.</b> Positive feedback in parturition, sex decided by the sperm, colostrum and IgA, testes outside the body for a lower temperature.</div>
</div>

## Worked examples

**Example 1 (level 4, DNA and ploidy).** A spermatogonium has 4 pg of DNA in G1. What are the DNA amount and ploidy of a secondary spermatocyte and of a sperm? *Solution.* G1 spermatogonium is 2C = 4 pg, so 1C = 2 pg. Primary spermatocyte after S: 4C = 8 pg. Meiosis I halves it: secondary spermatocyte 2C = 4 pg, haploid (n) but each chromosome has two chromatids. Meiosis II: spermatid and sperm 1C = 2 pg, n. **4 pg (n), 2 pg (n).**

**Example 2 (level 4, cycle reading).** In a 32-day cycle, what phase is the woman in on day 15, and which hormone dominates? *Solution.* Ovulation ≈ 32 − 14 = day 18. Day 15 is before it, so follicular phase, with estrogen rising towards its peak. **Follicular, estrogen.**

**Example 3 (level 3, assertion-reason).** A: Progesterone level falls just before menstruation. R: The corpus luteum degenerates when there is no fertilisation. *Solution.* Both true; the loss of the corpus luteum is why progesterone drops, and the drop sheds the lining. **Both true, R explains A.**

**Example 4 (level 5, trap).** Which is correct? (a) Meiosis II of oogenesis completes at ovulation; (b) The trophoblast gives rise to the embryo; (c) hCG maintains the corpus luteum in early pregnancy; (d) Oxytocin release in labour is controlled by negative feedback. *Solution.* **(c).** (a) meiosis II ends at sperm entry; (b) the inner cell mass makes the embryo; (d) it is positive feedback.

<div class="myth">

**"The mother decides whether the baby is a boy or a girl."** Every egg carries an X. The sperm carries X or Y, so the father's gamete decides the sex.

</div>

## Practise

<div class="qbank" data-bank="entrance-biology" data-topics="human-reproduction" data-mode="practice" data-exam="neet"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Learn the duct orders as journeys: sperm, egg and milk.
- Sertoli cells nourish (FSH), Leydig cells make testosterone (LH).
- Primary spermatocyte → 4 sperm; secondary → 2; primary oocyte → 1 ovum.
- Secondary spermatocyte: n but 2C; arrests in oogenesis: prophase I and metaphase II.
- Ovulation ≈ cycle length − 14; corpus luteum makes progesterone; placenta makes hCG, hPL, relaxin.
- Parturition uses positive feedback with oxytocin.

</div>
