**Biology · Class 12 · about 20 minutes.** Read [Reproductive health](/learn/courses/medical-entrance/reproductive-health/) first. Here you meet every question type NEET builds from this chapter, then practise.

<div class="bigidea">This chapter is a set of lists: methods, mechanisms, infections, techniques and dates. Questions pick one item and ask its group, its mechanism or its exception. Learn each list with its odd one out and you can answer almost anything here.</div>

## The question types

<div class="steps" data-ms="8000">
<div class="step"><b>1. Method to mechanism.</b> Copper IUD: Cu ions lower sperm motility. Hormone IUD: uterus unsuitable for implantation, cervix hostile. Pill: stops ovulation and implantation, thickens mucus. Lactational amenorrhoea: no ovulation. <i>Trap:</i> "copper IUDs stop ovulation".</div>
<div class="step"><b>2. Classify methods</b> (how many are barriers, IUDs, natural?). <i>Method:</i> write the group next to each name. <i>Trap:</i> the vault is a barrier, not an IUD; Saheli is a pill.</div>
<div class="step"><b>3. Numbers and dates.</b> 1951 (family planning), 1971 (MTP Act), days 10 to 17 (periodic abstinence), 6 months (lactational amenorrhoea), 72 hours (emergency), 21 days of pills, 12 weeks (safest MTP), 15 to 24 years (STI risk), 18 and 21 (marriage age), 8 blastomeres (ZIFT vs IUT).</div>
<div class="step"><b>4. STIs.</b> <i>Method:</i> remember the three not completely curable: hepatitis B, genital herpes, HIV. <i>Trap:</i> hepatitis B and HIV are not only sexually spread (needles, blood, mother to fetus).</div>
<div class="step"><b>5. Choose the ART.</b> No ova → GIFT (donor ovum). Very low sperm count → AI or IUI, or ICSI. Blocked tubes → IVF with IUT. <i>Trap:</i> GIFT moves gametes, ZIFT moves a zygote.</div>
<div class="step"><b>6. Surgical methods.</b> Vasectomy (vas deferens) and tubectomy (fallopian tube). Hormones and sexual life are unchanged; reversal is poor.</div>
<div class="step"><b>7. Statements on population and programmes.</b> RCH aims, ban on amniocentesis for sex determination, reasons for population growth (falling death rate, MMR and IMR).</div>
<div class="step"><b>8. Matching lists.</b> Method to mechanism, technique to what is transferred. Lock the surest pair first.</div>
</div>

## Worked examples

**Example 1 (level 4, how many correct).** How many of these statements are correct? (i) Condoms protect against STIs; (ii) Saheli is a daily steroidal pill; (iii) Hormone IUDs make the cervix hostile to sperm; (iv) Lactational amenorrhoea works reliably for two years; (v) Emergency pills work within 72 hours. *Solution.* (i) ✓, (ii) ✗ (weekly, non-steroidal), (iii) ✓, (iv) ✗ (about six months), (v) ✓. **Three.**

**Example 2 (level 4, ART choice).** In vitro fertilisation gives an embryo with 16 blastomeres. Where and by which technique is it transferred? *Solution.* More than 8 blastomeres goes to the uterus: **IUT**. Up to 8 would go to the fallopian tube by ZIFT.

**Example 3 (level 3, assertion-reason).** A: Genital herpes and hepatitis B can be treated completely if detected early. R: Early symptoms of most STIs are mild. *Solution.* A is false (these two, with HIV, are not completely curable). R is true. **A false, R true.**

**Example 4 (level 5, trap).** Which pair is wrongly matched? (a) Multiload 375 – copper IUD; (b) Progestasert – hormone-releasing IUD; (c) Lippes loop – copper-releasing IUD; (d) Cervical cap – barrier. *Solution.* **(c).** Lippes loop is non-medicated.

<div class="myth">

**"MTP was legalised to control population."** India legalised it in 1971 with strict conditions; it is a way to end unwanted or unsafe pregnancies, not a method of contraception, and sex-selective use is illegal.

</div>

## Practise

<div class="qbank" data-bank="entrance-biology" data-topics="reproductive-health" data-mode="practice" data-exam="neet"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Learn mechanism per method; copper IUDs act on sperm, hormone IUDs on implantation and the cervix.
- Know the groups and their odd items: vault, Saheli, Lippes loop.
- Dates and numbers: 1951, 1971, days 10 to 17, 6 months, 72 hours, 12 weeks, 8 blastomeres.
- Not completely curable: hepatitis B, genital herpes, HIV.
- GIFT gamete, ZIFT zygote to tube, IUT embryo to uterus, ICSI sperm into egg, IUI semen into uterus.

</div>
