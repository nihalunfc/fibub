**Biology · Class 11 · about 24 minutes.** Read [Photosynthesis in higher plants](/learn/courses/medical-entrance/photosynthesis/) first. Here you meet every question type NEET builds from this chapter, then practise.

<div class="bigidea">Photosynthesis questions test places (grana, stroma, lumen, mesophyll, bundle sheath), sequences (the Z scheme, the three Calvin steps) and counts (3 ATP and 2 NADPH per CO₂). Draw the Z scheme and the Calvin cycle once from memory and most questions become lookups.</div>

## The question types

<div class="steps" data-ms="8000">
<div class="step"><b>1. Scientists and experiments.</b> <i>Trap:</i> Engelmann used Cladophora and bacteria (action spectrum); Calvin used Chlorella and ¹⁴C; van Niel used sulphur bacteria.</div>
<div class="step"><b>2. Pigments and spectra.</b> Colours on a chromatogram; absorption vs action spectrum. <i>Trap:</i> chlorophyll b is yellow-green, chlorophyll a blue-green.</div>
<div class="step"><b>3. Photosystems and the Z scheme.</b> Order of events, which PS splits water, which reduces NADP⁺. <i>Trap:</i> PS II works first despite its number.</div>
<div class="step"><b>4. Cyclic vs non-cyclic.</b> Photosystems used, products, location. <i>Trap:</i> cyclic makes ATP only and happens in stroma lamellae.</div>
<div class="step"><b>5. Chemiosmosis.</b> Where protons build up and why; CF₀ in the membrane, CF₁ in the stroma.</div>
<div class="step"><b>6. Calvin cycle steps and counts.</b> Carboxylation, reduction, regeneration; 3 ATP + 2 NADPH per CO₂; 18 + 12 per glucose. <i>Method:</i> multiply per-CO₂ cost by 6 per glucose.</div>
<div class="step"><b>7. C4 pathway.</b> Acceptor, enzyme, first product, cell types, extra ATP. <i>Trap:</i> mesophyll of C4 plants has no RuBisCO.</div>
<div class="step"><b>8. Photorespiration.</b> When and where; products (PGA + phosphoglycolate); no ATP or sugar gained. <i>Trap:</i> it happens in C3, not C4, plants.</div>
<div class="step"><b>9. Limiting factors.</b> Blackman's law, light saturation (about 10%), CO₂ saturation (C4 near 360, C3 beyond 450 µL/L), greenhouse CO₂.</div>
</div>

## Worked examples

**Example 1 (level 4, counts).** How many turns of the Calvin cycle, ATP and NADPH make 3 glucose molecules in a C3 plant? *Solution.* 3 glucose = 18 CO₂ = 18 turns; ATP = 18 × 3 = **54**; NADPH = 18 × 2 = **36**. In a C4 plant, ATP would be 18 × 5 = 90.

**Example 2 (level 4, assertion-reason).** A: C4 plants show little or no photorespiration. R: The C4 pathway raises the CO₂ concentration around RuBisCO in bundle sheath cells. *Solution.* Both true, and R explains A: with plenty of CO₂, RuBisCO acts as a carboxylase. **Both true; R explains A.**

**Example 3 (level 5, sequence).** Put in order: (a) NADP⁺ reduced, (b) P680 excited, (c) water split, (d) P700 excited, (e) electrons through cytochromes. *Solution.* b → c → e → d → a. (Water splitting refills PS II right after it loses its electron.)

**Example 4 (level 5, statements).** Statement I: In cyclic photophosphorylation O₂ is released. Statement II: Stroma lamellae lack PS II and NADP reductase. *Solution.* Cyclic flow uses only PS I and splits no water, so no O₂: I is false. II is true, and it is why only cyclic flow happens there. **I incorrect, II correct.**

<div class="myth">

**"O₂ comes from CO₂."** All the O₂ comes from water split at PS II. That is why the balanced equation shows 12 H₂O for 6 O₂.

</div>

<div class="meaning">
<dl>
<dt>Per CO₂</dt><dd>C3: 3 ATP, 2 NADPH; C4: 5 ATP, 2 NADPH</dd>
<dt>Per O₂</dt><dd>2 H₂O split, 4 electrons, enough for 2 NADPH</dd>

## Practise

<div class="qbank" data-bank="entrance-biology" data-topics="photosynthesis" data-mode="practice" data-exam="neet"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- PS II splits water and works first; PS I reduces NADP⁺.
- Cyclic: PS I only, stroma lamellae, ATP only; non-cyclic: ATP, NADPH, O₂.
- Protons pile up in the lumen; ATP made as they return through CF₀–CF₁.
- C3: RuBP, RuBisCO, 3-PGA; 18 ATP + 12 NADPH per glucose.
- C4: PEP, PEP carboxylase, OAA in mesophyll; Calvin cycle in bundle sheath; 30 ATP per glucose.
- Photorespiration: RuBisCO with O₂, in C3 plants, wastes energy.

</div>
