**Biology · Class 12 · about 22 minutes.** Read [Sexual reproduction in flowering plants](/learn/courses/medical-entrance/plant-reproduction/) first. Here you meet every question type NEET builds from this chapter, then practise.

<div class="bigidea">Almost every question here is one of three checks: which generation a cell belongs to (n, 2n or 3n), how many divisions or nuclei a structure needs, and which example goes with which term. Learn the generations and the example lists, and the chapter becomes quick marks.</div>

## The question types

<div class="steps" data-ms="8000">
<div class="step"><b>1. Ploidy of a cell.</b> "Leaf 2n = 24; how many chromosomes in the aleurone layer?" <i>Method:</i> sort the cell: gametophyte (n), parent tissue or embryo (2n), endosperm and aleurone (3n). <i>Trap:</i> nucellus, integuments, seed coat and pericarp belong to the mother plant, so they are 2n even though they sit next to n cells.</div>
<div class="step"><b>2. Counting meioses and mitoses.</b> <i>Method:</i> 1 meiosis = 4 pollen but only 1 embryo sac. From megaspore to embryo sac: 3 mitoses. From microspore to male gametes: 2 mitoses (one makes the generative cell, one splits it). <i>Trap:</i> forgetting that three megaspores die.</div>
<div class="step"><b>3. Embryo sac counts.</b> 7 cells, 8 nuclei; egg apparatus = egg + 2 synergids; 3 antipodals; 2 polar nuclei in 1 central cell. <i>Trap:</i> "8-celled".</div>
<div class="step"><b>4. Wall layers and pollen.</b> Epidermis, endothecium, middle layers, tapetum (inner, nourishing). Exine of sporopollenin with germ pores; intine of cellulose and pectin. <i>Trap:</i> swapping exine and intine materials.</div>
<div class="step"><b>5. Pollination type and agent.</b> <i>Method:</i> ask where the pollen comes from (same flower, same plant, other plant) and what each agent needs. <i>Trap:</i> geitonogamy is genetically self; most aquatic plants are not water-pollinated.</div>
<div class="step"><b>6. Outbreeding devices.</b> <i>Trap:</i> monoecy (castor, maize) stops autogamy only; dioecy (papaya) stops autogamy and geitonogamy.</div>
<div class="step"><b>7. Double fertilisation and products.</b> Syngamy gives the 2n zygote, triple fusion the 3n PEN. Ovule → seed, ovary → fruit, integuments → seed coat, ovary wall → pericarp. <i>Trap:</i> "fertilisation makes the fruit wall from the integuments".</div>
<div class="step"><b>8. Examples to terms.</b> Albuminous (wheat, maize, castor) vs non-albuminous (pea, groundnut); perisperm (black pepper, beet); polyembryony (citrus, mango); parthenocarpy (banana); false fruit (apple, strawberry, cashew); cleistogamy (<i>Viola, Oxalis, Commelina</i>).</div>
<div class="step"><b>9. Statements and assertion-reason.</b> NCERT lines with one word changed: "always", "only", "never". Mark each line true or false before reading the options.</div>
</div>

## Worked examples

**Example 1 (level 4, ploidy chain).** In a plant, the generative cell of the pollen has 12 chromosomes. How many chromosomes are in the cells of (i) the endosperm, (ii) the seed coat, (iii) the scutellum? *Solution.* n = 12. Endosperm 3n = 36. Seed coat comes from integuments (mother plant) = 24. Scutellum is part of the embryo = 24. **36, 24, 24.**

**Example 2 (level 4, counting).** How many mitotic divisions happen in forming one mature embryo sac from a megaspore mother cell, and one 3-celled pollen grain from a microspore mother cell? *Solution.* Embryo sac: 1 meiosis, then 3 free-nuclear mitoses (1 → 2 → 4 → 8). Pollen: 1 meiosis gives four microspores; for one microspore, 1 mitosis gives vegetative and generative cells, and 1 more divides the generative cell. **3 and 2.**

**Example 3 (level 3, assertion-reason).** A: A dioecious plant cannot show geitonogamy. R: In dioecious plants each plant bears flowers of one sex only. *Solution.* Geitonogamy needs a pollen-producing flower and a pistil on the same plant. Each dioecious plant has only one kind, so A is true and R explains it. **Both true, R explains A.**

**Example 4 (level 5, trap).** Which statement is correct? (a) Apomictic seeds of a hybrid would show segregation of traits in the next generation; (b) In cleistogamous flowers xenogamy is common; (c) Polar nuclei are found in the egg apparatus; (d) Nucellar embryos in citrus are genetically identical to the mother plant. *Solution.* **(d).** Nucellar cells are maternal 2n tissue, so these embryos are clones. (a) is the reverse: apomixis prevents segregation. (b) cleistogamous flowers never open. (c) polar nuclei sit in the central cell.

<div class="myth">

**"Pollen grains are male gametes."** The pollen grain is the male gametophyte; the male gametes are the two cells made by the generative cell inside it.

</div>

## Practise

<div class="qbank" data-bank="entrance-biology" data-topics="plant-reproduction" data-mode="practice" data-exam="neet"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Sort every cell into n, 2n or 3n by tracing where it came from.
- 1 meiosis gives 4 pollen grains but 1 embryo sac; seeds need seeds + seeds ÷ 4 meioses.
- Embryo sac: 7 cells, 8 nuclei, 3 mitoses; pollen: 2 mitoses to reach 3 cells.
- Geitonogamy is genetically self; dioecy blocks both kinds of self-pollination.
- Learn the example lists: albuminous, perisperm, polyembryony, parthenocarpy, false fruits, cleistogamy.

</div>
