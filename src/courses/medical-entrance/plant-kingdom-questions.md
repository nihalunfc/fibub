**Biology · Class 11 · about 22 minutes.** Read [Plant kingdom](/learn/courses/medical-entrance/plant-kingdom/) first. Here you meet every question type NEET builds from this chapter, then practise.

<div class="bigidea">Plant kingdom questions test a grid: for each group, which phase is n or 2n and which is dominant, and for each algal class, its pigment, food, wall and examples. NEET builds traps by swapping one cell of the grid.</div>

## The question types

<div class="steps" data-ms="8000">
<div class="step"><b>1. Algal class from a clue.</b> "Stored food laminarin" or "phycoerythrin". <i>Method:</i> keep the 3 × 5 table (pigment, food, wall, flagella, examples). <i>Trap:</i> floridean starch (red) versus starch (green); chlorophyll c (brown) versus d (red).</div>
<div class="step"><b>2. Algal examples and products.</b> Agar (Gelidium, Gracilaria), algin (brown), carrageen (red), Chlorella (protein). <i>Trap:</i> Chara and Volvox are green, Sargassum and Dictyota brown, Porphyra and Polysiphonia red.</div>
<div class="step"><b>3. Gamete types.</b> Isogamy, anisogamy, oogamy with one example each. <i>Trap:</i> Spirogyra gametes are alike and have no flagella, yet it is still isogamous.</div>
<div class="step"><b>4. Ploidy of a structure.</b> "Which is haploid: protonema, seta, capsule wall, prothallus?" <i>Method:</i> anything before fertilisation and after meiosis is n. <i>Trap:</i> the moss capsule and seta are 2n; spores, protonema, prothallus and gemmae are n; endosperm in angiosperms is 3n, in gymnosperms n.</div>
<div class="step"><b>5. Life cycle pattern.</b> Haplontic, diplontic, haplo-diplontic with examples. <i>Trap:</i> Fucus (diplontic) and Ectocarpus, Polysiphonia, kelps (haplo-diplontic) are the algal exceptions.</div>
<div class="step"><b>6. Bryophytes vs pteridophytes.</b> Dominant phase, vascular tissue, true roots, need for water. <i>Trap:</i> both need water for fertilisation.</div>
<div class="step"><b>7. Heterospory and the seed habit.</b> Selaginella and Salvinia. <i>Trap:</i> Equisetum and ferns are homosporous.</div>
<div class="step"><b>8. Gymnosperm features.</b> Naked ovules, coralloid roots, mycorrhiza, sunken stomata, cones, wind pollination, archegonia.</div>
<div class="step"><b>9. Classification systems.</b> Artificial, natural, phylogenetic, numerical, cyto- and chemotaxonomy. <i>Trap:</i> Linnaeus used the androecium (artificial); Bentham and Hooker were natural.</div>
</div>

## Worked examples

**Example 1 (level 4, ploidy count).** How many are haploid: moss protonema, fern prothallus, moss seta, pine endosperm, angiosperm endosperm, *Marchantia* gemma? *Solution.* Protonema n ✓, prothallus n ✓, seta 2n ✗, pine endosperm n ✓ (it is the female gametophyte, formed before fertilisation), angiosperm endosperm 3n ✗, gemma n ✓. **Four.**

**Example 2 (level 4, assertion-reason).** A: Red algae can grow at greater depths in the sea than green algae. R: r-phycoerythrin absorbs the blue-green light that penetrates deep water. *Solution.* Both true, and R is the reason. **Both true; R explains A.**

**Example 3 (level 5, match the lists).** List I: *Laminaria*, *Gelidium*, *Chlorella*, *Sphagnum*. List II: peat, agar, algin, protein supplement. *Solution.* *Sphagnum* gives peat (surest). *Gelidium* gives agar. *Laminaria* is a brown alga: algin. *Chlorella*: protein supplement.

**Example 4 (level 5, statements).** Statement I: In pteridophytes, the gametophyte is the dominant, long-lived phase. Statement II: Pteridophytes are the first land plants with xylem and phloem. *Solution.* The fern plant is the sporophyte, and the prothallus is small and short-lived, so I is false. II is true. **I incorrect, II correct.**

<div class="myth">

**"Gymnosperm endosperm is triploid like that of angiosperms."** It is the haploid female gametophyte, made before fertilisation. Only angiosperms have double fertilisation and a 3n endosperm.

</div>

## Practise

<div class="qbank" data-bank="entrance-biology" data-topics="plant-kingdom" data-mode="practice" data-exam="neet"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Algae grid: pigment, food, wall, flagella, examples for green, brown and red.
- Agar from Gelidium and Gracilaria, algin from brown, carrageen from red algae.
- Ploidy from the life cycle: spores, protonema, prothallus, gemmae, gymnosperm endosperm are n.
- Algal life cycle exceptions: Fucus diplontic; Ectocarpus, Polysiphonia, kelps haplo-diplontic.
- Selaginella and Salvinia are heterosporous; Cycas has coralloid roots, Pinus mycorrhiza.
- Linnaeus artificial; Bentham and Hooker natural.
