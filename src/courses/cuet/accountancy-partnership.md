**Module 10 · about 18 minutes.** By the end you will know the default rules, keep capital accounts both ways and prepare a profit and loss appropriation account.

<div class="bigidea">A partnership's profit is shared in two stages: first the partners' agreed rewards (interest on capital, salaries, commission), then whatever is left in the profit-sharing ratio.</div>

## 1. Deed or no deed

A **partnership deed** records the ratio, interest, salaries and other terms. Where it is silent, the **Indian Partnership Act, 1932** fills the gap:

| Item | Without a deed |
|---|---|
| Profit-sharing ratio | equal |
| Interest on capital | none |
| Interest on drawings | none |
| Salary or commission to a partner | none |
| Interest on a partner's loan to the firm | 6% a year |

## 2. The appropriation account

<figure class="fig" data-fig="appropriation"><figcaption>Change the profit, the rate of interest on capital and B's salary, and see how much each partner takes home.</figcaption></figure>

<div class="steps" data-title="Order of the appropriation account">
<div class="step">Start with net profit from the profit and loss account (after interest on partners' loans, which is a charge, not an appropriation).</div>
<div class="step">Add interest on drawings (partners pay it to the firm).</div>
<div class="step">Take away interest on capital, partners' salaries and commission.</div>
<div class="step">What is left is the divisible profit, shared in the profit-sharing ratio and credited to capital or current accounts.</div>

## 3. Fixed or fluctuating capital

With **fixed capital**, the capital account holds only the capital put in or taken out; interest, salary, drawings and profit go to a separate **current account**. With **fluctuating capital**, everything goes through the one capital account, so its balance changes every year.

<div class="myth">

**"Interest on a partner's loan is shared like profit."** It is a charge against profit, paid whether or not there is a profit, and is debited to the profit and loss account, not the appropriation account.

</div>

## 4. Practice

<div class="qbank" data-bank="cuet-accountancy" data-topics="partnership-fundamentals" data-n="10" data-mode="practice" data-type="mcq"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- No deed: equal shares, no interest on capital or drawings, no salary, 6% on partners' loans.
- Appropriation order: net profit + interest on drawings − interest on capital − salaries/commission = divisible profit.
- Interest on partners' loans is a charge, not an appropriation.
- Drawings at start / middle / end of each month: 6.5 / 6 / 5.5 months.

</div>
