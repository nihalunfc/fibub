**Module 5 · about 16 minutes.** By the end you will answer the everyday science questions in general awareness by linking each fact to something familiar.

<div class="bigidea">General awareness science is Class 8 to 10 science asked about everyday life. If you can explain why the sky is blue or why ice floats, you can answer most of it.</div>

## 1. Physics around you

<div class="steps" data-title="Five everyday puzzles">
<div class="step"><b>Why is the sky blue and the sunset red?</b> Air scatters blue light most. At sunset light crosses more air, the blue is scattered away, and red is left.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Why does a straw look bent in water?</b> Light bends (refracts) as it moves between water and air at different speeds.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Why do we see lightning before we hear thunder?</b> Light travels at about 300,000 km/s; sound in air at about 343 m/s.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Why does ice float?</b> Water expands when it freezes, so ice is less dense than liquid water.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Why does a pressure cooker cook faster?</b> Higher pressure raises the boiling point of water above 100 °C.</div>
</div>

**Units to know:** force newton (N), energy joule (J), power watt (W), pressure pascal (Pa), current ampere (A), resistance ohm (Ω), frequency hertz (Hz). Each is named after a scientist.

## 2. The human body and health

| Deficiency | Disease |
|---|---|
| Vitamin A | night blindness |
| Vitamin B1 | beriberi |
| Vitamin C | scurvy |
| Vitamin D | rickets |
| Iodine | goitre |
| Iron | anaemia |

Diseases spread in different ways: malaria and dengue by mosquitoes, cholera and typhoid by contaminated water, tuberculosis by air. Insulin from the pancreas controls blood sugar; red blood cells carry oxygen using haemoglobin.

## 3. Chemistry at home

Baking soda is sodium hydrogen carbonate; washing soda is sodium carbonate; common salt is sodium chloride; bleaching powder releases chlorine to disinfect water. Acids turn blue litmus red; bases turn red litmus blue; the pH scale runs from 0 (strongly acidic) through 7 (neutral) to 14 (strongly basic).

<div class="myth">

**"Heavier objects fall faster."** Without air resistance, all objects fall with the same acceleration (about 9.8 m/s²). A feather falls slowly only because of air; in a vacuum it falls as fast as a hammer.
