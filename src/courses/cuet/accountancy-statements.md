**Module 10 · about 15 minutes.** By the end you will place items in the Schedule III balance sheet and read comparative and common-size statements.

<div class="bigidea">Financial statements follow a fixed layout so anyone can compare companies. Analysis turns the raw numbers into changes over time (comparative) and shares of a total (common-size).</div>

## 1. Where items go

| Heading | Examples |
|---|---|
| Shareholders' funds | share capital; reserves and surplus (securities premium, general reserve, surplus) |
| Non-current liabilities | long-term borrowings (debentures, long-term loans), long-term provisions |
| Current liabilities | short-term borrowings, trade payables, other current liabilities, short-term provisions |
| Non-current assets | property, plant and equipment; intangible assets; non-current investments |
| Current assets | current investments, inventories, trade receivables, cash and cash equivalents |

**Try it:** Press Next entry and watch the two accounts named in each entry change.

## 2. Two analysis tools

<div class="steps" data-title="Revenue ₹10,00,000 last year, ₹12,50,000 this year; expenses ₹6,00,000 and ₹7,00,000">
<div class="step"><b>Comparative:</b> change in revenue = ₹2,50,000, a 25% rise; expenses rose ₹1,00,000, 16.7%.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Common-size:</b> expenses were 60% of revenue last year and 56% this year.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Reading it:</b> revenue grew faster than costs, so the profit margin improved from 40% to 44%.</div>
</div>

<div class="myth">

**"A bigger profit always means better performance."** Compare profit with revenue and capital: a larger profit on much larger revenue can still be a smaller margin.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Schedule III: equity and liabilities (shareholders' funds, non-current, current), then assets (non-current, current).
- Due within 12 months or the operating cycle: current.
- Comparative statements show change; common-size statements show shares of a base.
- Common-size base: revenue for profit and loss, total assets for the balance sheet.
