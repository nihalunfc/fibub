**Module 9 · about 22 minutes.** By the end you will trace a flower from pollen and ovule to seed and fruit, and answer every ploidy question.

<div class="bigidea">A flower is a reproductive shoot. The stamen makes pollen (the male side), the pistil holds ovules (the female side), and after pollination two separate fusions, double fertilisation, give both the embryo and its food store.</div>

**Everyday picture:** a coconut. The white flesh and the water are endosperm, the triploid food store made by the second fertilisation. The tiny embryo near one "eye" is what grows into a new palm.

## 1. Making the gametes

<div class="steps" data-title="From flower to gametes">
<div class="step"><b>Anther:</b> pollen mother cells (2n) divide by meiosis into tetrads of microspores (n).</div>
<div class="step"><b>Pollen grain:</b> a microspore divides by mitosis into a large vegetative cell and a small generative cell. The generative cell later makes two male gametes.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Ovule:</b> a megaspore mother cell (2n) divides by meiosis; three megaspores die and one (n) survives.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Embryo sac:</b> the surviving megaspore's nucleus divides three times by mitosis: 8 nuclei in 7 cells (egg apparatus of egg + 2 synergids, 3 antipodals, central cell with 2 polar nuclei).</div>
</div>

## 2. Pollination

| Kind | Carried by | Flower features |
|---|---|---|
| Wind | air | light, dry, plenty of pollen; feathery stigmas (grasses, maize) |
| Water | water | rare; Vallisneria, Hydrilla, seagrasses (Zostera) |
| Insects and animals | bees, birds, bats | large, coloured, scented, nectar; sticky pollen |

Plants avoid self-pollination by releasing pollen and readying the stigma at different times, placing anther and stigma apart, self-incompatibility (a genetic block), or having separate male and female flowers (unisexual flowers, as in papaya on separate plants). Artificial hybridisation uses emasculation (removing anthers) and bagging.

## 3. Double fertilisation and after

The pollen tube grows through the style and enters a synergid. One male gamete + egg → zygote (2n) → embryo. The other + 2 polar nuclei → primary endosperm nucleus (3n) → endosperm. The ovule becomes the seed, the ovary becomes the fruit. Apomixis makes seeds without fertilisation; polyembryony gives more than one embryo in a seed (citrus, mango).

<div class="myth">

**"Every fruit comes from a fertilised ovary."** Parthenocarpic fruits such as banana develop without fertilisation, and so have no real seeds. Some false fruits, like apple, form mainly from the thalamus.

</div>
