**Module 11 · about 15 minutes.** By the end you will classify workers and kinds of unemployment and explain sustainable development.

<div class="bigidea">Jobs and the environment are two sides of development: an economy must create enough good work for its people without using up the natural resources its children will need.</div>

<div class="steps" data-title="Employment">
<div class="step"><b>Workers:</b> anyone in a productive activity, including the self-employed and casual wage workers.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Formal and informal:</b> formal jobs have regular wages and social security; most Indian workers are in the informal sector.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Unemployment types:</b> open, disguised (more workers than needed, common in farming), seasonal (between harvests).</div>
</div>

<div class="steps" data-title="Environment">
<div class="step"><b>Four functions:</b> supplies resources, absorbs waste (sink), sustains life, provides beauty and enjoyment.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Carrying capacity:</b> use resources slower than they renew and produce waste slower than it can be absorbed.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Sustainable development:</b> meeting today's needs without harming future generations' ability to meet theirs: renewable energy, conservation, cleaner technology.</div>
</div>

<div class="myth">

**"Protecting the environment always costs jobs."** Renewable energy, efficient technology and conservation also create work; the real trade-off is between short-term use and long-term costs.

</div>
