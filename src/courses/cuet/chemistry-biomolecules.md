**Module 7 · about 20 minutes.** By the end you will describe the main classes of biomolecules and their key structures and tests. It links straight to [molecular inheritance in Biology](/learn/courses/cuet/biology-molecular-inheritance/).

<div class="bigidea">Life is built from a few kinds of large molecules made by joining small units: sugars into carbohydrates, amino acids into proteins, nucleotides into DNA and RNA. Each join releases a water molecule.</div>

**Everyday picture:** a necklace of beads. The beads are the small units; the string is the bond that joins them (glycosidic in sugars, peptide in proteins, phosphodiester in DNA). The order of the beads holds the information.

## 1. Carbohydrates

| Type | Examples | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| Monosaccharides | glucose (aldohexose), fructose (ketohexose) | glucose exists mostly as a ring (α and β anomers) |
| Disaccharides | sucrose (glucose + fructose), maltose (2 glucose), lactose (glucose + galactose) | sucrose is non-reducing; maltose and lactose reduce |
| Polysaccharides | starch (amylose + amylopectin, α-links), cellulose (β-links), glycogen | humans digest α-links but not β-links, so we can eat starch but not grass |

## 2. Proteins

<div class="steps" data-title="Four levels of protein structure">
<div class="step"><b>Primary:</b> the order of amino acids, joined by peptide bonds (−CO−NH−).</div>
<div class="step"><b>Secondary:</b> the chain coils into an α-helix or lies in β-pleated sheets, held by hydrogen bonds.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Tertiary:</b> the whole chain folds into a 3-D shape (fibrous like keratin, or globular like insulin).</div>
<div class="step"><b>Quaternary:</b> several chains pack together, as in haemoglobin (four chains).</div>
<div class="step"><b>Denaturation</b> undoes levels 2 to 4 but leaves the primary chain. The egg white turns solid and stays solid.</div>
</div>

Amino acids exist as zwitterions (H₃N⁺−CHR−COO⁻). Essential amino acids must come from food. Enzymes are protein catalysts: very specific (lock and key) and they work by lowering activation energy.

## 3. Vitamins, nucleic acids, hormones

| Group | Facts |
|---|---|
| Fat-soluble vitamins | A (night blindness), D (rickets), E, K (blood clotting) |
| Water-soluble vitamins | B group (B₁ beriberi, B₁₂ pernicious anaemia), C (scurvy) |
| DNA | deoxyribose, bases A, G, C, T, double helix |
| RNA | ribose, bases A, G, C, U, usually single strand |
| Nucleoside / nucleotide | base + sugar / base + sugar + phosphate |

<div class="myth">

**"Cellulose and starch are different sugars."** Both are made only of glucose. The difference is how the units are linked (β in cellulose, α in starch), and that one difference makes cotton tough and rice digestible.

</div>
