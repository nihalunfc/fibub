**Module 9 · about 24 minutes.** By the end you will describe gametogenesis, the menstrual cycle and pregnancy, and the main methods of contraception and assisted reproduction.

<div class="bigidea">Humans make gametes by meiosis, join them inside the female body, and grow the embryo in the uterus with the placenta as its life-support link. Hormones run every step, like a timetable.</div>

**Everyday picture:** the menstrual cycle is a monthly room booking. Each month the uterus lining is prepared (the room made ready); if no embryo arrives, the booking is cancelled and the lining is shed, which is menstruation.

## 1. Making gametes

| | Spermatogenesis | Oogenesis |
|---|---|---|
| Where | seminiferous tubules of the testis | ovary |
| Starts | puberty, continues for life | before birth; paused, resumes at puberty one per month |
| Products of one primary cell | 4 sperms | 1 ovum + polar bodies |
| Helped by | Sertoli cells (nourish), Leydig cells (testosterone) | follicle cells |

## 2. The cycle and pregnancy

<div class="steps" data-title="A 28-day menstrual cycle">
<div class="step"><b>Days 1-5, menstrual phase:</b> the lining breaks down and is shed.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Days 5-13, follicular phase:</b> FSH grows a follicle; oestrogen rebuilds the lining.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Day 14, ovulation:</b> the LH surge releases the ovum.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Days 15-28, luteal phase:</b> the empty follicle becomes the corpus luteum and makes progesterone, keeping the lining ready.</div>
<div class="step"><b>If fertilised:</b> the embryo (blastocyst) implants, hCG keeps the corpus luteum alive and periods stop. If not, the corpus luteum fades and the cycle restarts.</div>
</div>

Pregnancy lasts about 9 months. The placenta passes food and oxygen, removes wastes, and makes hormones (hCG, hPL, oestrogen, progesterone). Birth (parturition) is driven by oxytocin making the uterus contract.

## 3. Reproductive health

| Topic | Key facts |
|---|---|
| Natural methods | periodic abstinence, withdrawal, lactational amenorrhoea (up to ~6 months) |
| Barriers | condoms, diaphragms, cervical caps |
| IUDs | copper-T (copper reduces sperm movement), hormone-releasing IUDs |
| Pills | progestogen or progestogen-oestrogen; stop ovulation |
| Surgical | vasectomy, tubectomy (very hard to reverse) |
| STIs | gonorrhoea, syphilis, genital herpes, HIV, hepatitis B; condoms and early treatment |
| Assisted reproduction | IVF with embryo transfer (ZIFT, IUT), GIFT, ICSI, artificial insemination |
| Law | amniocentesis for sex determination is banned in India; MTP is legal under set conditions |

<div class="myth">

**"Contraceptive pills protect against infections."** They only prevent pregnancy. Condoms are the method that also lowers the risk of sexually transmitted infections.

</div>
