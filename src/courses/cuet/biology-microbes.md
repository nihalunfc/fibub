**Module 9 · about 16 minutes.** A short chapter of facts that score fast. By the end you will match each microbe to what it gives us.

<div class="bigidea">Most microbes are not germs. They ferment our food, make our medicines, clean our waste water, fuel biogas plants and feed our crops.</div>

**Everyday picture:** idli and dosa batter rises overnight because bacteria and yeasts ferment it and release carbon dioxide, the same gas that makes bread dough puff up.

## 1. Microbes at home and in industry

| Product | Microbe |
|---|---|
| Curd | Lactobacillus |
| Bread, beer, wine | Saccharomyces cerevisiae (baker's / brewer's yeast) |
| Swiss cheese (big holes) | Propionibacterium sharmanii |
| Roquefort cheese | fungi that ripen it |
| Penicillin (first antibiotic) | Penicillium notatum (Fleming; Chain and Florey) |
| Citric acid | Aspergillus niger |
| Lactic acid / acetic acid | Lactobacillus / Acetobacter aceti |
| Streptokinase (clot buster) | Streptococcus |
| Cyclosporin A (immunosuppressant) | Trichoderma polysporum |
| Statins (lower cholesterol) | Monascus purpureus |
| Lipases (detergents), pectinases (clear juices) | microbes grown in fermenters |

## 2. Sewage, biogas and farms

<div class="steps" data-title="Treating sewage">
<div class="step"><b>Primary treatment:</b> filtering and settling remove floating and heavy solids (primary sludge).</div>
<div class="step"><b>Secondary treatment:</b> air is pumped in so aerobic microbes form flocs and eat organic matter. This cuts the BOD (biochemical oxygen demand).</div>
<div class="step"><b>Activated sludge</b> settles; some goes back into the tank as a starter.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Anaerobic digesters</b> break down the rest of the sludge, giving biogas.</div>
</div>

Biogas plants (developed in India by IARI and KVIC) feed cattle dung into a tank where methanogens make methane for cooking and lighting. **Biocontrol:** ladybirds eat aphids, Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) kills caterpillars, Trichoderma fights plant fungi, and baculoviruses target specific insects. **Biofertilisers:** Rhizobium in legume root nodules and free-living Azotobacter fix nitrogen; mycorrhizal fungi (Glomus) help roots absorb phosphorus; cyanobacteria (Anabaena, Nostoc) enrich paddy fields.

<div class="myth">

**"A high BOD means clean water."** The opposite: BOD measures how much oxygen microbes need to break down the organic matter present. More pollution means a higher BOD.

</div>
