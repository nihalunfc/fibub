**Module 9 · about 22 minutes.** By the end you will match diseases to their causes and explain immunity, vaccination, AIDS and cancer.

<div class="bigidea">Disease comes from pathogens, faulty body cells or harmful habits. The immune system defends us in two layers: a fast general one we are born with, and a slower, precise one that remembers past enemies.</div>

**Everyday picture:** a vaccine is a "wanted" poster. It shows the immune system a harmless picture of the criminal (a weakened or inactivated pathogen, or one of its proteins), so the real one is recognised and caught fast if it ever turns up.

## 2. Immunity

<div class="steps" data-title="How the body remembers">
<div class="step"><b>Innate immunity:</b> skin, mucus, stomach acid, phagocytes, fever and interferons. Fast but general.</div>
<div class="step"><b>First exposure:</b> B cells make antibodies (humoral) and T cells attack infected cells (cell-mediated). This primary response is slow and weak.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Memory cells</b> remain after the infection is cleared.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Second exposure:</b> memory cells launch a fast, strong secondary response, often before you feel ill.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Vaccination</b> causes the first exposure safely. Active immunity is your own; passive immunity is ready-made antibodies (colostrum, anti-venom).</div>
</div>

Allergy is an over-reaction to harmless things (pollen, dust mites) by IgE and histamine. Autoimmunity is when the body attacks itself (rheumatoid arthritis). Primary lymphoid organs are bone marrow and thymus; secondary ones include spleen, lymph nodes, tonsils and MALT.

## 3. AIDS, cancer and drugs

**AIDS:** HIV is a retrovirus. It enters helper T cells, uses reverse transcriptase to copy its RNA into DNA, and slowly destroys them. Spread by unprotected sex, infected blood and shared needles, and from mother to child; diagnosed by ELISA. No cure, but antiretroviral drugs keep people healthy for many years. **Cancer:** cells lose contact inhibition and divide without control; malignant tumours spread (metastasis). Causes include carcinogens (tobacco smoke, UV, X-rays) and oncogenic viruses. **Drugs:** opioids (heroin), cannabinoids, cocaine; long-term alcohol abuse damages the liver (cirrhosis).

<div class="myth">

**"Antibiotics cure viral diseases like the common cold."** Antibiotics act on bacteria. They do nothing against viruses, and misuse breeds resistant bacteria.

</div>
