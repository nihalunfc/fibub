**Module 12 · 60 minutes plus review.** A full CUET General Test paper: 50 questions, 60 minutes, +5 for each right answer, −1 for each wrong one, 0 for a blank. A new paper is drawn every time, so you can take it again and again.

<div class="bigidea">Treat this like the real thing: a quiet room, a timer, no notes, and no pausing. Then spend at least as long reviewing as you spent on the test.</div>

## Before you start

The paper mixes numerical ability, logical and analytical reasoning and general awareness. The real General Test also asks current affairs questions; this mock leaves them out because they change every month, so keep up with them using the method in Lesson 5.5.

<div class="steps" data-title="How to use a mock">
<div class="step"><b>Sit it in one go.</b> 60 minutes, no breaks, three rounds as in Lesson 1.2: sure ones, workable ones, educated guesses.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Read the report.</b> Your score out of 250, accuracy, and marks by topic.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Review every question you got wrong or guessed.</b> Read the full solution and write one line on why you slipped: did not know, misread, ran out of time, or careless.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Go back to the lesson</b> for any topic where you lost three or more questions, and do one practice set before your next mock.</div>
</div>
