**Module 2 · about 14 minutes.** By the end you will fill blanks using grammar clues, signal words and collocations, and work through a cloze passage.

<div class="bigidea">Decide what kind of word the blank needs before you look at the options: grammar narrows the choice, signal words give the direction, and collocations settle the last two.</div>

## 1. Three questions for every blank

<div class="steps" data-title="Fill a blank in three questions">
<div class="step"><b>What part of speech?</b> After "a very ___ idea" you need an adjective; after "she ___ the letter" a verb in the right tense.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Which direction?</b> Signal words: "although", "but", "despite" (contrast); "because", "so", "therefore" (cause and result); "and", "moreover" (same direction).</div>
<div class="step"><b>Which word goes with its neighbours?</b> Collocations: heavy rain (not strong rain), make a mistake (not do), commit a crime, pay attention, take a step.</div>
<div class="step"><b>Read it back.</b> Read the whole sentence with your choice in it. If it sounds forced or changes the meaning, look again.</div>
</div>

## 2. Double blanks and cloze passages

In **double blanks**, fill the easier blank first and keep only options whose word fits it; then check the second word. In a **cloze passage** (a paragraph with five blanks), read the whole paragraph once first: the topic and tone tell you a lot, and a later sentence often explains an earlier blank.

<div class="myth">

**"The most difficult-looking word is usually the answer."** Examiners include hard words as distractors. The right answer is the one that fits the grammar, the direction and the collocation, simple or not.

</div>

## 3. Practice

<div class="qbank" data-bank="cuet-english" data-topics="fill-in-blanks" data-n="12" data-mode="practice" data-type="mcq"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Part of speech first, then direction from signal words, then collocation.
- Contrast words: although, but, despite, however. Result words: so, therefore, hence.
- Double blank: settle the surer blank first. Cloze: read the whole paragraph first.
- Hard-looking words are often traps.
