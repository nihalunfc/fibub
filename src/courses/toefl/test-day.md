**Module 1 · about 11 minutes.** By the end you will know which way of taking the test suits you, what happens on the day, and the checks that keep a good score from being lost to a technical or ID problem.

<div class="bigidea">The same test can be taken at a test centre or at home with an online proctor. Pick the one where you can control the room, the computer and the connection.</div>

## 1. Test centre or Home Edition?

| | Test centre | Home Edition |
|---|---|---|
| Where | An approved centre | Your own private room |
| Equipment | Provided | Your computer, webcam, microphone, speakers or headset; it must pass ETS's checks |
| Supervision | Staff in the room | A live human proctor watching online |
| Notes | Materials provided | Only the note-taking materials ETS allows, wiped at the end |
| Good for | People without a quiet room, a reliable computer or a stable connection | People with a quiet room and reliable equipment, or far from a centre |

Some universities and organisations accept only one of the two. Check before you book.

## 2. Test day, step by step

<div class="steps">
<div class="step">

**Before.** Check your booking, the ID rules and, for Home Edition, run the official system check on the computer you will use.

</div>
<div class="step">

**Check-in.** At a centre: ID check, photo, locker for your things. At home: the proctor checks your ID by webcam and asks you to show the room, the desk and your note-taking materials.

</div>
<div class="step">

**The test.** Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking, about 90 minutes of tasks. Follow the on-screen instructions; a timer shows the time left.

</div>
<div class="step">

**Speaking.** You speak into the microphone. At a centre others may be speaking too; start when told and speak at a steady volume.

</div>
<div class="step">

**After.** Scores appear in your ETS account once Writing and Speaking are rated. ETS gives the current waiting time and how to send scores (lesson 6.3).

</div>
</div>

## 3. Home Edition: the room check

- A **private room** with the door closed and nobody coming in.
- A **clear desk**: no phones, papers, books or second screens.
- A computer that passed the **system check**, plugged in, with notifications and other programs closed.
- Good **lighting** on your face; the webcam must see you throughout.
- A **stable internet** connection; avoid busy times on shared Wi-Fi if you can.

<div class="myth">

**"At home I can glance at my notes or my phone."** The proctor watches the whole time and the session is recorded. Anything that looks like cheating can cancel your score. Treat the room exactly like a test centre.

</div>

## 4. Check yourself

<figure class="fig" data-fig="drill" data-set="testday" data-n="5"><figcaption>What would you do? Original situations.</figcaption></figure>

<div class="keypoints">

- Choose the test centre if you cannot control your room, computer or connection; choose Home Edition if you can.
- Check that your universities accept the option you choose.
- Bring the exact ID you booked with; at home, show it on the webcam.
- At home: private room, clear desk, passed system check, only allowed note-taking materials.
- Scores appear in your ETS account after rating; you send them from there.

</div>
