**Module 1 · about 13 minutes.** By the end you will be able to work out an overall band from section bands, read a university's requirement, and see how far your current level is from it.

<div class="bigidea">Each TOEFL section gets a band from 1 to 6. Your overall band is the average of the four, rounded to the nearest half band, and universities usually look at both the overall band and each section.</div>

## 1. Section bands and the overall band

You receive **four section bands** (Reading, Listening, Writing, Speaking) and **one overall band**, all on a scale from **1** to **6**.

<div class="steps">
<div class="step">

**Step 1: add the four section bands.** For example 5 + 4.5 + 4 + 5 = 18.5.

</div>
<div class="step">

**Step 2: divide by 4.** 18.5 ÷ 4 = 4.625.

</div>
<div class="step">

**Step 3: round to the nearest half band.** The half bands near 4.625 are 4.5 and 5.0. It is 0.125 from 4.5 and 0.375 from 5.0, so the overall band is **4.5**.

</div>
<div class="step">

**ETS's own example:** an average of 5.125 gives an overall score of **5**, because 5.125 is nearer to 5 than to 5.5.

</div>
</div>

<div class="meaning">
<dl>
<dt>Section band</dt><dd>Your level in one skill, from 1 to 6.</dd>
<dt>Overall band</dt><dd>The average of the four section bands, rounded to the nearest 0.5.</dd>
<dt>Physical meaning</dt><dd>Raising one section by a whole band raises the average by 0.25. So one weak section drags the overall down, and improving your weakest section is often the fastest way to gain a half band.</dd>
</dl>
</div>

## 2. Bands and CEFR

ETS designed the 1 to 6 scale to line up with the **CEFR** (Common European Framework of Reference), the six-level scale many universities and governments use. Roughly:

| TOEFL band | CEFR | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | C2 | Understands almost everything; expresses fine shades of meaning |
| 5 | C1 | Uses English flexibly for study and work, with few errors |
| 4 | B2 | Follows lectures and discussions on familiar subjects; writes clear, detailed texts |
| 3 | B1 | Handles everyday situations and simple study tasks |
| 2 | A2 | Understands and uses simple, familiar language |
| 1 | A1 | Very basic phrases |

This is a guide, not an official conversion. ETS publishes the exact score descriptors and comparison tables on its website.

## 3. The 0 to 120 comparison

Many universities still write their requirement on the old **0 to 120** scale (for example "TOEFL 90"). To help during the change, for **two years after January 2026** your score report also shows a comparable overall score on the 0 to 120 scale. If a university gives only an old-scale number, use that comparable score, or the comparison table on ets.org, and check the university's admissions page: many have already published the band they want.
