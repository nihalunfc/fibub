**Module 18 · about 20 minutes · read first: 13.4 and 13.6.** You'll be able to plan a private AI workshop for creative and language work.

## 1. Why this sector

Unpublished manuscripts, scripts, client translations and brand work are valuable and often confidential. Creators also use AI in long sessions that burn through cloud limits. A local setup keeps work private and has no cap.

## 2. Top uses

- **Writers:** brainstorming, outlines, line edits, consistency checks across a long manuscript (with document search).
- **Translators:** first drafts in common language pairs, glossary checks, terminology lists from past work.
- **Video makers and podcasters:** transcripts and subtitles (whisper.cpp), show notes, chapter titles.
- **Designers:** image ideas and mood boards with local image models (Lesson 13.6).

## 3. Care points

- **Originality:** check that output does not copy existing text; do your own creative work on it.
- **Credit and disclosure:** follow publishers', platforms' and clients' AI rules.
- **Licences:** check image models' licences for commercial use.
- **Quality:** small models translate common pairs well and rare pairs poorly; test on your own past work (Lesson 17.1).

<details class="maths" open>
<summary>Maths and principle</summary>

**Principle:** price your time, not the AI's.

**Equation:** effective rate = fee ÷ (drafting hours with AI + editing hours).

**Analogy:** a carpenter with a power saw still sands and finishes by hand.

**Try it:** a 4,000-word job at 400, 8 hours by hand, or 2 hours AI draft plus 3 hours editing? (50 an hour against 80 an hour, if quality holds.)
</details>

## 4. Worked scenario

*Made-up translator.* Ravi translates insurance documents from English to Malayalam and Tamil under client NDAs. A local model drafts; he edits every sentence and keeps a glossary file the model reads each time. His output rises from 2,500 to 4,000 words a day, and no client document leaves his laptop.
