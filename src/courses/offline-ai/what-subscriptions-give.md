## 1. Why this matters

Before you work out whether local AI saves money, you need the honest price of the alternative. Plans look simple (US$20 a month) but differ in limits, models, extras and price from country to country. Prices here were read on 19 November 2026; they change often, so check before you buy.

## 2. The plans, side by side

Pick your country:

<!--fig:plan-prices-->

Three families, three tiers each:

- **Entry** (ChatGPT Go, Google AI Plus): cheap, smaller or capped models, good for casual use.
- **Standard** (Claude Pro, ChatGPT Plus, Google AI Pro): about US$20, the full everyday product, including coding tools (Claude Code, Codex) and longer limits.
- **Heavy** (Claude Max, ChatGPT Pro, Google AI Ultra): US$100 to US$500 for 5 to 20 times the standard usage, early features and priority.

## 3. How the limits work

None of these plans is truly unlimited. Each meters use over a rolling window, often 5 hours, and most add a weekly cap. A long document, a big coding task or a "thinking" model uses up the window faster than a short chat, because what is really counted is compute, which follows tokens (Lesson 3.4). OpenAI publishes estimates: on Plus, between about 5 and 3,000 Codex messages per 5 hours depending on the model. When you hit the limit you wait, switch to a smaller model, or upgrade.

A local model has no window and no weekly cap: its only limit is how fast your hardware writes (Lesson 3.9).

## 4. Country notes

- **India:** Anthropic, OpenAI and Google publish rupee prices, which are shown including 18% GST. UPI works for some plans. With GST included, rupee prices land a little above the US list price.
- **Canada:** most plans are charged in US$, converted by your bank, with GST or HST added at checkout. Watch your card's foreign-exchange fee.
- **USA:** list prices are before state sales tax, which depends on where you live.
- **UK:** some plans have pound prices (ChatGPT Plus at £20); others are charged in US$ with 20% VAT added.

## 5. What you get beyond the model

Cloud plans bundle extras that take work to copy at home: web search and deep research, file and project storage, voice mode, image and video generation, coding agents that run in the cloud, phone apps and syncing. Module 17.8 maps each one to its local equivalent. Plans also differ in data settings: check whether your chats may be used to improve models, and switch it off if you prefer.

<summary>Maths and principle</summary>

**Principle:** compare plans per unit of what you actually use, not per month.

**Equation:** price per useful hour = monthly price ÷ hours of real use per month.

**Analogy:** a gym membership. Cheap if you go every day; expensive if you go twice.

**Try it:** a US$20 plan used 10 hours a month, and a US$100 plan used 80 hours? (US$2.00 an hour against US$1.25 an hour.)

## 6. Worked example

*Made-up usage, real list prices.* Ananya in Bengaluru writes reports and some code. She pays ₹1,999 a month for ChatGPT Plus and keeps hitting Codex limits in the evening. Her options: Claude Pro at ₹2,000 for a second allowance, ChatGPT Pro at ₹10,699, or a local coding model on her 16 GB laptop for the routine work so her Plus allowance lasts for the hard tasks. Lesson 16.6 shows why the last one is often the best value.
