**Practice · Class 11 Maths · Chapter 9.** Read the lesson, [Straight Lines](/learn/courses/ncert-class-11-maths/straight-lines/), first. Try each question on paper before you open the answer.

<div class="bigidea">Most line questions are solved in two moves: find the slope (from two points, or from -A/B), then write the equation with the point-slope form. For distances, bring the equation to Ax + By + C = 0 first.</div>

## How to set out answers

1. Draw a rough sketch with the given points and lines.
2. Write the formula you use, then substitute with brackets.
3. Simplify to a final equation `$Ax + By + C = 0$` or a clear number.
4. Check by putting a known point into your equation.

## 1-mark questions

1. Find the slope of the line through (2, 3) and (6, 11).
<details><summary>Answer</summary>`$m = (11 - 3)/(6 - 2) = 2$`.</details>

2. What is the slope of a line parallel to the x-axis?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>0.</details>

3. Write the equation of the y-axis.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>`$x = 0$`.</details>

4. Find the slope of a line perpendicular to a line of slope 4.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>`$-1/4$`.</details>

5. Write the intercept form of the line cutting the axes at (3, 0) and (0, 5).
<details><summary>Answer</summary>`$\dfrac x3 + \dfrac y5 = 1$`.</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

6. Find the equation of the line through (1, 2) with slope 3. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

`$y - 2 = 3(x - 1)$`, that is `$3x - y - 1 = 0$`.

</details>

7. Find the equation of the line through (−1, 4) and (3, 0). *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

`$m = (0 - 4)/(3 + 1) = -1$`. `$y - 0 = -1(x - 3)$`, so `$x + y - 3 = 0$`. Check (−1, 4): `$-1 + 4 - 3 = 0$` ✓.

</details>

8. Show that the points A(1, 2), B(3, 6) and C(5, 10) are collinear. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Slope of AB `$= (6-2)/(3-1) = 2$`; slope of BC `$= (10-6)/(5-3) = 2$`. Equal slopes with the common point B, so the points are collinear.

</details>

9. Find the acute angle between the lines of slopes 1 and `$\sqrt3$`. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

`$\tan\theta = \left|\dfrac{1 - \sqrt3}{1 + \sqrt3}\right| = \dfrac{\sqrt3 - 1}{\sqrt3 + 1} = 2 - \sqrt3 = \tan 15^\circ$`. The angle is `$15^\circ$`.

</details>

10. Find the distance of (3, −2) from the line `$4x + 3y - 10 = 0$`. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

`$|4(3) + 3(-2) - 10| / \sqrt{16 + 9} = |12 - 6 - 10|/5 = 4/5$`.

</details>

11. Find the slope and the intercepts of `$3x + 2y = 12$`. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

`$y = -\tfrac32 x + 6$`: slope `$-3/2$`, y-intercept 6. Put `$y = 0$`: `$x = 4$`. Intercepts 4 and 6; intercept form `$\tfrac x4 + \tfrac y6 = 1$`.

</details>

## 5-mark questions (model answers)

12. Find the equation of the line through (2, 3) perpendicular to the line `$x + 2y - 5 = 0$`, and the foot of the perpendicular from (2, 3) to that line. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Given line: slope `$-\tfrac12$`. A perpendicular has slope 2: `$y - 3 = 2(x - 2)$`, that is `$2x - y - 1 = 0$`.

Foot: solve `$x + 2y = 5$` and `$2x - y = 1$`. From the second, `$y = 2x - 1$`; then `$x + 4x - 2 = 5$`, so `$x = 7/5$` and `$y = 9/5$`. The foot is `$(7/5, 9/5)$`.

</details>

13. Find the distance between the parallel lines `$5x - 12y + 7 = 0$` and `$10x - 24y - 13 = 0$`, and the equation of the line midway between them. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Write the second as `$5x - 12y - 6.5 = 0$`. Then `$d = |7 - (-6.5)|/\sqrt{25 + 144} = 13.5/13 = 27/26$`.

The midway line has the average constant: `$5x - 12y + \tfrac{7 - 6.5}{2} = 0$`, that is `$5x - 12y + 0.25 = 0$`, or `$20x - 48y + 1 = 0$`.

</details>

## Case-based question

A village road is planned along the line `$3x + 4y = 24$` (x and y in km, origin at the school). A farmhouse F is at (1, 1).

14. (a) Where does the road cut the two axes? (b) What is the slope of the road? (c) How far is the farmhouse from the road? (d) A second road is to be built parallel to the first and passing through F. Write its equation.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) `$y = 0$`: `$x = 8$`; `$x = 0$`: `$y = 6$`. The road cuts the axes at (8, 0) and (0, 6).

(b) `$y = -\tfrac34 x + 6$`, so the slope is `$-3/4$`.

(c) `$|3 + 4 - 24|/5 = 17/5 = 3.4$` km.

(d) Parallel: `$3x + 4y = k$` with F: `$k = 3 + 4 = 7$`. So `$3x + 4y - 7 = 0$`.

</details>

## Assertion and reason

15. **Assertion (A):** The lines `$y = 2x + 1$` and `$y = -\tfrac12 x + 4$` are perpendicular. **Reason (R):** The product of their slopes is `$-1$`. Choose: (a) both true and R explains A; (b) both true, R does not explain A; (c) A true, R false; (d) A false, R true.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(a). `$2 \times (-\tfrac12) = -1$`.</details>

16. **Assertion (A):** The slope of the vertical line `$x = 3$` is not defined. **Reason (R):** For a vertical line the run `$x_2 - x_1$` is zero, so the quotient rise/run cannot be formed. Same options.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(a). Both are true and R is exactly why the slope is undefined.</details>

<div class="keypoints">

- Slope from two points, or `$-A/B$` from the general form.
- Parallel: equal slopes; perpendicular: product `$-1$`.
- Point-slope form builds almost any equation.
- Distance of a point: `$|Ax_0 + By_0 + C|/\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}$`; between parallels: `$|C_1 - C_2|/\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}$`.

</div>
