**Practice · Class 10 Science · Chapter 6.** Read the chapter lesson, [Control and Coordination](/learn/courses/ncert-class-10-science/control-and-coordination/), first. Try each question on paper before you open its answer.

<div class="bigidea">Most questions test pathways and "who does what": the order of a reflex arc, the job of each brain part, which plant hormone causes which effect, and which gland releases which hormone.</div>

## How to set out answers

1. For a pathway, write the steps with arrows, in order.
2. For "difference between" questions, use two columns.
3. For plant hormone answers, name the hormone, where it is made, and the effect.
4. For feedback, say what changes, what the hormone does, and how the change is reversed.

## 1-mark questions

1. Name the part of the neuron that carries the impulse away from the cell body.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The axon.</details>

2. What is the gap between two neurons called?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A synapse.</details>

3. Name the hormone whose deficiency causes diabetes.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Insulin.</details>

4. Which plant hormone promotes the ripening of fruits?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Ethylene.</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

5. What is a reflex action? Give two examples. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A sudden, automatic response to a stimulus, without thinking. Examples: pulling the hand away from a hot object; blinking when dust enters the eye.</details>

6. What is the difference between a voluntary action and a reflex action? *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A voluntary action is decided by the brain, is thought out and is slower, e.g. writing. A reflex action is automatic, happens without thinking, is quick, and is controlled mainly by the spinal cord, e.g. withdrawing the hand from a hot tava.</details>

7. Why is the use of iodised salt advised? *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The thyroid gland needs iodine to make thyroxine. Lack of iodine swells the gland (goitre). Iodised salt supplies the iodine needed.</details>

8. How does a shoot bend toward light? *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Auxin is made at the shoot tip. Light makes the auxin move to the shaded side, where it makes the cells grow longer than on the lighted side. The longer shaded side makes the shoot curve toward the light.</details>

## 5-mark questions

9. (a) Draw a neat labelled diagram of a neuron. (b) State the function of the dendrite, the axon and the myelin sheath. (c) How is a signal passed from one neuron to the next? *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Model answer</summary>(a) Cell body with nucleus in the middle, branching dendrites on one side, one long axon on the other with a myelin sheath and branching axon end. (b) Dendrite: picks up the signal. Axon: carries the impulse away from the cell body. Myelin sheath: insulates and speeds up the impulse. (c) At the synapse the electrical impulse makes the axon end release a chemical, which crosses the gap and starts a new impulse in the next neuron.</details>

10. (a) Name two endocrine glands and the hormone each releases. (b) Explain with an example how a feedback mechanism controls the hormone level. (c) What is the role of the hypothalamus? *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Model answer</summary>(a) Pancreas: insulin. Thyroid: thyroxine. (b) When blood sugar rises after a meal, the pancreas releases insulin; insulin makes cells and the liver take up sugar, so the sugar falls. The fall reduces the signal for insulin, so the level is kept steady: negative feedback. (c) The hypothalamus controls hunger, thirst and body temperature, and controls the pituitary gland.</details>

## Case-based question

Ravi touches a hot pan by mistake and pulls his hand away immediately. A second later he feels pain and shouts. In another test his friend drops a ruler and Ravi catches it at the 22 cm mark.

11. (a) Name the type of action when Ravi pulls his hand away. (b) Why did he feel the pain after the hand moved? (c) What is the effector? (d) Estimate his reaction time for the ruler catch using t = √(2d/g), g = 9.8 m/s².
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(a) A reflex action. (b) The reflex arc passes through the spinal cord, so the hand moves first; the signal reaches the brain slightly later, so he feels the pain afterwards. (c) The muscle in the arm. (d) t = √(2 × 0.22 / 9.8) = √0.0449 = 0.21 s, about 210 ms.</details>

## Assertion and reason

12. **A:** Plant shoots grow toward light. **R:** Auxin accumulates on the shaded side and makes the cells there grow longer.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.</details>

13. **A:** The cerebellum controls the voluntary actions that need thinking, like writing a poem. **R:** The cerebellum is the largest part of the brain.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Both A and R are false. The cerebrum, not the cerebellum, is the thinking part and also the largest. The cerebellum controls balance and the smoothness of movement.</details>
