**Module 4 · about 10 minutes.** By the end you will find volume and surface area quickly and know how they grow with scale.

<div class="bigidea">Volume counts unit cubes; surface area counts unit squares on the outside. Scale every length by k, and area grows by k², volume by k³.</div>

## 1. The formulas

| Solid | Volume | Surface area |
|---|---|---|
| Box l × w × h | lwh | 2(lw + lh + wh) |
| Cube of side s | s³ | 6s² |
| Cylinder, radius r, height h | πr²h | 2πr² + 2πrh |

Longest diagonal of a box: √(l² + w² + h²).

<div class="steps" data-title="Worked example: scaling">
<div class="step">A tank 2 m by 3 m by 4 m holds 24 m³. A second tank has every edge 1.5 times as long.</div>
<div class="step">Volume × 1.5³ = × 3.375: 24 × 3.375 = <b>81 m³</b>.</div>
<div class="step">Surface area × 1.5² = × 2.25: it needs 2.25 times as much paint.</div>
</div>

<div class="myth">

**"Double the size, double the material."** Doubling the edges quadruples the surface and multiplies the volume by eight.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Box: V = lwh, S = 2(lw + lh + wh). Cube: s³ and 6s². Cylinder: πr²h and 2πr² + 2πrh.
- Longest diagonal of a box: √(l² + w² + h²).
- Scale by k: lengths × k, areas × k², volumes × k³.

</div>
