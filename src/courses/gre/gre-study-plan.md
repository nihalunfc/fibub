**Module 1 · about 10 minutes.** By the end you will have a plan that fits your week and a habit that turns mistakes into points.

<div class="bigidea">A steady routine beats long weekends of cramming: learn one method, practise it at once, and review what you got wrong a few days later.</div>

## 1. Pick your plan

| Plan | Time a day | Weeks | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fast | 2 hours | 6 | Strong maths and reading, or an early deadline |
| Standard | 1.5 hours | 10 | Most people with a job or classes |
| Steady | 1 hour | 14 | Busy schedules, or rebuilding school maths |

## 2. The week-by-week map (standard plan)

| Week | Lessons | Also do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Module 1, Module 2 | Diagnostic below; start an error log |
| 2 | Module 3 (the guided routes through the shared maths lessons) | 10 quant questions a day |
| 3 | Module 3 continued | Mixed review |
| 4 | Module 4 (geometry) | Quantitative Comparison every day |
| 5 | Module 5 (data) | Quant review, lesson 5.4 |
| 6 | Module 6 (completions, vocabulary) | 10 new words a day, reviewed after 3 and 7 days |
| 7 | Module 7 (reading) | One passage a day from a quality magazine |
| 8 | Module 8 (the Issue essay) | Write one essay, timed |
| 9 | Lesson 9.1, practice test (9.2) | Fix the three weakest topics |
| 10 | Light review, lesson 9.3 | Second practice test early in the week; rest the day before |

For the 6-week plan, do about 1.7 weeks of the table each week. For 14 weeks, give Modules 3 to 5 two weeks each.

## 3. One session, done right

<div class="steps" data-title="A 90-minute session">
<div class="step"><b>10 minutes:</b> redo three questions from your error log without notes.</div>
<div class="step"><b>30 minutes:</b> one lesson: read it, play its figures, step through the worked example.</div>
<div class="step"><b>35 minutes:</b> its practice set. Check each answer and read the solution even when you are right.</div>
<div class="step"><b>15 minutes:</b> log every miss with its reason, and 10 words in sentences.</div>
</div>

<div class="myth">

**"I will just do practice tests until my score rises."** Tests measure; lessons and error logs improve. One test every two or three weeks is plenty.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Pick 6, 10 or 14 weeks and follow the map in order.
- Each session: review old misses, learn one method, practise it, log the misses.
- Learn words a few a day, in sentences, with spaced review.
- Take the diagnostic now and a full practice test every two or three weeks.

</div>

## Diagnostic: where are you now?

Ten mixed questions: Quantitative Comparison, formats and geometry. Note which ones you miss: that is where your plan should start.
