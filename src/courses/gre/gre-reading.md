This lesson shows how it fits the GRE.

<div class="bigidea">Read for the author's point and the job of each paragraph, not for facts. Then go back to the text for every answer.</div>

## 1. Three kinds of passage

- **Short:** one paragraph, one to three questions. Map it in a sentence.
- **Long:** several paragraphs, about four questions. One line per paragraph.
- **Argument:** a short argument with one question (assumption, strengthen, weaken). Lesson 7.3 covers these.

## 2. Answer from the text

<div class="steps" data-title="For every question">
<div class="step">Name the type: main idea, detail, inference, function, tone.</div>
<div class="step">Go back to the lines (your map tells you where).</div>
<div class="step">Predict in your own words.</div>
<div class="step">Eliminate: out of scope, distortion, too extreme, wrong part.</div>
</div>

<figure class="fig" data-fig="wrong-answers"><figcaption>Sort wrong answers into the four families from the shared lesson. The same families appear on the GRE.</figcaption></figure>

<div class="myth">

**"Reading faster gets more questions right."** Reading with a map and going back for each answer is faster overall than reading quickly and guessing from memory.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- One line per paragraph, plus the main point.
- Go back to the text for every question; predict before reading choices.
- Wrong answers: out of scope, distortion, extreme, wrong part.
- Never use outside knowledge.

</div>

**Try it:** Tap each paragraph to label its job, then press Check.
