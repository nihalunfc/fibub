**Module 1 · about 9 minutes.** By the end you will read a GRE score report and set a target that fits your programmes.

<div class="bigidea">Your GRE score is three numbers, not one. Programmes weigh them differently, so your target comes from the schools you want, not from the maximum.</div>

## 1. Three scores

| Score | Range | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Verbal Reasoning | 130 to 170 (1-point steps) | reading, sentence logic, word meaning |
| Quantitative Reasoning | 130 to 170 (1-point steps) | arithmetic, algebra, geometry, data |
| Analytical Writing | 0 to 6 (half-point steps) | a clear, supported argument in writing |

Each Verbal and Quant score comes with a **percentile**: the share of recent test takers who scored lower. Engineering and science programmes often look hardest at Quant; humanities programmes at Verbal and Writing.

## 2. How the adaptive section counts

Your score depends on how many questions you answer right **and** how hard your second section was. That is why a strong first section matters: it opens the harder second section, where the top scores are.

<figure class="fig" data-fig="adaptive"><figcaption>The same idea in the GMAT course: harder questions reached by doing well are worth more. On the GRE the switch happens once, between the two sections.</figcaption></figure>

There is no penalty for a wrong answer, so **never leave a question blank**.

## 3. Setting your target

<div class="steps" data-title="Set a target in three steps">
<div class="step">List 4 to 6 programmes. Look up the typical admitted scores most of them publish.</div>
<div class="step">Set your target 2 to 3 points above the middle of those numbers in each section.</div>
<div class="step">Take a first practice set (the diagnostic in lesson 1.3) to see how far you are from it.</div>
</div>

<div class="myth">

**"A 170 is the only safe score."** Admission looks at the whole application. Past a programme's typical range, extra points matter much less than your experience, statement and references.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Verbal and Quant: 130 to 170 each. Writing: 0 to 6.
- Section 1 decides how hard section 2 is; the harder section leads to higher scores.
- No penalty for wrong answers: always answer.
- Set your target from your programmes' published scores, a little above the middle.

</div>
