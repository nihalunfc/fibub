**Module 4 · about 14 minutes.** By the end you will solve right triangles in seconds by spotting their shape.

<div class="bigidea">Most GRE triangles are one of a few families: a Pythagorean triple, a 45-45-90 or a 30-60-90. Spot the family and the sides follow without square roots.</div>

## 1. Facts for every triangle

- Angles add to **180°**. Equal sides face equal angles (isosceles); all sides equal means all angles 60°.
- **Triangle inequality:** each side is less than the sum of the other two and more than their difference.
- **Area** = ½ × base × height, where the height is at right angles to the base.

## 2. Right triangles

**Pythagoras:** a² + b² = c², with c the longest side, across from the right angle.

<figure class="fig" data-fig="special-triangles"><figcaption>Pick a family and scale it. The ratio of the sides never changes; only the scale does.</figcaption></figure>

<div class="steps" data-title="Worked example: spot the family">
<div class="step">A right triangle has legs 9 and 12. Find the hypotenuse.</div>
<div class="step">9 = 3 × 3 and 12 = 4 × 3: a 3-4-5 triangle scaled by 3.</div>
<div class="step">Hypotenuse = 5 × 3 = <b>15</b>. Check: 81 + 144 = 225 = 15².</div>
</div>

Learn these triples: **3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25**, and their multiples.

<div class="myth">

**"The longest side is always the one at the bottom."** The hypotenuse is the side across from the right angle, wherever the figure puts it.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Angles add to 180°; equal sides face equal angles.
- Third side: between the difference and the sum of the other two.
- Area = ½ × base × height.
- a² + b² = c²; know 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25.
- 45-45-90: s, s, s√2. 30-60-90: s, s√3, 2s.

</div>
