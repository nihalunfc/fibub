**Module 5 · about 30 minutes.** Take the two sets below timed, then use the checklist.

<div class="bigidea">A review set is a mirror: it shows which ideas are solid and which still wobble. Score it, then spend as long on the misses as you spent on the set.</div>

## 1. The common mistakes checklist

<div class="steps" data-title="Before you choose, check">
<div class="step">Did I answer what was asked (the area, not the side; x + y, not x)?</div>
<div class="step">In a comparison with unknowns, did I try 0, 1, a negative and a fraction?</div>
<div class="step">Did I flip an inequality when multiplying by a negative?</div>
<div class="step">For a percent: did I divide by the starting value? For Numeric Entry: the right unit and rounding?</div>
</div>
