**Module 1 · about 11 minutes.** By the end you will know every part of the GRE, how long it takes and how it is scored, so nothing on test day is a surprise.

<div class="bigidea">The GRE tests three skills you can learn: reasoning with numbers, reading closely, and arguing in writing. It rewards method and practice far more than talent or a large vocabulary.</div>

## 1. The test at a glance

Since September 2023 the GRE General Test takes just under **2 hours**. It always starts with the essay.

| Section | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Analytical Writing: Analyze an Issue | 1 essay | 30 minutes |
| Verbal Reasoning, section 1 | 12 | 18 minutes |
| Verbal Reasoning, section 2 | 15 | 23 minutes |
| Quantitative Reasoning, section 1 | 12 | 21 minutes |
| Quantitative Reasoning, section 2 | 15 | 26 minutes |

After the essay, the Verbal and Quant sections can come in any order. Check the [ETS test structure page](https://www.ets.org/gre/test-takers/general-test/prepare/test-structure.html) before you book: formats change.

## 2. The question types

- **Quantitative Reasoning:** Quantitative Comparison (compare two quantities), multiple choice with one answer, select-all multiple answer, and Numeric Entry (type the number). Some come in data sets that share a table or chart.
- **Verbal Reasoning:** Reading Comprehension, Text Completion (fill one to three blanks) and Sentence Equivalence (choose two words that give the same meaning).
- **Analytical Writing:** one Issue task: take a position on a claim and support it.

## 3. Adaptive by section

<div class="steps" data-title="How the second section is chosen">
<div class="step">Everyone gets a first Verbal section and a first Quant section of medium difficulty.</div>
<div class="step">Do well, and your second section in that subject is harder. Do less well, and it is easier.</div>
<div class="step">The harder second section gives access to the higher scores, so every question in section 1 matters.</div>
<div class="step">Inside a section you can skip, mark, and go back to change answers. Unlike some tests, no answer is final until the section ends.</div>
</div>

<div class="myth">

**"The GRE is a vocabulary test."** Text Completion and Sentence Equivalence do use harder words, but they are mostly about reading the sentence's logic. Lesson 6.1 shows how the clue and the signal words point to the answer before you even look at the choices.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- About 2 hours: one 30-minute Issue essay, two Verbal sections (27 questions), two Quant sections (27 questions).
- Quant has four formats: Quantitative Comparison, one answer, select-all, Numeric Entry. A basic calculator is on screen.
- Verbal has Reading, Text Completion and Sentence Equivalence.
- The test adapts by section: section 1 decides how hard section 2 is.
- Inside a section you can move back and forth and change answers.

</div>
