**Module 6 · about 11 minutes.** By the end you will find the right pair quickly and avoid the synonym trap.

<div class="bigidea">Sentence Equivalence is a one-blank completion with a twist: two choices are right, and they must give the sentence the same meaning.</div>

## 1. The method

<div class="steps" data-title="Four steps">
<div class="step">Cover the choices; find the clue and signal; predict a word (as in lesson 6.1).</div>
<div class="step">Look for <b>every</b> choice that matches your prediction.</div>
<div class="step">If you find two, check that each one fits the sentence alone.</div>
<div class="step">If you find one, look for its closest partner among the rest.</div>
</div>

<figure class="fig" data-fig="se-pairs"><figcaption>Pick two words. One pair of synonyms is a trap: they agree with each other but not with the sentence.</figcaption></figure>

<div class="myth">

**"Find the two synonyms and you are done."** Often there are two synonym pairs among the six choices, and only one of them fits the sentence.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Predict first; then find the two choices that match.
- Both words must fit the sentence and give the same meaning.
- Watch for a second, wrong pair of synonyms.
- Both must be right to score.

</div>
