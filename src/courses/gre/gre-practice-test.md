**Module 9 · about 2 hours.** Take it in one sitting if you can, in the real order: the essay, then the four sections.

<div class="bigidea">A practice test is a measurement, not a lesson. Take it like the real thing, then learn from it in lesson 9.3.</div>

## Before you start

<div class="steps" data-title="Set up test-day conditions">
<div class="step">Find a quiet place, scrap paper and a basic calculator (the real Quant sections have an on-screen calculator).</div>
<div class="step">Close other tabs and silence your phone.</div>
<div class="step">Each section has its own timer below. Answers stay on this page and are never sent anywhere.</div>
</div>

These sections are **not adaptive** and use fibub's own questions. Each real GRE section mixes every question type; here the first section of each subject practises the GRE's own formats (Quantitative Comparison, numeric entry, select-all, geometry and data; Text Completion and Sentence Equivalence) and the second practises the shared topics (arithmetic, algebra, word problems and statistics; reading passages and arguments). Every paper is new, so you can take the test again.

## Analytical Writing: one Issue essay (30 minutes)

Set a 30-minute timer and write on this original prompt in a plain text editor with spelling checks turned off:

<div class="principle">

*"People who stay in one field for their whole career achieve more than people who change fields."*

Write a response in which you discuss the extent to which you agree or disagree with the claim. In developing and supporting your position, address the most compelling reasons someone might disagree.

</div>

Score it afterwards with the six-point checklist in [lesson 8.3](/learn/courses/gre/issue-writing/).
