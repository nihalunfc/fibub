**Practice · Class 8 Maths · Chapter 12.** Read the lesson, [Tales by Dots and Lines](/learn/courses/ncert-class-8-maths/tales-by-dots-and-lines/), first. Try each question on paper before you open the answer.

<div class="bigidea">Pick the graph by the story: categories (bar), parts of a whole (pie), grouped numbers (histogram), change over time (line). For the numbers: add and divide for the mean, sort and take the middle for the median, count for the mode.</div>

## How to set out answers

1. For a mean: write the sum first, then divide by how many values there are.
2. For a median: write the values in order first, then pick the middle (or the mean of the middle two).
3. For a pie chart: write the angle for each group, then check they add up to 360°.
4. For a graph: label both axes, give a title, and use equal class widths for a histogram.

## 1-mark questions

1. Name the graph used to show how a whole is divided into parts.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A pie chart.</details>

2. Find the mode of 3, 4, 4, 5, 4, 6.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>4 (it appears three times).</details>

3. What is the median of 7, 2, 9, 4, 5?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>In order: 2, 4, 5, 7, 9. The median is 5.</details>

4. Do the bars of a histogram touch or have gaps?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>They touch, because the class intervals are continuous.</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

5. Find the mean of 12, 15, 18, 20 and 25. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Sum = 90. Mean = 90 ÷ 5 = 18.</details>

6. Out of 72 students, 18 chose mango. Find the angle of the mango sector. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(18 ÷ 72) × 360° = 90°.</details>

7. Find the median of 12, 7, 9, 15, 20 and 11. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

In order: 7, 9, 11, 12, 15, 20. There are 6 values, so the median is the mean of the 3rd and 4th: (11 + 12) ÷ 2 = 11.5.

</details>

8. The mean of 8 numbers is 25. One number, 33, is removed. Find the mean of the rest. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Total = 8 × 25 = 200. After removing 33: 167. Mean = 167 ÷ 7 ≈ 23.9.

</details>

9. A survey of 60 people about their favourite drink gives tea 24, coffee 18, juice 12, milk 6. Find the angle for each sector. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

One person = 360° ÷ 60 = 6°. Tea 24 × 6 = 144°, coffee 108°, juice 72°, milk 36°. Check: 144 + 108 + 72 + 36 = 360°.

</details>

## 5-mark questions (model answers)

10. The marks of 9 students are 12, 15, 11, 18, 14, 15, 13, 16, 60. (a) Find the mean. (b) Find the median. (c) Which is a better description of a typical student, and why? *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) Sum = 12 + 15 + 11 + 18 + 14 + 15 + 13 + 16 + 60 = 174. Mean = 174 ÷ 9 ≈ 19.3. (b) In order: 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 15, 16, 18, 60. The 5th value is 15. (c) The median (15) is better: one very large mark (60) pulls the mean to 19.3, higher than eight of the nine students.

</details>

11. The heights (cm) of 20 students are: 132, 135, 138, 140, 141, 143, 145, 146, 148, 149, 150, 152, 153, 155, 156, 158, 160, 162, 165, 170. Make a frequency table with class intervals of width 10 starting at 130 and say which class has the most students. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

130–140: 132, 135, 138 → 3 (140 goes in the next class). 140–150: 140, 141, 143, 145, 146, 148, 149 → 7. 150–160: 150, 152, 153, 155, 156, 158 → 6. 160–170: 160, 162, 165 → 3. 170–180: 170 → 1. Total 3 + 7 + 6 + 3 + 1 = 20. The class 140–150 has the most students (7).

</details>

## Case-based question

12. A shopkeeper records how many ice creams he sold on five days: 24, 30, 27, 30, 39.

(a) Find the mean sale per day. *(1 mark)*
(b) Find the median and the mode. *(2 marks)*
(c) On the sixth day he sells 120 ice creams on a festival. Does the median or the mean change more? *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) 150 ÷ 5 = 30. (b) In order: 24, 27, 30, 30, 39. Median 30, mode 30. (c) New data: 24, 27, 30, 30, 39, 120. Mean = 270 ÷ 6 = 45 (it jumps by 15). Median = (30 + 30) ÷ 2 = 30 (no change). The mean changes more.

</details>

## Assertion and reason

For each, choose: (A) both true and R explains A; (B) both true but R does not explain A; (C) A true, R false; (D) A false, R true.

13. **A:** The angles of the sectors of a pie chart add up to 180°. **R:** A full turn at the centre of a circle is 360°.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(D). A is false: the angles add up to 360°. R is true.</details>

14. **A:** One very large value affects the mean more than the median. **R:** The median depends only on the middle value or values in order.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(A). Both are true and R explains A.</details>

<div class="keypoints">

- Pie angle = (count ÷ total) × 360°; the angles add to 360°.
- Mean = sum ÷ count; median = middle in order; mode = most common.
- Histogram bars touch; bar graph bars have gaps; a line graph shows change over time.
- Outliers pull the mean, not the median.
