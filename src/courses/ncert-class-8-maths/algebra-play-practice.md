**Practice · Class 8 Maths · Chapter 13.** Read the lesson, [Algebra Play](/learn/courses/ncert-class-8-maths/algebra-play/), first. Try each question on paper before you open the answer.

<div class="bigidea">Treat every equation as a balance: do the same to both sides. Collect the x terms, clear the number, divide, and always check by putting your answer back in.</div>

## How to set out answers

1. For a story: write “Let the … be x”.
2. Write the equation the story gives.
3. Solve it step by step, saying what you do to both sides.
4. Check in the original equation, and answer the question in words with the unit.

## 1-mark questions

1. Is 4x + 7 an expression or an equation?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>An expression: it has no equals sign.</details>

2. Find the value of 5n − 3 when n = 6.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>5 × 6 − 3 = 27.</details>

3. Solve x + 9 = 15.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>x = 6.</details>

4. How many matchsticks are needed for 7 triangles in a row (rule 2n + 1)?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>2 × 7 + 1 = 15.</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

5. Solve 3x − 7 = 14. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Add 7 to both sides: 3x = 21. Divide by 3: x = 7. Check: 21 − 7 = 14.</details>

6. Simplify 6y + 4 − 2y + 9. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>6y − 2y = 4y and 4 + 9 = 13, so 4y + 13.</details>

7. Solve 7x − 5 = 3x + 15. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Subtract 3x from both sides: 4x − 5 = 15. Add 5: 4x = 20. Divide by 4: x = 5. Check: 35 − 5 = 30 and 15 + 15 = 30.

</details>

8. Two numbers differ by 7 and add up to 45. Find them. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Let the smaller be x. Then x + (x + 7) = 45, so 2x = 38 and x = 19. The numbers are 19 and 26. Check: 19 + 26 = 45 and 26 − 19 = 7.

</details>

9. Think of a number, multiply by 3, add 12, divide by 3 and take away your number. Show that the answer is always 4. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Let the number be n. 3n; then 3n + 12; then (3n + 12) ÷ 3 = n + 4; then n + 4 − n = 4. The n cancels, so the answer is 4 for every number.

</details>

## 5-mark questions (model answers)

10. A taxi charges ₹ 30 plus ₹ 15 per km. (a) Write the fare for k km. (b) Find the fare for 8 km. (c) A passenger paid ₹ 135. How many km did she travel? *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) Fare = 30 + 15k. (b) 30 + 15 × 8 = 30 + 120 = ₹ 150. (c) 30 + 15k = 135, so 15k = 105 and k = 7 km. Check: 30 + 105 = 135.

</details>

11. A father is 4 times as old as his son. In 5 years the sum of their ages will be 55. Find their present ages. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Let the son be x years, so the father is 4x. In 5 years: (x + 5) + (4x + 5) = 55, so 5x + 10 = 55, 5x = 45, x = 9. The son is 9 and the father 36. Check: in 5 years 14 + 41 = 55.

</details>

## Case-based question

12. Ravi makes squares in a row with matchsticks. One square needs 4 sticks, two squares need 7, three squares need 10.

(a) Write the rule for n squares. *(1 mark)*
(b) How many sticks are needed for 12 squares? *(1 mark)*
(c) Ravi has 61 sticks. How many squares can he make? *(2 marks)*
(d) Can he make a row using exactly 50 sticks? *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) 3n + 1. (b) 3 × 12 + 1 = 37. (c) 3n + 1 = 61, so n = 20. (d) 3n + 1 = 50 gives n = 49/3, which is not a whole number, so no.

</details>

## Assertion and reason

For each, choose: (A) both true and R explains A; (B) both true but R does not explain A; (C) A true, R false; (D) A false, R true.

13. **A:** If 4x = 20 then x = 5. **R:** We may divide both sides of an equation by the same non-zero number.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(A). Both are true and R is the step used.</details>

14. **A:** The statement x + 1 = x + 2 is true for every x. **R:** The left side is always one less than the right side.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(D). A is false, because the two sides are never equal. R is true.</details>
