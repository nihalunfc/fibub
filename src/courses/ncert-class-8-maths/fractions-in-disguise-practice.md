**Practice · Class 8 Maths · Chapter 8.** Read the lesson, [Fractions in Disguise](/learn/courses/ncert-class-8-maths/fractions-in-disguise/), first. Try each question on paper before you open the answer.

<div class="bigidea">Underline the question word, then ask "percent of what?" For profit and loss the base is the cost price, for discount it is the marked price, for change it is the old value. Write the base before you calculate.</div>

## How to set out answers

1. Write the formula you use, for example Profit % = Profit ÷ CP × 100.
2. Substitute the numbers with their units (₹, years, %).
3. Work out step by step and write the answer in words ("the profit percent is 20%").
4. Check that the answer is sensible: a discount must make the price smaller.

## 1-mark questions

1. Write 35% as a fraction in simplest form.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>35/100 = 7/20.</details>

2. Find 10% of ₹450.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>₹45.</details>

3. On what amount is profit percent calculated?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>On the cost price.</details>

4. Write the formula for simple interest.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>SI = P × R × T ÷ 100.</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

5. A bag costs ₹500 and is sold at a loss of 12%. Find the selling price. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Loss = 12% of 500 = ₹60. SP = 500 − 60 = ₹440.</details>

6. A ₹800 jacket has a 25% discount. What do you pay? *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Discount = ₹200, so you pay 800 − 200 = ₹600.</details>

7. A price rises from ₹40 to ₹50. Find the percent increase. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Rise = 10; 10 ÷ 40 × 100 = 25%.</details>

8. A ₹500 item has 12% GST. What is the bill? *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>GST = 12% of 500 = ₹60. Bill = 500 + 60 = ₹560.</details>

9. Find the simple interest and the amount on ₹8,000 at 5% a year for 4 years. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>SI = 8000 × 5 × 4 ÷ 100 = ₹1,600. Amount = 8000 + 1600 = ₹9,600.</details>

## 5-mark questions

10. A shopkeeper marks a cooler at ₹6,000 and gives a 10% discount. GST of 18% is added to the discounted price. (a) Find the discount. (b) Find the price after discount. (c) Find the GST. (d) Find the final bill. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) 10% of 6000 = ₹600. (b) 6000 − 600 = ₹5,400. (c) 18% of 5400 = ₹972. (d) 5400 + 972 = ₹6,372.

</details>

11. Find the compound interest on ₹10,000 at 10% a year for 2 years, and the extra it earns over simple interest. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Year 1 amount = 10000 × 1.1 = ₹11,000. Year 2 amount = 11000 × 1.1 = ₹12,100. Compound interest = 12100 − 10000 = ₹2,100. Simple interest for 2 years = 10000 × 10 × 2 ÷ 100 = ₹2,000. The extra is ₹100, the interest earned in year 2 on the first year's interest (10% of ₹1,000).

</details>

## Case-based question

**The scooter.** Meera's family buys a scooter for ₹80,000 on 1 January. Its value falls by 10% every year because it ages.

(a) Find its value after 1 year. *(1)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>10% of 80,000 = 8,000. Value = ₹72,000.</details>

(b) Find its value after 2 years. *(1)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>10% of 72,000 = 7,200. Value = ₹64,800.</details>

(c) Is the value after 2 years 20% lower than the start? Explain. *(1)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>No. 20% lower would be ₹64,000. The second 10% is taken on a smaller amount (₹72,000), so the scooter is worth ₹64,800.</details>

(d) The scooter is sold after 2 years for ₹64,800 after being bought at ₹80,000. Find the loss percent. *(1)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Loss = ₹15,200; 15,200 ÷ 80,000 × 100 = 19%.</details>

## Assertion and reason

Options: (A) both true and R explains A; (B) both true but R does not explain A; (C) A true, R false; (D) A false, R true.

12. **A:** A 20% rise followed by a 20% fall leaves the price lower than the start. **R:** The 20% fall is taken on a bigger amount than the 20% rise.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(A). 100 → 120 → 96.</details>

13. **A:** Profit percent is calculated on the selling price. **R:** Profit percent = profit ÷ cost price × 100.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(D). A is false; R is true.</details>

14. **A:** Compound interest for 2 years is more than simple interest for 2 years at the same rate. **R:** In the second year, compound interest is also earned on the first year's interest.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(A). Both true and R explains A.</details>

<div class="keypoints">

- Always find the base first: CP, marked price or old value.
- Profit % = (SP − CP) ÷ CP × 100; discount is taken off the marked price; GST is added after.
- Percent change = change ÷ old × 100.
- SI = PRT ÷ 100; compound amount A = P(1 + R/100)ⁿ.
- Successive percent changes multiply: they do not add.

</div>
