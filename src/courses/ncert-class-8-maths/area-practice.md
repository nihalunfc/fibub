**Practice · Class 8 Maths · Chapter 14.** Read the lesson, [Area](/learn/courses/ncert-class-8-maths/area/), first. Try each question on paper before you open the answer.

<div class="bigidea">Pick the formula that matches the shape, name the base and the perpendicular height, and write the unit as square units. For combined shapes, split them into simple parts and add or subtract.</div>

## How to set out answers

1. Sketch the shape and mark every given length.
2. Write the formula (for example area = ½ × (a + b) × h).
3. Substitute step by step, using π = 22/7 when the radius is a multiple of 7.
4. Give the unit: cm², m², or ₹ for cost questions.

## 1-mark questions

1. Write the formula for the area of a triangle.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Area = ½ × base × height.</details>

2. Find the area of a parallelogram with base 12 cm and height 5 cm.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>12 × 5 = 60 cm².</details>

3. What is the formula for the area of a circle of radius r?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>π r².</details>

4. State Pick’s theorem in one line.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Area = I + B/2 − 1, where I is the number of dots inside and B the number on the boundary.</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

5. The parallel sides of a trapezium are 12 cm and 18 cm and the distance between them is 10 cm. Find its area. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>½ × (12 + 18) × 10 = 150 cm².</details>

6. Find the area of a circle of radius 21 m (use π = 22/7). *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

22/7 × 21 × 21 = 22 × 3 × 21 = 1386 m².

</details>

7. A parallelogram has area 72 cm² and one base 9 cm. Find the height on that base. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Height = area ÷ base = 72 ÷ 9 = 8 cm.</details>

8. A trapezium has area 60 cm² and parallel sides 7 cm and 13 cm. Find the distance between them. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

60 = ½ × (7 + 13) × h = 10h, so h = 6 cm.

</details>

9. The diagonals of a rhombus are 16 cm and 12 cm. Find its area and the length of one side. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Area = ½ × 16 × 12 = 96 cm². The half-diagonals are 8 and 6 and meet at a right angle, so the side = √(8² + 6²) = √100 = 10 cm.

</details>

## 5-mark questions (model answers)

10. A rectangular garden 24 m by 15 m has a path 3 m wide running inside it all round. (a) Find the area of the garden. (b) Find the area of the inner rectangle left for the lawn. (c) Find the area of the path. (d) Find the cost of paving it at ₹ 50 per m². *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) 24 × 15 = 360 m². (b) The inner rectangle is (24 − 6) × (15 − 6) = 18 × 9 = 162 m². (c) Path = 360 − 162 = 198 m². (d) Cost = 198 × 50 = ₹ 9,900.

</details>

11. A circular pond of radius 7 m is dug in the middle of a square lawn of side 28 m. Find the area of the grass left (use π = 22/7). *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Square: 28 × 28 = 784 m². Pond: 22/7 × 7 × 7 = 154 m². Grass = 784 − 154 = 630 m².

</details>

## Case-based question

12. Farmer Kavita has a field in the shape of a trapezium with parallel sides 60 m and 40 m, 30 m apart. She also has a circular well of radius 7 m at one corner (not part of the field).

(a) Find the area of the field. *(2 marks)*
(b) She wants to sow wheat at 5 kg per 100 m². How much seed is needed? *(2 marks)*
(c) Find the area of the circular well mouth (π = 22/7). *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) ½ × (60 + 40) × 30 = 1500 m². (b) 1500 ÷ 100 = 15 hundreds, 15 × 5 = 75 kg. (c) 22/7 × 7 × 7 = 154 m².

</details>

## Assertion and reason

For each, choose: (A) both true and R explains A; (B) both true but R does not explain A; (C) A true, R false; (D) A false, R true.

13. **A:** A rectangle and a parallelogram with the same base and height have the same area. **R:** A parallelogram can be cut and rearranged into such a rectangle.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(A). Both are true and R is the reason.</details>

14. **A:** Doubling the radius of a circle doubles its area. **R:** The area of a circle is π r².
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(D). A is false: the area becomes 4 times as big. R is true.</details>

<div class="keypoints">

- Parallelogram `$bh$`, triangle `$\tfrac{1}{2}bh$`, trapezium `$\tfrac{1}{2}(a+b)h$`, circle `$\pi r^2$`.
- For a path: outer rectangle minus inner rectangle (or minus garden).
- Combined shapes: split, find each part, add or subtract.
- Pick: `$I + B/2 - 1$`. Area is in square units.
