**Practice · Class 8 Maths · Chapter 7.** Read the lesson, [Proportional Reasoning – 1](/learn/courses/ncert-class-8-maths/proportional-reasoning-1/), first. Try each question on paper before you open the answer.

<div class="bigidea">First put everything in the same unit. Then decide whether the job is "equal ratios" (cross multiply), "sharing" (parts) or "one then many" (unitary method). Check your answer for sense: more items should cost more.</div>

## How to set out answers

1. Write the given numbers with units.
2. Change to the same unit if needed.
3. Show the method in a line or two: one part = total ÷ parts, or cost of one = total cost ÷ number.
4. Write the answer with its unit and check it makes sense.

## 1-mark questions

1. Write 15 : 25 in simplest form.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>3 : 5 (divide both by 5).</details>

2. Are 2, 3, 8, 12 in proportion?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>2 × 12 = 24 and 3 × 8 = 24. Yes.</details>

3. Express 2 hours to 40 minutes as a ratio.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>120 : 40 = 3 : 1.</details>

4. A map scale is 1 : 100 000. What real distance in km does 7 cm stand for?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>7 × 100 000 cm = 700 000 cm = 7 km.</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

5. Share ₹750 between Dev and Esha in the ratio 2 : 3. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>5 parts, one part ₹150. Dev gets ₹300 and Esha ₹450.</details>

6. If 12 mangoes cost ₹150, find the cost of 20 mangoes. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>One mango costs ₹12.50. 20 mangoes cost ₹250.</details>

7. Find x if 6 : 9 = 14 : x. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>6x = 9 × 14 = 126, so x = 21.</details>

8. A bus covers 270 km in 5 hours at a steady speed. How far will it go in 8 hours? *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Speed = 270 ÷ 5 = 54 km per hour. In 8 hours: 54 × 8 = 432 km.</details>

9. Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7 and differ by 18. Find them. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The difference is 7 − 5 = 2 parts = 18, so one part is 9. The numbers are 45 and 63.</details>

## 5-mark questions

10. Anil, Bela and Chetan share ₹4,500 in the ratio 3 : 4 : 2. Find each share and check that the three add up. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Parts: 3 + 4 + 2 = 9. One part = 4500 ÷ 9 = ₹500. Anil: 3 × 500 = ₹1,500. Bela: 4 × 500 = ₹2,000. Chetan: 2 × 500 = ₹1,000. Check: 1500 + 2000 + 1000 = 4500. ✓

</details>

11. (a) A recipe for 4 people uses 300 g of rice. How much for 10 people? (b) On a map, two towns are 6.5 cm apart and the scale is 1 : 400 000. Find the real distance in km. (c) Is "cost of n kg of rice at ₹60 per kg" in direct proportion with n? Explain with a graph feature. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) 300 ÷ 4 = 75 g per person; 75 × 10 = 750 g.

(b) 6.5 × 400 000 = 2 600 000 cm = 26 km.

(c) Yes: cost = 60n, so cost ÷ n = 60 always, and the graph is a straight line through the origin.

</details>

## Case-based question

**The tea stall.** A tea stall makes masala chai with milk, water and tea leaves. For 4 cups it uses 200 ml of milk, 100 ml of water and 6 g of tea leaves.

(a) Write the ratio milk : water in simplest form. *(1)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>200 : 100 = 2 : 1.</details>

(b) How much milk is needed for 10 cups? *(1)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>50 ml per cup, so 500 ml.</details>

(c) How many grams of tea leaves are needed for 18 cups? *(1)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>1.5 g per cup, so 27 g.</details>

(d) The stall has 900 ml of milk. How many cups can it make if milk is the only limit? *(1)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>900 ÷ 50 = 18 cups.</details>

## Assertion and reason

Options: (A) both true and R explains A; (B) both true but R does not explain A; (C) A true, R false; (D) A false, R true.

12. **A:** 15 : 25 and 3 : 5 are equal ratios. **R:** Dividing both terms of 15 : 25 by 5 gives 3 : 5.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(A). Both true and R gives the reason.</details>

13. **A:** The ratio 1 m : 1 cm equals 1 : 1. **R:** Quantities must be in the same unit before they are compared as a ratio.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(D). A is false: 100 cm : 1 cm = 100 : 1. R is true.</details>

14. **A:** If the cost is directly proportional to the number of items, cost ÷ items is the same every time. **R:** Doubling the items doubles the cost.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(A). Both true; doubling cost with items is exactly what keeps the quotient constant.</details>
