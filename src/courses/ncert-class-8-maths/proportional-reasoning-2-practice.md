**Practice · Class 8 Maths · Chapter 10.** Read the lesson, [Proportional Reasoning – 2](/learn/courses/ncert-class-8-maths/proportional-reasoning-2/), first. Try each question on paper before you open the answer.

<div class="bigidea">First decide: does doubling one double the other (direct) or halve the other (inverse)? For inverse questions, find the fixed product (the job, the distance, the stock) and divide it by the new number.</div>

## How to set out answers

1. Write what stays the same, such as “job = workers × days” or “distance = speed × time”.
2. Work out its value from the first situation.
3. Divide by the new number to get the answer.
4. Check: more workers or more speed should give less time.

## 1-mark questions

1. If x and y are in inverse proportion, which of x × y or x ÷ y stays the same?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>x × y (the product).</details>

2. A vehicle’s speed is doubled for the same distance. What happens to the time?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>It becomes half.</details>

3. 5 pens cost ₹ 60. Is the cost of 8 pens in direct or inverse proportion to the number of pens?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Direct proportion: more pens, more cost.</details>

4. What is the shape of the graph of x × y = 12?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A smooth curve that falls and does not touch either axis.</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

5. 8 men build a wall in 15 days. How many days will 10 men take? *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Job = 8 × 15 = 120 man-days. Days = 120 ÷ 10 = 12 days.</details>

6. A train at 60 km/h takes 5 hours. How long at 75 km/h? *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Distance = 60 × 5 = 300 km. Time = 300 ÷ 75 = 4 hours.</details>

7. Ravi mows a lawn in 12 hours and Sita in 4 hours. How long will they take together? *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Ravi does 1/12 an hour, Sita 1/4. Together 1/12 + 3/12 = 4/12 = 1/3 per hour. Time = 3 hours.

</details>

8. Food in a camp lasts 45 people for 24 days. If 15 more people join, how many days will it last? *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Food = 45 × 24 = 1080 person-days. People now = 60. Days = 1080 ÷ 60 = 18.

</details>

9. In a table, x = 2, 4, 6, 8 and y = 24, 12, 8, 6. Show that they are in inverse proportion and write the rule. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

The products are 2 × 24 = 48, 4 × 12 = 48, 6 × 8 = 48, 8 × 6 = 48. The product is constant, so the rule is x × y = 48, that is y = 48 ÷ x.

</details>

## 5-mark questions (model answers)

10. A tap fills a tank in 6 hours. A drain empties the full tank in 12 hours. (a) How much of the tank does the tap fill in 1 hour? (b) How much does the drain empty in 1 hour? (c) If both are open together, in how many hours is the empty tank full? *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) 1/6 of the tank. (b) 1/12 of the tank. (c) Net per hour = 1/6 − 1/12 = 2/12 − 1/12 = 1/12 of the tank. So the tank is full in 12 hours.

</details>

11. Meera cycles to school at 12 km/h and reaches in 40 minutes. At what speed must she cycle to reach in 30 minutes? Show your working. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Time = 40 minutes = 2/3 hour. Distance = 12 × 2/3 = 8 km. New time = 30 minutes = 1/2 hour. Speed = 8 ÷ 1/2 = 16 km/h. Check: faster speed, shorter time.

</details>

## Case-based question

12. A school canteen has a stock of 600 kg of rice. It is enough for 120 students for 25 days.

(a) How many student-days is the stock? *(1 mark)*
(b) For how many days will the rice last if there are 150 students? *(2 marks)*
(c) The canteen wants the stock to last 20 days. How many students can it feed? *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) 120 × 25 = 3000 student-days. (b) 3000 ÷ 150 = 20 days. (c) 3000 ÷ 20 = 150 students.

</details>

## Assertion and reason

For each, choose: (A) both true and R explains A; (B) both true but R does not explain A; (C) A true, R false; (D) A false, R true.

13. **A:** If 4 painters take 6 days, then 8 painters take 3 days. **R:** Painters × days is constant for the same job.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(A). Both are true and R is the reason: 4 × 6 = 24 = 8 × 3.</details>

14. **A:** The graph of inverse proportion is a straight line. **R:** In inverse proportion the product of the two quantities is fixed.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(D). A is false (it is a curve). R is true.</details>

<div class="keypoints">

- Inverse proportion: the product is fixed. Direct: the ratio is fixed.
- Workers, pipes and stock questions: find the fixed total, then divide.
- Speed and time: for the same distance, speed × time is fixed. Convert minutes to hours.
- Work together: add the daily parts; a drain subtracts.

</div>
