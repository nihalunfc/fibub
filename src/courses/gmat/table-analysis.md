**Module 8 · about 13 minutes.** By the end you will judge three statements about a table in about two and a half minutes, using sorting instead of arithmetic.

<div class="bigidea">A table question is a search problem. Decide which column each statement is about, sort by it, and read the answer off the top, the bottom or the middle.</div>

## 1. What you get

A table with a dozen or more rows and several columns, which you can sort by any column on screen. Then three statements, each to be marked true or false (or "yes or no", "consistent or not"). You must get all three right to score the question.

## 2. Sort first

<figure class="fig" data-fig="table-sort"><figcaption>Tap a column heading to sort by it. Then try the three statements under the table: each one needs only one sort.</figcaption></figure>

<div class="steps" data-title="Worked example: three statements, three sorts">
<div class="step">"The store with the most staff has the highest sales." Sort by staff: top row is Carver (38 staff). Sort by sales: top is Dunmore. <b>False.</b></div>
<div class="step">"The median growth is above 4%." Sort by growth. With 7 rows the median is the 4th: 5%. <b>True.</b></div>
<div class="step">"Ashford's sales per staff member are higher than Belmont's." Two rows only: 960 ÷ 24 = 40 against 750 ÷ 25 = 30. <b>True.</b></div>
</div>

## 3. Statement types you will meet

| Statement says | Do this |
|---|---|
| highest, lowest, most, least | sort that column; read the top or bottom row |
| median | sort; with n rows the median is row (n + 1) ÷ 2, or the average of the two middle rows when n is even |
| more than half, at least a third | count rows past a line after sorting; turn "more than half of 9" into "at least 5" |
| per person, per unit, ratio | divide two columns for the rows named; estimate first |
| percent of the total | add the column once, then compare |

<div class="myth">

**"I must calculate everything exactly."** Most statements can be judged by estimate. 960 ÷ 24 is clearly 40; you don't need decimals unless two values are very close.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Find the column each statement is about, then sort by it.
- Median: sort, then take the middle row (or the average of the two middle rows).
- "More than half" of n means at least ⌊n/2⌋ + 1 rows.
- Ratios need two columns; estimate before dividing exactly.
- All three statements must be right to score.

</div>

## Practise: Table Analysis

Each table is new. Select every statement that is true.
