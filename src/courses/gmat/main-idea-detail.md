**Module 7 · about 13 minutes.** By the end you will answer the two most common Reading Comprehension question types reliably.

<div class="bigidea">A main-idea answer must fit the whole passage, no bigger and no smaller. A detail answer must point to words you can put your finger on.</div>

## 1. Main idea and primary purpose

Use your passage map. The right answer:

- covers **all** paragraphs, not just one (too narrow) and not the whole field (too broad);
- uses a **verb that matches** the author: describe, explain, argue, evaluate, compare, challenge;
- keeps the author's **strength**: "suggests" is not "proves".

<div class="steps" data-title="Worked example: the fertiliser passage">
<div class="step">Map: puzzle (farmers under-use fertiliser) → information explanation rejected → timing explanation supported by an experiment → policy point.</div>
<div class="step">Main idea in my words: farmers under-use fertiliser mainly because saving until planting time is hard.</div>
<div class="step">"Fertiliser is too expensive" → wrong: the passage is about timing, not price.</div>
<div class="step">"Governments should lower fertiliser prices" → wrong: the opposite of the author's view.</div>
<div class="step">"Farmers under-use fertiliser mainly because it is hard to save money until planting time" → matches.</div>
</div>

## 2. Detail questions

"According to the passage", "the passage states", "the author mentions X as…". Find the lines, reread them, and choose the answer that **says the same thing in different words**.

## 3. The four wrong-answer types

<figure class="fig" data-fig="wrong-answers"><figcaption>Sort sample wrong answers into the four families. Most wrong RC choices belong to one of them.</figcaption></figure>

1. **Out of scope:** true-sounding but never discussed.
2. **Distortion:** uses the passage's words but changes the meaning (reverses a cause, mixes up two groups).
3. **Extreme:** stronger than the passage (all, never, proves, the only).
4. **Wrong part:** a real detail from the passage that does not answer this question.

<div class="myth">

**"If a choice repeats words from the passage, it is probably right."** Wrong answers often borrow exact words and twist the meaning. Right answers usually paraphrase.

</div>
