**Module 9 · about 11 minutes.** By the end you will turn any practice test into a list of exactly what to study next.

<div class="bigidea">Your score tells you where you are. Your error log tells you how to move. Spend as long reviewing a test as taking it.</div>

## 1. Build an error log

For every question you got wrong **or guessed**, write one line: section, topic, what went wrong, and what you will do differently.

<figure class="fig" data-fig="error-log"><figcaption>Sort a sample set of misses into the four kinds of error. The chart shows which kind costs most, and the plan below it changes to match.</figcaption></figure>

## 2. Four kinds of error

| Kind | Sounds like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Content** | "I didn't know how to find a median of an even list." | Redo that lesson and its practice set. |
| **Method** | "I knew the maths but set it up the long way." | Study the worked solution; redo the question a day later. |
| **Careless** | "I solved for x but they asked for 2x." | Add a checking step: re-read the question before choosing. |
| **Timing** | "I ran out of time on the last four." | Pacing checkpoints (lesson 9.1); guess earlier. |

<div class="steps" data-title="Worked example: one test, one plan">
<div class="step">Quant: 6 misses. 3 content (probability, counting), 2 careless, 1 timing.</div>
<div class="step">Verbal: 5 misses, 4 of them assumption or flaw questions (method).</div>
<div class="step">Plan for the next 10 days: redo lessons 5.3 and 5.4 with their practice sets; redo lessons 6.2 and 6.4; add "re-read the question" before every choice.</div>
<div class="step">Retake the full test after 10 days, and compare the log.</div>
</div>

## 3. Spaced review

Redo the questions in your log after a day, then after a week. Getting them right a week later means you have really learned the idea. Spacing out practice and testing yourself are two of the best-supported study methods in learning research.

<div class="keypoints">

- Log every wrong and every guessed question.
- Name the kind of error: content, method, careless or timing.
- Fix the biggest kind first; each has its own fix.
- Redo logged questions after a day and after a week.
- Retake a full test after one to two weeks of fixes.

</div>
