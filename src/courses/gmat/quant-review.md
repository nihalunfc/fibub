**Module 5 · about 15 minutes.** By the end you will pick the fastest route for each question, the real skill behind a high Quant score.

<div class="bigidea">Most GMAT Quant questions can be solved three ways. Top scorers do not calculate faster; they choose the shortest route.</div>

## 1. The five routes

| Route | Use it when | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Algebra** | the setup is clear and short | 3(x − 2) = 2x + 5 |
| **Smart numbers** | variables or percents in the question and in the choices | "if x is 20% more than y…" → let y = 100 |
| **Work backwards** | numeric choices, one unknown, messy algebra | test the middle choice |
| **Estimate** | choices far apart | 0.48 × 61 ≈ 30 |
| **Spot the concept** | "must be true", divisibility, units digits | odd × odd is odd |

## 2. Smart numbers

Pick easy values for the unknowns, work out the answer with them, and see which choice gives the same result.

<div class="steps" data-title="Worked example: a price p rises 25% and then falls 20%. The new price is?">
<div class="step">Let p = 100.</div>
<div class="step">Up 25%: 125. Down 20%: 100.</div>
<div class="step">So the new price is <b>p</b>, unchanged. Any choice that does not give 100 when p = 100 is out.</div>
</div>

Avoid 0 and 1 (they make different expressions equal), and pick numbers that fit the question (multiples of every denominator, values that obey the given conditions).

## 3. Work backwards

Numeric choices are listed in order. Try the **middle** one first. If it gives a result that is too big, the answer is smaller; you have ruled out three choices with one test.

## 5. Two-minute discipline

If you have no plan after 30 seconds, or you are past 2.5 minutes with no end in sight, make your best guess (eliminate what you can), bookmark it, and move on. One stubborn question can cost you three easy ones at the end. Lesson 9.1 covers pacing in detail.

<div class="keypoints">

- Five routes: algebra, smart numbers, work backwards, estimate, spot the concept.
- Smart numbers: 100 for percents, multiples of all denominators; avoid 0 and 1.
- Work backwards from the middle numeric choice.
- Estimate when choices are far apart.
- No plan in 30 seconds or no end in 2.5 minutes: guess, bookmark, move on.

</div>
