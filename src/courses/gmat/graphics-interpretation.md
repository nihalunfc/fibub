**Module 8 · about 13 minutes.** By the end you will complete chart sentences quickly and avoid the percent and unit traps.

<div class="bigidea">Read the chart's frame before its data: title, axes, units and scale. Most wrong answers come from a misread frame, not from bad arithmetic.</div>

## 1. What you get

A chart (bar, line, scatter, pie or a mix) and one or two sentences with blanks. Each blank has a drop-down list of choices. You pick the choice that makes the sentence true.

## 2. Read the frame first

<figure class="fig" data-fig="chart-reader"><figcaption>Drag across the chart to read any month. Switch the question to see which bars it needs: a difference, a percent change or an average.</figcaption></figure>

<div class="steps" data-title="Worked example: percent change from a chart">
<div class="step">Sentence: "From January to June, units sold rose by closest to ___ percent." Choices: 25, 40, 60, 75, 150.</div>
<div class="step">Read the frame: units sold per month, axis from 0 to 200.</div>
<div class="step">January ≈ 80, June ≈ 140. Change ≈ 60. Percent change = 60 ÷ 80 = 0.75.</div>
<div class="step">Answer <b>75</b>. Trap: 60 ÷ 140 ≈ 43%, so 40 is there to catch people who divide by the end value.</div>
</div>

## 3. Common chart questions

| Asks for | Do this |
|---|---|
| change, rise, fall | later minus earlier; watch the sign |
| percent change | change ÷ starting value |
| average | add the readings, divide by how many |
| how many points above a line | count; a ruler-straight look across helps |
| trend or correlation (scatter) | up to the right: positive; down: negative; a cloud: weak |

<div class="keypoints">

- Title, axes, units, scale: always first.
- Percent change divides by the starting value.
- Read only as precisely as the choices need.
- Write the readings down before you calculate.

</div>
