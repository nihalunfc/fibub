**Module 9 · about 2 hours 20 minutes.** Take all three sections in one sitting if you can, with one short break, in any order you like.

<div class="bigidea">A practice test is a measurement, not a lesson. Take it like the real thing, then learn from it in lesson 9.3.</div>

## Before you start

<div class="steps" data-title="Set up test-day conditions">
<div class="step">Find a quiet place, a pen, scrap paper and no calculator (the Data Insights section on the real test has an on-screen calculator; use your phone's only for that section).</div>
<div class="step">Close other tabs and silence your phone.</div>
<div class="step">Decide your section order and your break (after the first or the second section).</div>
<div class="step">Each section has its own 45-minute timer below. Answers stay on this page and are never sent anywhere.</div>
</div>

These tests are **not adaptive** and use fibub's own questions, so the score is a guide to your strengths and pace, not a predicted GMAT score. Every paper is new, so you can take the test again later.

**Try it:** Press Play to run the clock and set your own seconds per question.
