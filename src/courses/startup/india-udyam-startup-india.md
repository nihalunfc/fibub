**Module 4 · about 15 minutes · India.** You'll know which of these three registrations you need, what each gives you and how to get it.

<div class="bigidea">Udyam is a free MSME registration that unlocks loans, schemes and the 45-day payment protection; DPIIT recognition opens startup tax and IP benefits; a Shops and Establishments registration covers your premises.</div>

## 1. Udyam (MSME) registration

**Udyam** registers you as a micro, small or medium enterprise (MSME). It is <mark>free, online and takes minutes</mark> on udyamregistration.gov.in with the Aadhaar of the proprietor, managing partner or director, and the business PAN. Investment and turnover figures are pulled from your income tax and GST records.

Why bother: priority-sector bank lending, collateral-free loans under the CGTMSE guarantee, protection against late payment (buyers must pay micro and small suppliers within 45 days, and big buyers can lose the tax deduction for late MSME payments), preference in government tenders, and many state subsidies. Proprietors often use it as proof of business for a current account.

## 2. DPIIT startup recognition

The **Startup India** portal lets eligible entities apply for recognition by DPIIT, free.

**Eligible:** a private limited company, registered partnership firm or LLP, up to **10 years** from incorporation, turnover **under ₹100 crore** in every year, working towards innovation, development or improvement of products, processes or services, or a scalable model with high potential for jobs or wealth creation, and not formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business.

**Benefits (as of November 2026):**

- **Tax holiday:** eligible recognised startups (companies and LLPs incorporated before 1 April 2030) can apply to the Inter-Ministerial Board for a **100 % deduction of profits for any 3 consecutive years out of their first 10**.
- **Angel tax** worries are gone for everyone: the tax on share premium above fair value (section 56(2)(viib)) was abolished for shares issued from the 2024-25 financial year onward.
- Self-certification under several labour and environmental laws, fast-track patent examination with an 80 % fee rebate, 50 % trademark fee rebate, easier public procurement (relaxed experience and turnover rules), and faster winding up.
- Access to the **Startup India Seed Fund Scheme** (through incubators) and the Fund of Funds for Startups.

> **Key idea:** Recognition is the gateway; the tax holiday is a separate application to the Inter-Ministerial Board after recognition, and not every recognised startup gets it.

## 3. Shops and Establishments registration

Almost every state requires a registration (or intimation) under its **Shops and Establishments Act** within a set time (often 30 days) of opening an office, shop, restaurant or other workplace, including a small office with staff. It sets working hours, holidays and leave for employees. Apply on your state's labour portal; some states link it through AGILE-PRO-S at incorporation. Many cities also need a separate **trade licence** from the municipal corporation for commercial premises.

## 4. Steps

<div class="su-steps">

After incorporation, the usual order

1. Udyam registration on udyamregistration.gov.in (same day).
2. Shops and Establishments registration on the state labour portal for each workplace.
3. Municipal trade licence if your premises or activity needs one.
4. Startup India: create a profile, apply for DPIIT recognition with a short pitch and proof of incorporation (decision usually in a few weeks).
5. If eligible and profitable soon, apply to the Inter-Ministerial Board for the tax holiday certificate.

</div>

## 5. Worked example

*Made-up founders.* Clinivo Health (from Lesson 4.2), one year old, ₹40 lakh turnover, ₹8 lakh in laptops and servers: **micro** enterprise. They register on Udyam the same day, file Shops and Establishments registration for their Pune office on the Maharashtra portal, and get DPIIT recognition five weeks after applying, describing their no-show prediction model. They will apply for the tax holiday in the first year they expect a profit.

<div class="myth">

**"DPIIT recognition gives every startup a 3-year tax holiday."** Recognition is the gateway. The 80-IAC tax holiday needs a separate application to the Inter-Ministerial Board, and only some recognised startups get it.

</div>

## 6. Practice

1. A company has ₹3 crore of machinery and ₹8 crore turnover. MSME class?
2. Can an OPC get DPIIT recognition?
3. Name two benefits of Udyam registration.

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Small: investment is above the ₹2.5 crore micro limit (turnover alone would be micro).
2. No, not as an OPC; it would need to convert to a private limited company.
3. Any two of: CGTMSE collateral-free loans, the 45-day payment protection, priority-sector lending, tender preferences, state subsidies.
</details>

<div class="keypoints">

- Udyam is free, online and uses Aadhaar and PAN; class depends on investment and turnover.
- MSEs must be paid within 45 days under the MSMED Act, and buyers lose the deduction under 43B(h) if they pay late.
- DPIIT recognition: company, LLP or registered firm, under 10 years, turnover under ₹100 crore, innovative or scalable.
- Benefits: self-certification for labour laws, cheaper IP filing, 80-IAC for some.
- Shops and Establishments registration is a state law; follow your state’s portal.

</div>
