**Module 6 · about 12 minutes.** You'll open the right account with the right papers and keep business money clean from day one.

## 1. Separate money, always

Open a business account <mark>before your first sale</mark>, even as a sole proprietor. All business income goes in; all business costs go out; you pay yourself by transfer. Benefits: bookkeeping takes minutes, tax deductions are easy to prove, lenders see clear history, and for a company or LLC you protect the liability shield.

> **Key idea:** Never pay personal bills from the company account. If you must, record it as a salary, a dividend or a loan to you, so your accountant can treat it correctly.

## 2. Documents

<!--fig:account-docs-->

<div class="cc" data-cc="in">

Companies usually open a **current account**, often through the SPICe+ AGILE-PRO-S link. Proprietors open a current account in the business name with two proofs of business. Interest-bearing savings accounts are not meant for business use. Keep the GSTIN, PAN and Udyam details updated with the bank.

</div>

<div class="cc" data-cc="ca">

Banks and credit unions offer **business chequing** accounts; corporations bring the articles and a directors' resolution, and the bank records anyone owning 25 % or more. Compare monthly fees against your transaction count; many offer a no-fee tier for small volumes.

</div>

<div class="cc" data-cc="us">

Bring the EIN letter, formation documents and operating agreement or bylaws. Under FinCEN's customer due diligence rule, banks identify owners of 25 % or more and one controlling person. Online banks are popular with startups; check deposits are FDIC-insured (and how much above $250,000).

</div>

## 3. Set it up well

- **Signing authority:** name who can approve payments; for teams, require two approvals above a limit.
- **Tax savings sub-account:** move sales tax collected and an estimate of income tax there each month.
- **Business card:** a separate card for business spending keeps receipts in one place.
- **Accounting link:** connect the account to your bookkeeping software (Lesson 6.3).

## 4. Worked example

*Made-up founders.* Clinivo Health Private Limited opens a current account through AGILE-PRO-S; the board resolution names both directors with joint signing above ₹2 lakh. They deposit ₹1 lakh share capital (needed for INC-20A, Lesson 4.2), set up a second account for GST and TDS money, and link both to their accounting software.

## 5. Practice

1. Name two benefits of a separate account for a sole proprietor.
2. What document names a company's bank signatories?
3. What do banks in all three countries record about owners?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Any two: simple bookkeeping, easy-to-prove deductions, clear income history for loans, fewer mistakes at tax time.
2. A board (directors') resolution.
3. The identity of beneficial owners (usually anyone with 25 % or more) and those who control the business.
</details>
