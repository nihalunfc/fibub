## 1. Before you start

<div class="su-check" data-name="India company registration document checklist">

Documents for a private limited company

- [ ] Name ideas checked on the MCA portal and trademark search (Lesson 4.1)
- [ ] For each director and shareholder: PAN, Aadhaar (or passport for foreign nationals), photo, address proof, email, mobile
- [ ] Class 3 digital signature certificate (DSC) for each director and subscriber
- [ ] Registered office proof: rent agreement or ownership document, a recent utility bill, and the owner's no-objection certificate
- [ ] Main business activity (NIC code) and authorised share capital decided
- [ ] Shareholding agreed (who owns how many shares)
- [ ] Bank you want for the current account (from the AGILE-PRO-S list)

</div>

## 2. Company: SPICe+ step by step

One integrated web form, **SPICe+** (INC-32), handles name reservation, incorporation and a bundle of registrations.

<div class="su-steps">

Registering a private limited company on the MCA portal

1. Create an MCA portal account (business user) and buy DSCs for all directors and subscribers.
2. **SPICe+ Part A:** propose one or two names with their meaning; reserved for 20 days if approved. (You can also fill Part A and Part B together.)
3. **SPICe+ Part B:** company details, capital, registered office, directors (DINs are allotted here for up to three new directors), subscribers and their shares, and the declarations.
4. **e-MoA (INC-33) and e-AoA (INC-34):** the memorandum (objects) and articles (internal rules), generated online.
5. **AGILE-PRO-S (INC-35):** links GSTIN (optional), EPFO, ESIC, professional tax in some states, and a bank account.
6. **INC-9** and director consents (DIR-2) are produced by the system; download, sign with DSCs and upload.
7. Pay the fee and stamp duty online, and submit.
8. The Registrar checks the forms; reply to any query within the time given.
9. Receive the **Certificate of Incorporation** with the **CIN**, **PAN** and **TAN**.

</div>

<!--fig:spice-plus-->

**Costs (as of November 2026).** The MCA filing fee is **nil** for companies with authorised capital up to ₹15 lakh. You still pay **stamp duty** on the MoA, AoA and incorporation, which depends on your state and capital, plus DSCs (roughly ₹1,000 to ₹2,500 each from certifying authorities). Many founders also pay a CA or CS for help; it is not compulsory.

> **Key idea:** Your certificate arrives with the company's PAN and TAN, so the company is ready to open a bank account and deduct tax at source from day one.

## 3. LLP: FiLLiP step by step

<div class="su-steps">

Registering an LLP

1. Get DSCs for the designated partners.
2. Reserve a name with **RUN-LLP** (or within FiLLiP).
3. File **FiLLiP** (Form for incorporation of LLP) with partners' details, registered office proof and contributions; DPINs are allotted to new designated partners.
4. Pay the fee (it depends on the total contribution) and receive the **Certificate of Incorporation** with the LLPIN.
5. Apply for the LLP's **PAN and TAN** (now issued together with incorporation).
6. Sign the **LLP agreement**, pay state stamp duty, and file it in **Form 3** within 30 days.

</div>

## 4. The first 180 days (company)

<div class="su-check" data-name="India new company first 180 days">

After incorporation

- [ ] Open the current account and deposit the share capital from each shareholder
- [ ] Issue share certificates within 60 days (and dematerialise shares if required for your company)
- [ ] First board meeting within 30 days; appoint the first auditor within 30 days (file ADT-1 within 15 days of appointment)
- [ ] File **INC-20A** (declaration for commencement of business) within 180 days, with proof the capital was paid
- [ ] Put the company name, CIN, registered office and contact details on letterheads, invoices and the website
- [ ] Register for GST if needed (Lesson 7.2), and Shops and Establishments for your office
- [ ] Apply for Udyam and Startup India recognition (Lesson 4.3)
- [ ] Get the company's significant beneficial owners declared (BEN-1 / BEN-2 if applicable)

</div>

## 5. Worked example

*Made-up founders.* Asha and Imran, both resident in Pune, form "Clinivo Health Private Limited" with ₹1 lakh authorised and paid-up capital (10,000 shares of ₹10; 60/40). They file SPICe+ with DSCs, opt for EPFO, ESIC and a bank account through AGILE-PRO-S, and skip GST for now. Fees: ₹0 MCA fee, Maharashtra stamp duty of a few thousand rupees, two DSCs. The certificate with CIN, PAN and TAN arrives in about a week. They deposit ₹1 lakh into the new account and file INC-20A in month two.

## 6. Practice

1. Which form carries the LLP agreement, and by when?
2. Name three registrations that AGILE-PRO-S can link.
3. What must happen before filing INC-20A?

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Form 3, within 30 days of incorporation.
2. Any three of: GSTIN, EPFO, ESIC, professional tax (some states), bank account.
3. Subscribers must pay for their shares into the company's bank account (proof is attached).
</details>
