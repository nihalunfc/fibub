**Module 5 · about 13 minutes.** You'll list every licence and permit your business needs before you open, and know where to check.

## 1. Four layers

1. **The business:** registration and tax numbers (Module 4).
2. **The activity:** regulated work needs its own licence: food, alcohol, salons, child care, transport, health, finance, security, real estate.
3. **The premises:** zoning, building occupancy, fire safety, signs, and the general business licence many cities issue.
4. **The product:** safety standards, labels and certification (electrical goods, toys, cosmetics, packaged food).

<!--fig:licence-finder-->

> **Key idea:** Licences are given by all three levels of government. Missing one usually means a fine or being closed until you fix it, and insurance may not pay out for an unlicensed activity.

## 2. Official finders

<div class="cc" data-cc="in">

India's **National Single Window System** (nsws.gov.in) and each state's single-window portal list approvals by state and sector. Food licences run through **FoSCoS** (FSSAI); shop registration through the state labour portal; trade licences through the municipal corporation.

</div>

<div class="cc" data-cc="ca">

**BizPaL** (bizpal-perle.ca) asks your location and business type and lists federal, provincial and municipal permits with links. Your city's business licensing page has the local detail.

</div>

<div class="cc" data-cc="us">

The **SBA** licences guide links each state's licensing office; your city or county website lists local licences and zoning. Professional and trade licences come from state boards (cosmetology, contractors, real estate).

</div>

## 3. Worked example

*Made-up founder.* Ayesha opens a candle shop in Hamilton, Ontario. BizPaL lists: Hamilton business licence (not needed for her retail category, she confirms with the city), a sign permit, building permit for the shop fit-out, and fire safety rules for storing wax and fragrance oils. No provincial licence applies. Her candles must meet Health Canada's Candles Regulations under the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act (safe burning, no lead wicks).

## 4. Practice

1. Use the finder for your own idea; list each licence and the office that issues it.
2. Which level of government handles zoning?
3. Name two regulated activities that need their own licence in all three countries.
