**Practice · Class 10 Maths · Chapter 12.** Read the chapter lesson, [Surface Areas and Volumes](/learn/courses/ncert-class-10-maths/surface-areas-and-volumes/), first. Try each question on paper before you open the answer.

<div class="bigidea">For a combined solid, ask two questions: which surfaces are on the outside (for surface area), and do I add or subtract the volumes (for volume)? Draw the parts separately before you calculate.</div>

## How to set out an answer

1. Draw the solid and its parts. Write r and h (and l for cones) beside each part.
2. State clearly which surfaces you count, for example "curved surface of the cone + curved surface of the hemisphere".
3. Substitute and calculate. Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$` or 3.14 as the question says.
4. Write the answer with its unit.

## 1-mark questions

1. Write the formula for the curved surface area of a cone with radius `$r$` and slant height `$l$`.
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

`$\pi r l$`.

</details>

2. Find the volume of a hemisphere of radius 3 cm in terms of `$\pi$`.
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

`$\tfrac23 \pi \times 27 = 18\pi$` cm³.

</details>

3. A capsule has total length 10 cm with hemispherical ends of radius 2 cm. What is the length of the cylindrical part?
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

`$10 - 2 \times 2 = 6$` cm.

</details>

4. Two solids are joined along a circular face. Is that circle part of the total surface area of the joined solid?
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

No. It is inside the solid, so it is not counted.

</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

5. Find the total surface area of a cuboid 8 cm by 5 cm by 3 cm. *(2 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

`$2(lb + bh + hl) = 2(40 + 15 + 24) = 158$` cm².

</details>

6. A solid is a cone of height 12 cm on a hemisphere, both of radius 5 cm. Find its total surface area in terms of `$\pi$`. *(3 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Slant height `$l = \sqrt{25 + 144} = 13$` cm. Cone side `$\pi r l = 65\pi$`. Hemisphere side `$2\pi r^2 = 50\pi$`. Total `$= 115\pi$` cm².

</details>

7. A wooden toy is a cone on a hemisphere, both of radius 4.2 cm. The cone is 4 cm tall. Find the volume of the toy. Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`. *(3 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Cone `$= \tfrac13 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 4.2^2 \times 4 = \tfrac13 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 17.64 \times 4 = 73.92$` cm³.

Hemisphere `$= \tfrac23 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 4.2^3 = \tfrac23 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 74.088 = 155.232$` cm³.

Total `$= 229.152$` cm³.

</details>

8. A hemisphere of radius 7 cm is placed on a cube of edge 14 cm so that the hemisphere's flat face lies on the cube's top. Find the total surface area. Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`. *(3 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Cube `$6 \times 196 = 1176$`. The hemisphere covers a circle `$\pi r^2 = 154$` of the top, and adds its curved surface `$2\pi r^2 = 308$`. Total `$= 1176 - 154 + 308 = 1330$` cm².

</details>

9. A cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm has a hemispherical hollow of radius 7 cm scooped from one end. Find the volume left. Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`. *(3 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Cylinder `$= \tfrac{22}{7} \times 49 \times 10 = 1540$` cm³. Hemisphere `$= \tfrac23 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 343 = 718.67$` cm³. Left `$= 1540 - 718.67 = 821.33$` cm³.

</details>

10. A capsule is 14 mm long overall and the radius of each hemispherical end is 3.5 mm. Find its surface area. Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`. *(3 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Cylinder part `$h = 14 - 7 = 7$` mm. Side `$2\pi r h = 2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 3.5 \times 7 = 154$`. Two hemispheres `$4\pi r^2 = 4 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 12.25 = 154$`. Total `$= 308$` mm².

</details>

## 5-mark questions (with model answers)

11. **A circus tent is a cylinder of radius 14 m and height 3 m with a conical roof of height 48 m on top.** Find the canvas needed, and its cost at ₹40 per m². Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`. *(5 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Cylinder wall: `$2\pi r h = 2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 14 \times 3 = 264$` m².

Cone slant height: `$l = \sqrt{14^2 + 48^2} = \sqrt{196 + 2304} = 50$` m. Roof: `$\pi r l = \tfrac{22}{7} \times 14 \times 50 = 2200$` m².

Canvas `$= 264 + 2200 = 2464$` m². Cost `$= 2464 \times 40 = ₹98{,}560$`.

</details>

12. **A toy is a cone of height 24 cm on a hemisphere, both of radius 7 cm. Find (a) its total surface area, (b) its volume.** Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`. *(5 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) `$l = \sqrt{49 + 576} = 25$`. Cone side `$= \tfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 25 = 550$` cm². Hemisphere side `$= 2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 49 = 308$` cm². Total `$= 858$` cm².

(b) Cone `$= \tfrac13 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 49 \times 24 = 1232$` cm³. Hemisphere `$= \tfrac23 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 343 = 718\tfrac23$` cm³. Total `$= 1950\tfrac23$` cm³.

</details>

13. **A pen stand is a cuboid 15 cm by 10 cm by 3.5 cm with four conical depressions each of radius 0.5 cm and depth 1.4 cm cut into the top. Find the volume of wood.** Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`. *(5 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Cuboid `$= 15 \times 10 \times 3.5 = 525$` cm³.

One depression (cone) `$= \tfrac13 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 0.25 \times 1.4 = 0.3667$` cm³ (that is `$\tfrac{11}{30}$`). Four of them `$= 4 \times \tfrac{11}{30} = 1.4667$` cm³.

Wood `$= 525 - 1.4667 = 523.53$` cm³ (about).

</details>

## Case-based question

**The gulab jamun.** A halwai makes gulab jamuns shaped like a cylinder with a hemisphere at each end. Each is 5 cm long overall and 2.8 cm across (diameter). Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`.

(a) Find the radius and the length of the cylindrical part.
(b) Find the volume of one gulab jamun.
(c) A box holds 45 of them. If each absorbs sugar syrup equal to 30% of its volume, find the syrup (in cm³) for the box.
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) Radius `$= 1.4$` cm. Cylinder part `$h = 5 - 2.8 = 2.2$` cm.

(b) Cylinder `$= \tfrac{22}{7} \times 1.96 \times 2.2 = 13.552$`. Sphere `$= \tfrac43 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 2.744 = 11.498$`. Volume `$\approx 25.05$` cm³.

(c) 45 gulab jamuns `$\approx 45 \times 25.05 = 1127.3$` cm³. Syrup `$= 30\% \times 1127.3 \approx 338.2$` cm³.

</details>

## Assertion and reason

Choose: (A) both true and R explains A; (B) both true but R does not explain A; (C) A true, R false; (D) A false, R true.

14. **Assertion:** The total surface area of a solid made of a cone on a hemisphere is the sum of the total surface areas of the two solids. **Reason:** The circular face where the two touch is not on the outside.
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(D). A is false because the joined circles must be left out. R is true.

</details>

15. **Assertion:** The volume of a capsule is the volume of a cylinder plus the volume of a sphere with the same radius. **Reason:** The two hemispherical ends together form a sphere.
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(A). The cylinder part adds `$\pi r^2 h$` and the two hemispheres add `$\tfrac43\pi r^3$`. R is the reason.

</details>

16. **Assertion:** A cone and a cylinder of the same base and height have volumes in the ratio 1 : 3. **Reason:** The volume of a cone is `$\tfrac13\pi r^2 h$`.
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(A). Cone `$\tfrac13\pi r^2 h$`, cylinder `$\pi r^2 h$`: the ratio is 1 : 3, and R gives the formula that shows it.

</details>

<div class="keypoints">

- Surface of a combined solid: add only the outside surfaces; joined faces vanish.
- Volume of a combined solid: add the parts, or subtract a hollow.
- Toy: `$\pi r l + 2\pi r^2$`. Capsule: `$2\pi r h + 4\pi r^2$`, overall length `$h + 2r$`. Tent: cylinder side + cone side.
- Cone `$l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2}$`; use `$l$` in the surface and `$h$` in the volume.

</div>
