**Practice · Class 10 Maths · Chapter 11.** Read the chapter lesson, [Areas Related to Circles](/learn/courses/ncert-class-10-maths/areas-related-to-circles/), first. Try each question on paper before you open the answer.

<div class="bigidea">Write the fraction θ/360 first. Then arc, area or perimeter are each one line. For segments subtract the triangle; for combined figures split into simple pieces.</div>

## How to set out an answer

1. Write the formula and what π you use.
2. Substitute the numbers and keep fractions until the end.
3. For a segment or a combined figure, write the area of each part on its own line, then add or subtract.
4. Give the final answer with units (cm, cm², m²).

## 1-mark questions

1. Write the formula for the area of a sector of angle `$\theta$` in a circle of radius `$r$`.
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

`$\dfrac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2$`.

</details>

2. Find the length of the arc of a quarter of a circle of radius 14 cm. Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`.
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

`$\tfrac14 \times 2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 14 = 22$` cm.

</details>

3. What fraction of a circle is a sector of angle `$72^\circ$`?
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

`$72/360 = 1/5$`.

</details>

4. The area of a circle is 154 cm². Find its radius. Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`.
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

`$\pi r^2 = 154$` gives `$r^2 = 49$`, so `$r = 7$` cm.

</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

5. Find the area of a sector of angle `$120^\circ$` in a circle of radius 21 cm. Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`. *(2 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

`$\tfrac{120}{360} \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 21^2 = \tfrac13 \times 1386 = 462$` cm².

</details>

6. The minute hand of a clock is 14 cm long. Find the area swept by it between 6:00 and 6:25. Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`. *(3 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

In 25 minutes the hand turns `$6 \times 25 = 150^\circ$`. Area `$= \tfrac{150}{360} \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 14^2 = \tfrac{5}{12} \times 616 = 256\tfrac23$` cm² (about 256.67 cm²).

</details>

7. A sector of a circle of radius 6 cm has an arc 6.28 cm long. Find its angle. Use `$\pi = 3.14$`. *(2 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Circumference `$= 2 \times 3.14 \times 6 = 37.68$` cm. Fraction `$= 6.28 / 37.68 = \tfrac16$`, so the angle is `$60^\circ$`.

</details>

8. Find the perimeter of a sector of angle `$60^\circ$` in a circle of radius 21 cm. Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`. *(3 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Arc `$= \tfrac{60}{360} \times 2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 21 = 22$` cm. Perimeter `$= 22 + 2 \times 21 = 64$` cm.

</details>

9. A chord of a circle of radius 10 cm subtends `$90^\circ$` at the centre. Find the area of the minor segment. Use `$\pi = 3.14$`. *(3 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Sector `$= \tfrac14 \times 3.14 \times 100 = 78.5$` cm². Triangle `$= \tfrac12 \times 10 \times 10 = 50$` cm². Segment `$= 78.5 - 50 = 28.5$` cm².

</details>

10. Find the area of the major segment in question 9. *(2 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Circle `$= 3.14 \times 100 = 314$` cm². Major segment `$= 314 - 28.5 = 285.5$` cm².

</details>

## 5-mark questions (with model answers)

11. **A chord of a circle of radius 12 cm subtends `$120^\circ$` at the centre. Find the area of the corresponding minor segment.** Use `$\pi = 3.14$` and `$\sqrt3 = 1.73$`. *(5 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Sector OAB `$= \tfrac{120}{360} \times 3.14 \times 144 = 150.72$` cm².

For triangle OAB, draw `$OM \perp AB$`. Then `$\angle AOM = 60^\circ$`, `$OM = 12 \cos 60^\circ = 6$` cm and `$AM = 12 \sin 60^\circ = 6\sqrt3$` cm, so `$AB = 12\sqrt3$`. Area `$= \tfrac12 \times 12\sqrt3 \times 6 = 36\sqrt3 = 36 \times 1.73 = 62.28$` cm².

Minor segment `$= 150.72 - 62.28 = 88.44$` cm².

</details>

12. **A track has two straight sides of 90 m and semicircular ends of radius 21 m. Find (a) the length of one lap along the inner edge, (b) the area enclosed.** Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`. *(5 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) The two semicircles make one circle: `$2\pi r = 2 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 21 = 132$` m. Lap `$= 2 \times 90 + 132 = 312$` m.

(b) Rectangle: `$90 \times 42 = 3780$` m². Circle (two semicircles): `$\tfrac{22}{7} \times 441 = 1386$` m². Area `$= 3780 + 1386 = 5166$` m².

</details>

13. **A circular park of radius 35 m has a 7 m wide path round it. Find the area of the path and the cost of paving it at ₹50 per m².** Use `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`. *(5 marks)*
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Outer radius `$= 35 + 7 = 42$` m. Area of path `$= \tfrac{22}{7}(42^2 - 35^2) = \tfrac{22}{7}(1764 - 1225) = \tfrac{22}{7} \times 539 = 1694$` m².

Cost `$= 1694 \times 50 = ₹84{,}700$`.

</details>

## Case-based question

**The wiper and the pizza.** A car's wiper blade runs from 7 cm to 21 cm from its pivot, and the arm sweeps `$120^\circ$`. Take `$\pi = \tfrac{22}{7}$`.

(a) What fraction of a full circle does the wiper sweep?
(b) Find the area of the sector of radius 21 cm and angle `$120^\circ$`.
(c) Find the area of the cleaned part of the windscreen.
(d) A pizza of radius 14 cm is cut into 8 equal slices. Find the area of one slice.
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(a) `$120/360 = \tfrac13$`.

(b) `$\tfrac13 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 441 = 462$` cm².

(c) The small sector of radius 7 cm has area `$\tfrac13 \times \tfrac{22}{7} \times 49 = 51\tfrac13$` cm². Cleaned area `$= 462 - 51\tfrac13 = 410\tfrac23$` cm².

(d) Pizza `$= \tfrac{22}{7} \times 196 = 616$` cm². One slice `$= 616/8 = 77$` cm².

</details>

## Assertion and reason

Choose: (A) both true and R explains A; (B) both true but R does not explain A; (C) A true, R false; (D) A false, R true.

14. **Assertion:** The area of a sector of angle `$90^\circ$` is one quarter of the area of its circle. **Reason:** A sector of angle `$\theta$` is the fraction `$\theta/360$` of the circle.
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(A). `$90/360 = \tfrac14$`, and R is the reason.

</details>

15. **Assertion:** The perimeter of a sector equals its arc length. **Reason:** The arc is the curved part of the boundary.
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(D). A is false: the perimeter also includes the two radii. R is true.

</details>

16. **Assertion:** The area of the minor segment for a central angle `$60^\circ$` and radius `$r$` is `$\dfrac{\pi r^2}{6} - \dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}r^2$`. **Reason:** The triangle formed by the two radii and the chord is equilateral.
<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

(A). The sector is `$\pi r^2/6$` and the equilateral triangle has side r, area `$\tfrac{\sqrt3}{4}r^2$`. R explains why the triangle's area is that.

</details>
