<div class="bigidea">The Milky Way is a barred spiral of a few hundred billion stars, about 100,000 light-years across; the Sun orbits its centre once every 220 million years, and that orbit weighs everything inside it.</div>

## 1. Why this matters

On a dark night ([Sky watching 3.2](/learn/courses/sky-watching/light-pollution/)) a pale band arches across the sky. Galileo's telescope showed in 1610 that it is made of countless faint stars. It took until the 20th century to learn that this band is our view, from the inside, of a vast flat galaxy, one of perhaps a trillion galaxies. Knowing its shape and our place in it is the first step outward.

## 2. The shape of our Galaxy

- **Disc**: a thin, flat layer about 100,000 light-years across but only about 1,000 thick, with spiral arms where new stars form from gas and dust.
- **Bar and bulge**: a bar-shaped, crowded centre of older stars.
- **Halo**: a faint round cloud of old stars and globular clusters, and a much heavier halo of invisible dark matter (in the [cosmology course](/learn/courses/cosmology/)).
- **Centre**: a black hole of about 4 million solar masses, called Sagittarius A*, weighed by watching stars orbit it (Nobel Prize 2020).

<!--fig:milky-way-map-->

Things to notice:

- Tilt to 90°: from inside the disc, the stars blend into a band, thickest towards the centre in Sagittarius.
- The Sun is about a third of the way out from the centre, between two arms.

<details class="maths" open>
<summary>Maths and principle</summary>

**Orbital period** of a circular orbit of radius `$r$` at speed `$v$`:

```math
P = \frac{2\pi r}{v}
```

**Mass inside an orbit** (Lesson 2.2): `$M = v^2 r / G$`.

**Try it:** the Sun, `$r = 8.2$` kpc `$= 2.5\times10^{20}$` m, `$v = 230$` km/s: `$P = 2\pi\times2.5\times10^{20}/2.3\times10^5 = 6.9\times10^{15}$` s = 220 million years.
</details>

## 3. What it means physically

The disc is flat for the same reason a spinning pizza dough flattens: the gas that formed it was turning, and collisions smoothed out up-and-down motion while the spin stayed. The spiral arms are not solid things; they are traffic jams where stars and gas crowd together for a while as they orbit, and the crowding squeezes gas into new stars. That is why arms glow with young blue stars and pink hydrogen clouds.

<div class="myth">

**"The Milky Way is the whole universe."** It was thought so until 1925. It is one of perhaps two trillion galaxies; Andromeda, the nearest big one, is 2.5 million light-years away.

## 4. Worked example

*How much mass lies inside the Sun's orbit?*

```math
M = \frac{v^2 r}{G} = \frac{(2.3\times10^{5})^2\times2.5\times10^{20}}{6.674\times10^{-11}} = 2.0\times10^{41}\ \text{kg} \approx 1.0\times10^{11}\ M_\odot
```

About 100 billion Suns' worth inside our orbit. The Sun has been round about 20 times since it formed 4.6 billion years ago.

2. A star orbits at twice the Sun's distance from the centre at the same speed. How long does one lap take?

2. Twice as long: about 440 million years.
3. We would have to travel tens of thousands of light-years out of it; all pictures "from outside" are drawings or other galaxies.
