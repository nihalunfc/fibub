**Module 11 · about 12 minutes.** Rules and fees change and differ between centres; this lesson explains the options, and the sources link to the current official pages. Checked 2026-11-22.

<div class="bigidea">Your result is the start of a decision: is it enough? If not, decide between a re-mark, a single-skill retake or a full retake, based on how far short you are and what your organisation accepts.</div>

## 1. Getting your results

- **Computer-delivered:** usually within a few days.
- **Paper-based:** usually around two weeks.
- You see them online first, and receive a Test Report Form (TRF). Many organisations receive results electronically; your centre explains how to send them.
- Most organisations accept results for **two years**, but each sets its own rule.

## 2. Re-mark (Enquiry on Results)

If one skill is much lower than you expected (for example Writing 5.5 when your other skills are 7), you can apply, within a deadline set by IELTS, for that skill or several skills to be re-marked by a senior examiner. There is a fee, which is refunded if your band changes. Bands can go up, stay the same or, rarely, go down.

## 3. One Skill Retake

In many places you can retake **one** skill (Listening, Reading, Writing or Speaking) within a set period after your full test, instead of the whole test. You get a new result combining your new score with your other three. Before using it, check that your university, employer or visa authority **accepts** One Skill Retake results; not all do. Check IRCC's current rules if you are applying for Canadian immigration.

## 4. Decide what to do

| Situation | Usually sensible |
|---|---|
| One skill surprisingly low, others high | consider a re-mark |
| Short by 0.5 in one skill, organisation accepts single-skill retake | One Skill Retake after targeted practice |
| Short in two or more skills | study plan of several weeks, then a full retake |
| Overall OK, one skill below a minimum | targeted practice on that skill; retake the option your organisation accepts |

- Computer results arrive in days, paper results in about two weeks; check your centre.
- Most organisations accept results for two years; check yours.
- A re-mark costs a fee, refunded if your band changes.
- One Skill Retake replaces one skill, but not every organisation accepts it.
- Choose based on how far short you are and what your organisation accepts.
