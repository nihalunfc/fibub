**Module 7 · about 15 minutes.** By the end you will make an essay easy to follow through organisation, reference and well-chosen linking words, not a linking word at the start of every sentence.

<div class="bigidea">Coherence is a logical order of ideas; cohesion is the glue between sentences. The strongest glue is often invisible: clear topic sentences, reference words and repeated key ideas, with linking words only where they add meaning.</div>

## 1. Coherence: order

- One main idea per paragraph, announced by the topic sentence.
- Paragraphs in a logical order (the weaker view, then the stronger; problems, then matching solutions).
- Inside a paragraph: point → explanation → example → link.

## 2. Cohesion: four kinds of glue

| Glue | Example |
|---|---|
| Linking words | however, therefore, for example, as a result |
| Reference words | it, they, this, these, such measures, the former, the latter |
| Synonyms and word families | cars → private vehicles → traffic; pollute → pollution → polluted |
| Repeated key idea | "This problem..." "Such a change..." |

Example without and with reference:

> *Many cities have built bike lanes. Bike lanes make cycling safer. Because bike lanes make cycling safer, more people cycle.*
>
> *Many cities have built bike lanes. **These** make cycling safer, and **as a result**, more people choose **this form of transport**.*

## 3. Linking words: meaning and punctuation

| Meaning | Between sentences | Inside a sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Adding | Moreover, In addition, Furthermore, | and, as well as |
| Contrast | However, Nevertheless, On the other hand, | but, although, while, whereas |
| Cause | | because, since, as, due to + noun |
| Result | Therefore, As a result, Consequently, | so, which means that |
| Example | For example, For instance, | such as, like |
| Concession | Admittedly, | although, even though, despite + noun/-ing |

**Punctuation:** sentence linkers (However, Therefore) take a full stop or semicolon before and a comma after. Joining words (but, although, because) join clauses inside one sentence.

## 4. How many?

A rough guide: one or two linking words per paragraph beyond "and" and "but", placed where the relationship is not obvious. If you can remove the linker and the meaning is still clear, consider removing it.

<div class="myth">

**"Firstly, Secondly, Moreover, Furthermore in every paragraph will raise my score."** Over-linking is a well-known feature of lower-band writing. Natural writing uses fewer, well-placed linkers and more reference words.

</div>

## 5. Practise

<div class="keypoints">

- Coherence: one idea per paragraph, logical order, clear topic sentences.
- Cohesion: linking words, reference words, synonyms, repeated key ideas.
- Sentence linkers (However,) start a sentence; joining words (although) join clauses.
- Use linkers only where they add meaning; one or two per paragraph is often enough.
- Reference words (this, these, such) avoid repetition and connect ideas.

</div>
