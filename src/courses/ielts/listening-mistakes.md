**Module 2 · about 12 minutes.** Read first: [Accents](/learn/courses/ielts/accents/). This lesson pulls Module 2 together: ten mistakes that cost marks, each with its fix, and a checklist to use in every practice test.

<div class="bigidea">Most lost Listening marks are not about hearing; they are about writing. Fix the writing slips and your band rises without any extra listening.</div>

## 1. The ten mistakes

| # | Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Going over the word limit ("the old bridge" for TWO WORDS) | Circle the limit; leave out articles the gap already has |
| 2 | Missing a plural "s" | Check the grammar: "several ___", "all the ___", what you heard |
| 3 | Misspelling common words (accommodation, Wednesday, necessary) | Keep a spelling list from your mistake log; practise in Lesson 2.3 |
| 4 | Writing the first answer heard, which is then corrected | Wait for the final answer; listen for "actually", "sorry", "no, wait" |
| 5 | Losing your place and missing several answers | Follow headings; if two pass, move on |
| 6 | Writing a synonym instead of the word heard in completion tasks | Use the word from the recording |
| 7 | Repeating a printed unit ("£35 pounds") | Write only what the gap needs |
| 8 | Leaving blanks | Always guess: there is no penalty |
| 9 | Writing two answers in one gap ("bus/coach") | Choose one |
| 10 | Copying answers to the wrong number on the answer sheet | On paper, check numbers every five answers; on computer, check before the 2 minutes end |

## 2. The order of a perfect Listening routine

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><p><b>Reading time:</b> circle the word limit, predict every gap, label the options, find the map's starting point.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>While listening:</b> write fast, look one question ahead, wait for corrections, move on when headings pass.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Between parts:</b> don't fix the last part; read the next part's questions.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Transfer or check time:</b> spelling, plurals, word limits, guesses in every blank, the right numbers.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="myth">

**"My Listening is bad because I don't understand the recording."** Check your mistake log first. Many learners find that half their wrong answers were heard correctly and written wrongly.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Obey word limits, add plurals, spell correctly: these alone save several marks.
- Wait for corrections; follow headings; move on after two missed signposts.
- Use the word you heard; don't repeat printed units; one answer per gap.
- Never leave a blank.
- Use transfer or check time for spelling, plurals, limits and question numbers.
