**Module 7 · about 15 minutes.** By the end you will know which structures show range in an essay, which errors cost most, and how to check in five minutes.

<div class="bigidea">Range is a mix of sentence types used naturally; accuracy is how many of your sentences are free of errors. Higher bands need both: varied, complex sentences, mostly error-free.</div>

## 1. Structures that show range

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| Relative clause | Students **who work part-time** often manage their time better. |
| Conditional | **If** fees **were** lower, more young people **would** go to university. |
| Passive | Strict limits **should be placed** on advertising to children. |
| Concession | **Although** online shopping is convenient, it has hurt local shops. |
| Noun phrase | **The rapid growth of online shopping** has changed town centres. |
| -ing / participle clause | **Living in a city**, young people have access to more jobs. |
| Modal of possibility | This **could lead to** higher prices. |
| Comparative structures | **The more** people drive, **the worse** the air becomes. |

Don't force all of them in. Use the ones that fit your ideas.

## 2. Errors that cost most

| Error | Wrong | Right |
|---|---|---|
| Comma splice | Prices rose, people bought less. | Prices rose, so people bought less. |
| Subject-verb agreement | The number of cars are rising. | The number of cars is rising. |
| Articles | Government should protect the nature. | The government should protect nature. |
| Plurals | many problem, one of the reason | many problems, one of the reasons |
| Uncountables | advices, informations, researches | advice, information, research |
| Word form | It is benefit for students. | It is beneficial to students. |
| Fragment | Because it is cheaper. | People choose it because it is cheaper. |

## 3. A five-minute check

Read your essay once for each:

1. **Verbs:** tense consistent, subject-verb agreement.
2. **Nouns:** plural -s, articles (a/an/the/none), uncountables.
3. **Sentences:** every sentence has a subject and a verb; no comma splices.
4. **Spelling** of your topic words.

<div class="myth">

**"Long sentences show advanced grammar."** A long sentence with errors scores worse than two correct ones. Range means variety, used accurately.

</div>

## 4. Practise

<div class="qbank" data-bank="ielts" data-topics="writing-essay-skills" data-mode="practice" data-n="12" data-sub="errors"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Range: relative clauses, conditionals, passives, concessions, noun phrases, used where they fit.
- Accuracy: the share of error-free sentences matters as much as complexity.
- Most costly errors: comma splices, agreement, articles, plurals, uncountables, word forms.
- Check verbs, nouns, sentence boundaries and spelling in the last five minutes.
- Two correct sentences beat one long wrong one.

</div>
