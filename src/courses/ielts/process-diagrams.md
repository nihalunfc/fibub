**Module 5 · about 16 minutes.** Grammar help: [The passive voice](/learn/courses/ielts/passive-voice/) in Module 9. By the end you will describe any process, natural or man-made, in clear order with the right grammar.

<div class="bigidea">A process has no numbers to compare, so the marks come from clear order and correct grammar: every stage, in sequence, mostly in the present passive, joined by sequence words.</div>

## 1. Natural or man-made?

| | Natural (life cycle of a frog, water cycle) | Man-made (making bricks, recycling glass) |
|---|---|---|
| Voice | mostly active: *the eggs hatch* | mostly passive: *the clay is dug up* |
| Verbs | hatch, grow, develop, turn into, evaporate | is collected, is heated, is transported, is mixed |
| Overview | number of stages; a cycle with no start or end | number of stages; raw material to final product |

## 2. Walk through one

Step through the stages. Each step gives the sentence a report could use.

<div class="steps" data-fig="process-steps">
<div class="step"><p><b>Stage 1.</b> "First, used glass bottles and jars <b>are collected</b> from recycling bins and taken to a plant."</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Stage 2.</b> "At the plant, the glass <b>is sorted</b> by colour into clear, green and brown."</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Stage 3.</b> "It <b>is then washed</b> to remove labels and dirt."</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Stage 4.</b> "Next, the clean glass <b>is crushed</b> into small pieces."</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Stage 5.</b> "These pieces <b>are melted</b> in a furnace at about 1,500 °C."</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Stage 6.</b> "The liquid glass <b>is poured</b> into moulds, <b>where it is shaped</b> into new bottles."</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Stage 7.</b> "Finally, the new bottles <b>are ready to be</b> filled and sold, and the cycle can begin again."</p></div>

## 3. Sequence words, varied

| Start | Middle | End |
|---|---|---|
| First, / To begin with, / The process begins when | Next, / Then, / After that, / Once X has been..., / Following this, / At the next stage, / Subsequently, | Finally, / In the final stage, / The process ends when |

Two ways to join stages in one sentence:

- **after / once + present perfect passive:** *Once the glass has been crushed, it is melted.*
- **where / which:** *The pieces go into a furnace, where they are melted at about 1,500 °C.*

## 4. The model answer

<div class="meaning">

**Model answer (about 170 words)**

The diagram illustrates how used glass bottles and jars are recycled into new bottles.

Overall, there are seven stages in the process, beginning with the collection of used glass and ending with new bottles that are ready for use.

To begin with, used glass is collected from recycling bins and transported to a recycling plant. There it is sorted according to its colour, into clear, green and brown glass, and then washed in order to remove any labels and dirt. Once it is clean, the glass is crushed into small pieces.

In the next stage, these pieces are melted in a furnace at a temperature of around 1,500 °C. The molten glass is then poured into moulds, where it is shaped into new bottles. Finally, the bottles are ready to be filled and sold, after which they can be returned to recycling bins, so that the process can start again.

</div>

## 5. Tips

- **Use the labels, but change their form.** The diagram says "Collection"; you write "is collected". This shows grammar range and is not copying.
- **Do not skip stages.** Every stage on the diagram should appear.
- **Add a little detail** from the diagram: temperatures, times, places (*at about 1,500 °C*, *for 24 hours*).
- **No opinions** about whether recycling is good.

<div class="myth">

**"Process diagrams are easy because there is no data."** They are hard to make long enough and varied enough. Plan two body paragraphs (first half and second half of the process) and vary sequence words and sentence types.

</div>

## 6. Practise

<div class="qbank" data-bank="ielts" data-topics="writing-task1" data-mode="practice" data-n="3" data-sub="process"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Man-made processes: present simple passive. Natural cycles: mostly active.
- Overview: number of stages, the start and the end.
- Vary sequence words and join stages with once/after or where/which.
- Turn the labels into verbs; include every stage and small details from the diagram.
- Split the stages into two body paragraphs.

</div>
