**Module 5 · about 16 minutes.** Read first: [Maps and plans](/learn/courses/ielts/maps-and-plans/) from Listening; the direction language is the same. By the end you will describe how a town, campus or building changed, using precise verbs and location phrases.

<div class="bigidea">A map task is "spot the differences" written as a report. Say how the place changed overall, then describe each change with where it is and what happened to it.</div>

## 1. Read the maps

<div class="steps" data-fig="town-map">
<div class="step"><p><b>The overall change.</b> Compare the two maps as a whole first: more buildings, less green, better transport. This becomes your overview.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Farmland → housing.</b> In the north-east, the farmland has been replaced by a housing estate.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Railway.</b> The railway line has been extended eastwards and a new station built south of the river.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Factory → shops.</b> The factory beside the river has been converted into a shopping centre.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>School.</b> The school has been extended to the west.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Woodland → car park.</b> The woodland in the south-west has been cleared to make way for a car park.</p></div>
</div>

## 2. Verbs for change

| What happened | Verbs |
|---|---|
| New | was built, was constructed, was added, was opened, was set up |
| Gone | was demolished, was knocked down, was removed, was cleared (trees) |
| Changed use | was converted into, was turned into, was replaced by, made way for |
| Bigger / smaller | was extended, was expanded, was enlarged; was reduced in size |
| Moved | was relocated to, was moved to |
| No change | remained, stayed the same, was unchanged |

Note **"X was replaced by Y"** (the new thing comes after "by") and **"X made way for Y"** (same order).

## 3. Location language

- **Compass:** in the north, to the south of the river, in the north-east corner.
- **Relative:** next to, opposite, beside, between X and Y, along the road, across the river.
- **Use the map's own landmarks** (the river, the main road) to make the location clear.

## 4. Tense

The maps compare a past date with now: **present perfect passive** (*has been built*) or **past simple passive** (*was built between 2000 and today*). For two past dates, use the past simple. For a planned future map, use *will be* or *is planned to be*. Choose one and keep it consistent.

## 5. Model answer

<div class="meaning">

**Model answer (about 180 words)**

The maps show the village of Eastbrook in 2000 and how it looks today.

Overall, the village has become considerably more residential and better connected, with most of its green space replaced by new buildings and transport facilities.

The most striking change is in the north-east, where the farmland that covered a large part of the village has made way for a housing estate. To the south of the river, the railway line, which previously ended in the middle of the village, has been extended to the eastern edge, and a new station has been built beside it. The factory on the northern bank of the river has also been converted into a shopping centre.

On the western side of the main road, the school has been extended westwards, almost doubling in size. Further south, the woodland that once stood beside the railway has been cleared to make way for a car park. The river and the main road, however, have remained unchanged.

</div>

<div class="myth">

**"I should say why the village changed, for example because the population grew."** That is a reason, and the map does not show it. Describe only what you see.

</div>

## 6. Practise

<div class="qbank" data-bank="ielts" data-topics="writing-task1" data-mode="practice" data-n="2" data-sub="map"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Overview: the general change (more residential, less green, more developed).
- Verbs: built, demolished, converted into, replaced by, made way for, extended, relocated.
- Locate every change with compass words and the map's landmarks.
- Present perfect passive (has been built) for then-and-now; keep the tense consistent.
- Mention what stayed the same, briefly.

</div>
