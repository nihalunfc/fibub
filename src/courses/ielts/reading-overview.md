**Module 3 · about 12 minutes.** Read first: [The four papers, minute by minute](/learn/courses/ielts/test-format/). By the end you will know what the Reading paper looks like for your module, the 14 question types, and the three rules that every Reading answer must follow.

<div class="bigidea">IELTS Reading is not a test of reading every word. It is a test of finding the right place in a long text quickly and then reading that place very carefully.</div>

## 1. Academic and General Training

| | Academic | General Training |
|---|---|---|
| Section 1 | Long passage (easiest) | 2 or 3 short everyday texts: notices, adverts, timetables |
| Section 2 | Long passage | 2 work texts: job descriptions, policies, training |
| Section 3 | Long passage (hardest, often an argument) | 1 long text on a general topic |
| Total words | about 2,150 to 2,750 | about the same, but easier |
| Questions | 40 | 40 |

## 2. The 14 question types

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><p><b>Judging statements:</b> True/False/Not Given (facts, Lesson 4.1) and Yes/No/Not Given (the writer's views, Lesson 4.2).</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Matching:</b> headings to paragraphs (4.3), information to paragraphs (4.4), features or people to statements (4.5), sentence endings (4.6).</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Completion:</b> sentence, summary (with or without a word box), notes, table and flow chart (4.7), and diagram labels (4.9).</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Choosing:</b> multiple choice with one or more answers (4.8), and short answers to questions (4.9).</p></div>
</div>

## 3. Three rules for every answer

1. **Word limits are strict.** "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS FROM THE PASSAGE" means two words, copied exactly from the text.
2. **Spelling counts.** Copy carefully; a copied word spelled wrongly is wrong.
3. **No blanks.** There is no penalty for a wrong answer.

## 4. The order of answers

Most question types follow the order of the text: answer 5 is later in the passage than answer 4. This is your best navigation tool. Exceptions: matching headings, "which paragraph contains...", and some matching features tasks, where answers can be anywhere.

<div class="myth">

**"I must read the whole passage carefully first."** A careful read of 900 words takes most people 6 to 8 minutes, and there are three passages. Skim the passage for its shape in 2 minutes (Lesson 3.2), then go to the questions.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- 60 minutes, 3 sections, 40 questions, no transfer time.
- Academic: three long passages. General Training: short everyday texts, work texts, then one long text.
- 14 question types in four families: judging, matching, completion, choosing.
- Obey the word limit, copy spelling exactly, never leave a blank.
- Most answers follow the order of the text; headings and paragraph matching do not.
