**Module 3 · about 12 minutes.** Read first: [Spotting the paraphrase](/learn/courses/ielts/spot-the-paraphrase/). By the end you will have a time plan for Reading and the rules for when to move on, so you reach all 40 questions.

<div class="bigidea">Each question is worth the same one mark. The plan is simple: never let one hard question steal the time of three easy ones.</div>

## 1. The 60-minute plan

| Minutes | Academic | General Training |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 17 | Passage 1: skim 2, questions 15 | Section 1 (short texts): 15 minutes |
| 17 to 37 | Passage 2: skim 2, questions 18 | Section 2 (work texts): 20 minutes |
| 37 to 58 | Passage 3: skim 2, questions 19 | Section 3 (long text): 25 minutes, including the last 2 for checking |
| 58 to 60 | Check every answer is written, guess any blanks | Same, inside the 25 minutes for Section 3 |

Write the times on the question paper before you start (or note them at the top of the screen) so that you check your watch at 17 and 37 minutes (General Training: at 15 and 35).


Pick a paper and press Play: the clock runs one minute per second.

## 2. Inside a passage

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><p><b>Skim (2 minutes).</b> Title, first sentences, last paragraph; paragraph notes.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Read all the question types (1 minute).</b> Some are faster than others.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Do the "locating" questions first.</b> Completion, TFNG and multiple choice follow the text order and have findable words.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Then matching headings or "which paragraph".</b> After the first questions you know the passage well, so these become quicker.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Write answers on the sheet</b> before you start the next passage.</p></div>
</div>

## 3. The two-minute rule

If a question takes more than about **two minutes**, choose your best guess, mark the question with a small star, and move on. At the end, go back to the stars if you have time.

<div class="problem" data-problem="reading-pace"></div>

<div class="myth">

**"I'll do the passages in a different order, hardest first."** It can work, but it rarely helps: Passage 1 is usually the easiest marks. Do them in order unless practice tests show a clear reason to change.

</div>

## 4. Practise under time

Set a timer for 17 minutes and do this passage. Note how many questions you finished.

<div class="qbank" data-bank="ielts" data-topics="reading-academic" data-mode="practice" data-n="13"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Plan about 17, 20 and 21 minutes for the three passages (more for the hardest), plus 2 minutes to check.
- In each passage: skim, read the question types, do locating questions first, then headings.
- Two-minute rule: guess, star, move on.
- Write answers on the sheet as you go; there is no transfer time.

</div>
