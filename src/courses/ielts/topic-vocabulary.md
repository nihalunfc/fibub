**Module 10 · about 15 minutes.** By the end you will have a plan for building vocabulary across the themes IELTS uses most, with practice for each.

<div class="bigidea">IELTS reuses a small number of themes. A few dozen precise phrases per theme, practised in real answers, cover most of what you will need.</div>

## 1. The themes

| Theme | Sample phrases |
|---|---|
| Work | job security, a heavy workload, flexible working, career prospects |
| Housing | affordable housing, a rental market, overcrowding, commute to the city |
| Health | a balanced diet, sedentary lifestyle, preventive care, mental wellbeing |
| Money | a tight budget, cost of living, save up for, value for money |
| Transport | public transport, traffic congestion, a congestion charge, a cycle lane |
| Environment | carbon emissions, renewable energy, waste reduction, habitat loss |
| Education | tuition fees, distance learning, a well-rounded education, academic pressure |
| Technology | screen time, digital skills, online privacy, automation |
| Community | volunteer work, a sense of belonging, social isolation, local amenities |
| Travel | mass tourism, a cultural experience, a long-haul flight, sustainable travel |
| Food | processed food, a staple diet, food waste, locally grown produce |
| Science | carry out research, a breakthrough, evidence suggests, a controlled experiment |

## 2. A routine that works

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><p><b>Pick one theme a day.</b> Do the practice set below for that theme.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Write down ten phrases</b> you got wrong or liked.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Use five of them</b> in a Part 3 answer out loud and in one essay paragraph.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Review on day 3 and day 7.</b> Spaced review is what makes words stick.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="myth">

**"Learning 1,000 rare words will push me to band 8."** Words you cannot use correctly don't help. Fewer, well-practised phrases on common themes do.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- IELTS draws on a small set of everyday and social themes.
- Learn phrases and collocations per theme, not lists of single words.
- One theme a day: practise, note ten phrases, use five, review on days 3 and 7.
- General, precise vocabulary is enough; no specialist terms needed.
- The practice room has hundreds of theme questions, shared with the CELPIP and TOEFL courses.

</div>
