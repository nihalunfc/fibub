**Module 9 · about 18 minutes.** The last grammar lesson. By the end you will recognise the errors you are most likely to make and fix them in your final check.

<div class="bigidea">Most grammar errors in IELTS come from a short list. Learn the list, find which ones are yours, and check for those every time.</div>

## 1. The 30 errors in eight families

**Nouns and articles**
1. Uncountables made plural: ~~advices, informations, researches, equipments~~ → advice, information, research, equipment.
2. Missing plural: ~~many student~~ → many students; ~~one of the reason~~ → one of the reasons.
3. "The" with general nouns: ~~the society, the nature~~ → society, nature.
4. Missing article with singular countable: ~~It is good idea~~ → It is a good idea.
5. ~~Number of cars~~ → The number of cars.

**Agreement**
6. ~~The number of cars are~~ → The number of cars is.
7. ~~Everyone have~~ → Everyone has.
8. ~~There is many reasons~~ → There are many reasons.
9. Long subjects: ~~The cost of houses in cities are~~ → The cost ... is.

**Verbs and tenses**
10. ~~I have visited Rome last year~~ → I visited Rome last year.
11. ~~Since 2010 it increased~~ → Since 2010 it has increased.
12. ~~If I will have time~~ → If I have time.
13. Missing -s: ~~She work~~ → She works.
14. ~~He suggested me to go~~ → He suggested that I go / suggested going.

**Word form**
15. ~~It is benefit for~~ → It is beneficial for / It benefits.
16. ~~economic growth of economy~~: economic (adjective), economy (noun).
17. ~~affect~~ / effect: affect is a verb, effect a noun.

**Sentence structure**
18. Comma splice: ~~Prices rose, people bought less~~.
19. Fragment: ~~Because it is cheap.~~
20. Double subject: ~~My brother he lives~~ → My brother lives.
21. Missing subject: ~~Is important to study~~ → It is important to study.
22. Indirect questions: ~~I don't know where is it~~ → where it is.

**Prepositions**
23. ~~discuss about~~ → discuss; ~~emphasise on~~ → emphasise.
24. ~~depend of~~ → depend on; ~~interested about~~ → interested in.
25. ~~an increase of 5% in~~ is fine, but ~~rose of 5%~~ → rose by 5%.

**Confusing pairs**
26. make / do: make a decision, do research.
27. say / tell: tell someone, say something.
28. few / a few, little / a little: "few" is negative, "a few" positive.
29. despite / although: despite + noun, although + clause.
30. because / because of: because + clause, because of + noun.

## 2. Make it personal

Take your last two essays (or record one Speaking answer) and mark every error with its number from the list. The three numbers that appear most are your checklist for test day.

<div class="myth">

**"I'll fix grammar by learning more rules."** Most learners already know the rules for these errors. The fix is noticing: a personal checklist used in the final five minutes.
