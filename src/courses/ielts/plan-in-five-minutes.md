**Module 7 · about 13 minutes.** By the end you will have a five-minute routine that gives you a complete map of the essay before you write a word.

<div class="bigidea">Five minutes of planning buys you an essay that answers the question, stays on topic and never runs out of things to say. Decide everything important before you start writing.</div>

## 1. The five-minute routine

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><p><b>Minute 1: read and underline.</b> Topic words, the instruction verbs, every question. Name the type (Lesson 7.2).</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Minute 2: decide your position.</b> Agree, disagree, partly agree; which view; which side outweighs. Choose the one you can support best.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Minutes 3 and 4: two ideas, each with a "why" and an example.</b> Write them as notes, not sentences.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Minute 5: map.</b> Write I, B1, B2, C on your paper with two or three words beside each. Done.</p></div>
</div>


Pick a paper and press Play: the clock runs one minute per second.

<figure class="fig" data-fig="@lang-reading-pacer"><figcaption>Notice which part you should be on at each minute. If you are behind at a checkpoint, skip and come back.</figcaption></figure>

## 2. Finding ideas fast

If your mind goes blank, run through these angles. One or two usually give you a strong idea:

| Angle | Ask |
|---|---|
| People | Who is affected: children, workers, the elderly, families? |
| Money | Does it cost or save money, for whom? |
| Health | Physical or mental health effects? |
| Time | Short term versus long term? |
| Society | Effects on communities, equality, culture? |
| Environment | Effects on pollution, resources, nature? |
| Government | What can laws, taxes or public services do? |
| Technology | How does technology change the issue? |

## 3. A real plan

Prompt: *Some people think that all university students should study whatever they like. Others believe they should only study subjects useful in the future, such as science and technology. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.*

| | Notes |
|---|---|
| Type | Discussion: view A + view B + my opinion |
| Position | Students should choose freely; but governments can encourage useful subjects |
| B1 (view B: useful subjects) | economy needs engineers, doctors → jobs, growth; e.g. shortage of nurses |
| B2 (view A + me: free choice) | motivation → better results; future needs unpredictable; e.g. arts graduates in design, media |
| C | both have merit; free choice + incentives (cheaper fees for shortage subjects) |

That is all you need. It takes four or five minutes and the essay now almost writes itself.

## 4. Check the plan against the question

Before you write, look back at the instruction and tick: every part covered? Position clear? Each idea relevant to the exact topic? This 20-second check prevents the most expensive mistake: a good essay on the wrong question.

<div class="myth">

**"Planning wastes time I need for writing."** Candidates who plan usually write faster, because they never stop to think "what next?". They also avoid repeating ideas and drifting off topic.

</div>

## 5. Practise planning only

Open a prompt, set a five-minute timer and write only the plan in the box. Then compare your ideas with the model and its checklist.

- Stuck? Run through people, money, health, time, society, environment, government, technology.
