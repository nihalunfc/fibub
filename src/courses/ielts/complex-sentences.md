**Module 9 · about 16 minutes.** By the end you will combine ideas into accurate complex sentences, which is what "range" means to an examiner.

<div class="bigidea">A complex sentence is a main clause plus a dependent one. The dependent clause adds who, which, why, when or although, and it can never stand alone.</div>

## 1. Relative clauses

| Word | For | Example |
|---|---|---|
| who | people | Students **who work part-time** learn time management. |
| which | things | The bridge, **which opened in 1998**, carries 40,000 cars a day. |
| that | people or things (defining only) | The book **that I recommended** is out of stock. |
| whose | possession | A country **whose population is ageing** needs more carers. |
| where / when | places / times | the town **where I grew up**, the year **when I left** |

**Defining** clauses say which one (no commas). **Non-defining** clauses add extra information (commas, never "that"):

- *Students who live on campus pay less.* (only those students)
- *My sister, who lives on campus, pays less.* (I have one sister; extra information)

## 2. Subordinate clauses

| Relationship | Words | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Contrast | although, even though, while, whereas | **Although** fees are high, demand keeps rising. |
| Reason | because, since, as | Many people cycle **because** it is cheap. |
| Condition | if, unless, provided that, as long as | **Unless** prices fall, fewer people will buy homes. |
| Purpose | so that, in order to | Cities build bike lanes **so that** cycling is safer. |
| Time | when, while, after, before, once, until | **Once** the glass is crushed, it is melted. |

## 3. Combining: before and after

> *Many people work from home. They save time. They can feel lonely.*
>
> *Although people **who work from home** save time, they can feel lonely.*

Three ideas, one sentence, two kinds of clause.

## 4. Fragments and run-ons

- **Fragment:** *Because it is cheaper.* (no main clause) → *People choose buses because they are cheaper.*
- **Run-on / comma splice:** *Fees are high, many students work.* → *Fees are high, so many students work.* / *Because fees are high, many students work.*

<div class="myth">

**"Every sentence should be complex for band 7."** A mix is best. Short sentences give emphasis; complex ones show range. Accuracy matters most.

</div>

## 5. Practise

<div class="keypoints">

- who (people), which (things), whose (possession), where/when (place/time); that only in defining clauses.
- Non-defining clauses take commas on both sides.
- Subordinators show contrast, reason, condition, purpose and time.
- A dependent clause needs a main clause; avoid fragments and comma splices.
- Mix simple and complex sentences; accuracy first.

</div>
