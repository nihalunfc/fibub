**Module 6 · about 12 minutes.** A reference page to come back to. By the end of the module you should know a few phrases for every common purpose, in each tone, and use them flexibly.

<div class="bigidea">Learn phrases, not letters. A small set of flexible phrases for each purpose and tone saves time on the day; memorised whole letters do not fit the task and do not help your band.</div>

## 1. Openings and closings

| | Formal | Semi-formal | Informal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greeting | Dear Sir or Madam, / Dear Mr Lee, | Dear Mr Lee, / Dear Anna, | Dear Sam, / Hi Sam, |
| First line | I am writing to... | I am writing to let you know... / I hope you are well. | Thanks for your letter! / It's been ages! |
| Last line | I look forward to hearing from you. | Please let me know if you need anything else. | Write back soon! / Can't wait to see you. |
| Sign-off | Yours faithfully, (Sir or Madam) / Yours sincerely, (name) | Kind regards, / Best regards, | Best wishes, / Love, / Take care, |
| Name | full name | full name or first name | first name |

## 2. Phrases by purpose

| Purpose | Formal | Informal |
|---|---|---|
| Complain | I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with... | I'm a bit fed up with... |
| Apologise | Please accept my sincere apologies for... | I'm so sorry about... |
| Thank | I am writing to thank you for... / I am extremely grateful for... | Thanks so much for... / It was really kind of you to... |
| Invite | I would like to invite you to... | Would you like to come to...? / I'd love you to come... |
| Accept | I would be delighted to accept... | I'd love to! |
| Decline | I regret that I will be unable to... | I'm really sorry, but I can't make it. |
| Request | I would be grateful if you could... | Could you do me a favour? |
| Suggest | May I suggest that...? / It might be advisable to... | Why don't we...? / How about...? |
| Advise | I would recommend that you... | If I were you, I'd... / You should definitely... |
| Give news | I am pleased to inform you that... | Guess what! / I've got some great news. |
| Explain | This is because... / The reason for this is that... | The thing is, ... |

## 3. Linking ideas in letters

- **Adding:** also, in addition, as well as this (formal: furthermore).
- **Contrast:** however, although, but (informal), on the other hand.
- **Reason:** because, as, since, due to.
- **Result:** so, therefore (formal), as a result.
- **Moving to a new bullet:** As for..., Regarding... (formal), Anyway, ... / By the way, ... (informal).

**"Examiners like special phrases, so I should use as many as possible."** Phrases help only when they fit. Five natural phrases beat fifteen forced ones. Variety and accuracy matter more than length of phrase list.

<div class="keypoints">

- Match greeting, first line, last line and sign-off to the tone.
- Know one formal and one informal phrase for each purpose: complain, apologise, thank, invite, decline, request, suggest, advise.
- Move between bullets with As for / Regarding (formal) or Anyway / By the way (informal).
- Learn phrases, not whole letters.
- Use phrases only where they fit the task.

</div>
