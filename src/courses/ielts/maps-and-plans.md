**Module 2 · about 14 minutes.** Read first: [Multiple choice without the traps](/learn/courses/ielts/listening-multiple-choice/). By the end you will follow a speaker's directions around a plan and label places correctly, even when you have to turn the map in your head.

<div class="bigidea">Maps are about one thing: knowing where the speaker is standing and which way they are facing at every moment.</div>

## 1. What the task looks like

You see a plan (a park, a museum floor, a campus, a town centre) with some places named and others marked with letters. You match a list of places to the letters, or write the letter next to each numbered place. It is most common in Part 2.

## 2. Before the recording: 30 seconds

1. **Find the start**: "You are here", the entrance, the car park or the reception.
2. **Find north** (if there is a compass) and the named places: they are your landmarks.
3. **Read the list of places** you must find, in order. The answers come in that order.
4. **Notice the shape**: paths, corners, a lake, a central building. Speakers use them.

## 3. Watch it happen: following a route

<div class="steps" data-fig="park-map">
<div class="step"><p>"Start at the main gate, at the bottom of the plan (the south)."</p></div>
<div class="step"><p>"Walk straight ahead along the main path to the <b>fountain</b> in the centre." Straight ahead from the gate means up the page: north.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p>"Turn left at the fountain, and the <b>café</b> is on your left, in the south-west corner." Facing north, left is west. Answer: <b>A</b>.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p>"Go back to the fountain." The speaker resets. Your finger goes back too.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p>"Just to the right of the fountain, to the east, is the <b>bandstand</b>." Answer: <b>F</b>.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p>"Carry on past the bandstand and bear left: the <b>pond</b> is in the north-east corner." Answer: <b>D</b>.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p>"Follow the top path west. At the top of the main path are the <b>toilets</b>." Answer: <b>E</b>.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p>"Keep going west to the corner: the <b>rose garden</b>." Answer: <b>C</b>. B (the playground) was never visited: one letter is often left over.</p></div>
</div>

## 4. Direction language

| Words | Meaning |
|---|---|
| straight ahead, straight on, go up / down | keep going the same way |
| turn left / right, take the first left | change direction (from the walker's point of view) |
| on your left / right | where something is as you pass it |
| opposite, facing, across from | on the other side of a road or space |
| next to, beside, adjoining | very close, same side |
| beyond, past, just after | further along than |
| at the far end, at the top, in the corner | the edges of the plan |
| in the north-west corner, to the east of | compass directions on the page |
| clockwise / anticlockwise (counterclockwise) | around a circle or a building |

<div class="myth">

**"Left on the page is always left."** Only when the speaker faces up the page. If they walk down the page (south), their left is your right. Trace the route with your pencil so you always know which way they face.

</div>

## 5. Practise

<div class="qbank" data-bank="ielts" data-topics="listening-part2" data-mode="practice" data-n="10" data-facets="qtype=map"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Find the starting point, north and the named landmarks before the recording starts.
- Follow the speaker with your pencil; reset when they say "go back to...".
- Left and right are from the walker's point of view: turn the plan in your head.
- Learn the direction words: opposite, beside, beyond, at the far end, clockwise.
- One or more letters may never be used.

</div>
