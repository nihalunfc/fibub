**Module 7 · about 10 minutes.** By the end you will finish every essay with a short conclusion that confirms your answer.

<div class="bigidea">The conclusion answers the question one more time, in new words, with the main reasons in brief. Nothing new, nothing different.</div>

## 1. The pattern

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><p><b>Signal.</b> "In conclusion," or "To sum up," (once is enough).</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Answer again</b>, in different words from the introduction.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Main reasons in brief</b>, in the same order as the body paragraphs.</p></div>
</div>

## 2. Examples by type

- **Opinion:** *In conclusion, I firmly believe that private cars should be kept out of city centres, since this would make them both healthier and safer places to live.*
- **Discussion:** *To sum up, although focusing on science and technology may help the economy, I believe students should be free to choose their subject, as motivated students learn best and future needs are hard to predict.*
- **Advantages outweigh:** *Overall, despite the risk of isolation, the flexibility and saved travel time of home working make it a largely positive change.*
- **Problem / solution:** *In conclusion, traffic congestion harms both health and productivity, but better public transport and congestion charges could greatly reduce it.*
- **Two-part:** *To conclude, more people work from home mainly because of technology, and I see this as a positive trend overall.*

## 3. Avoid

- **New ideas or examples.**
- **A changed position.**
- **Empty sentences:** "This is a very important issue for everyone in the world."
- **Copying your introduction word for word.**

<div class="myth">

**"If I run out of time, I can skip the conclusion."** An essay without a clear ending can feel unfinished and can weaken task response. If time is short, write one sentence: your answer, in new words.

</div>

## 4. Practise

<div class="qbank" data-bank="ielts" data-topics="writing-essay-skills" data-mode="practice" data-n="8" data-sub="conclusion"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Signal, answer again in new words, main reasons in brief.
- One or two sentences, about 30 to 45 words.
- The same position as the introduction.
- No new ideas, no examples, no empty generalisations.
- Out of time? One sentence that restates your answer.

</div>
