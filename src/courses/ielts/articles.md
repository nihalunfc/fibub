**Module 9 · about 14 minutes.** By the end you will make far fewer article errors, which are the most frequent grammar errors in IELTS writing.

<div class="bigidea">Ask two questions about every noun: Is it countable and singular? (then it needs a/an or the). Does the reader know which one? (then it takes the).</div>

## 1. A decision method

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><p><b>Does the reader know exactly which one?</b> (mentioned before, unique, the only one in context, superlative) → <b>the</b>.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>If not, is it singular and countable?</b> → <b>a / an</b> (a car, an idea).</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Plural or uncountable, in general?</b> → <b>no article</b> (Cars pollute. Education matters.)</p></div>
</div>

## 2. a or an?

By **sound**: **an** before a vowel sound (an apple, an hour, an MBA), **a** before a consonant sound (a university, a one-way street, a European city).

## 3. Rules that matter in IELTS

| Use **the** | Example |
|---|---|
| second mention | I bought a laptop. **The** laptop was cheap. |
| unique things | **the** sun, **the** internet, **the** government (of a country) |
| superlatives and ordinals | **the** highest, **the** first, **the** same |
| groups: the + adjective | **the** elderly, **the** unemployed |
| some names | **the** UK, **the** USA, **the** Nile, **the** Pacific |
| graph categories | **the** number of..., **the** proportion of... |

| Use **no article** | Example |
|---|---|
| general plurals | **Children** need routine. |
| general uncountables | **Nature**, **society**, **technology**, **pollution**, **money** |
| most countries, cities, languages | Canada, Tokyo, English |
| institutions for their purpose | go to school / to hospital / to university |

## 4. The classic essay errors

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| The pollution is a big problem. | Pollution is a serious problem. |
| In the modern society... | In modern society... |
| Number of cars rose. | The number of cars rose. |
| It is important for student. | It is important for students / for a student. |

<div class="myth">

**"Articles don't matter because the meaning is still clear."** One article error is minor, but they are frequent, and many small errors together lower the accuracy score.

</div>

## 5. Practise

<div class="qbank" data-bank="english" data-topics="articles" data-mode="practice" data-n="15"></div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Known, unique or superlative → the; singular countable, not specific → a/an; general plural or uncountable → none.
- a/an by sound: an hour, a university.
- the + adjective for groups: the elderly.
- "The number of", "the proportion of" in Task 1.
- No "the" with general society, nature, technology, pollution.

</div>
