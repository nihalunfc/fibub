**Module 6 · about 16 minutes.** Read first: [Choosing the tone](/learn/courses/ielts/letter-tone/). By the end you will write a complaint letter that is clear, complete and firm, without sounding rude.

<div class="bigidea">A good complaint is a calm case with evidence: what happened, what you did about it, and exactly what you want now. Each bullet point gets its own paragraph.</div>

## 1. The plan: one paragraph per bullet

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><p><b>Opening (1 to 2 sentences).</b> Purpose, with the key facts: what, where, when. "I am writing to complain about..."</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Bullet 1: the problem.</b> Specific details: dates, prices, order numbers, what exactly went wrong.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Bullet 2: what you have done</b> (or how it affected you). Calls, emails, the result.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Bullet 3: what you want.</b> One exact action and, if useful, a deadline.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Close.</b> "I look forward to your prompt reply." and the sign-off.</p></div>
</div>

## 2. Useful phrases

| Job | Phrases |
|---|---|
| Purpose | I am writing to complain about... / to express my dissatisfaction with... |
| The problem | Unfortunately, ... / To my disappointment, ... / The item was damaged on arrival. / It stopped working after only a week. |
| Effect | As a result, ... / This has caused me considerable inconvenience, as... |
| What you did | I contacted your customer service team on... / I was told that... / However, I have not yet received... |
| Request | I would therefore like you to... / I would be grateful if you could... / I would appreciate a full refund. |
| Deadline | ...by the end of this month / ...within 14 days |
| Close | I look forward to your prompt reply. / I trust this matter will be resolved quickly. |

## 3. Model answer

Task: the coffee machine in [Choosing the tone](/learn/courses/ielts/letter-tone/).

<div class="meaning">

**Model answer (about 190 words)**

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to complain about a coffee machine (order number 48213) that I purchased from your website on 3 March.

The machine was delivered promptly and worked well for the first few days. However, after only a week it stopped heating the water, and it now produces only cold coffee. I have followed every step in the troubleshooting section of the manual, including descaling the machine, but the problem has not been solved.

On 12 March I telephoned your customer service line and was told that someone would contact me within three working days. Since then, I have sent two emails, but I have still not received any reply, which I find very disappointing given that the machine cost 180 euros.

I would therefore like you to arrange for the faulty machine to be collected and to send me a replacement. If a replacement is not available, I would expect a full refund. I would be grateful if this could be resolved within the next fourteen days.

I look forward to your prompt reply.

Yours faithfully,

Daniel Moreau

</div>

Check it against the bullets: problem (paragraph 2), what you have done (paragraph 3), what you want (paragraph 4). Each has specific details, and the tone stays polite even while it is firm ("which I find very disappointing").

## 4. Common mistakes

- **Rudeness:** "Your company is terrible." Firm is fine; insulting lowers the impression of control.
- **Missing a bullet** or answering it in half a sentence. Each bullet needs real development.
- **A vague request:** "Please help me." Say exactly what.
- **Invented details that contradict the task.** You can add details (a date, a price), but they must fit the situation.

<div class="myth">

**"I must write my address and the date at the top."** The task says you do not need addresses. Start with the greeting the task gives you.

</div>

## 5. Write one

<div class="keypoints">

- Purpose and key facts in the first sentence.
- One paragraph per bullet: the problem, what you did, what you want.
- Specific details: dates, numbers, prices, names of products.
- Firm but polite; an exact request with a deadline.
- Close with "I look forward to your prompt reply" and the matching sign-off.
