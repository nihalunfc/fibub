**Module 1 · about 13 minutes.** By the end you will know the range of bands people usually need, how to find the exact rule for your university, employer or visa, and how IELTS bands convert to Canadian Language Benchmarks.

<div class="bigidea">Your target is not "as high as possible". It is a specific band in each skill, set by the organisation you apply to. Write it down before you start studying.</div>

## 1. Read the rule carefully

Requirements are usually written like this: **"IELTS Academic 6.5 overall, with no band below 6.0."** That sentence has three parts, and you must meet all three:

1. **The module**: Academic or General Training.
2. **The overall band**: 6.5 or higher.
3. **A minimum for each skill**: here, no single skill below 6.0. Some ask for a higher minimum in one skill, such as "Writing 7.0" for teaching or "Speaking 7.0" for a customer-facing job.

Also check **how old** the result can be (usually two years from the test date) and whether results from **more than one test can be combined** (some accept the best band from each skill across tests within a time window, many do not).

## 2. Typical bands (always check the exact rule)

These are typical ranges as of late 2026, to help you plan. They change, and each organisation sets its own, so treat this as a starting point only:

| Purpose | Usual range |
|---|---|
| Undergraduate study (UK, Canada, Australia) | Academic 6.0 to 6.5, no band below 5.5 to 6.0 |
| Postgraduate study | Academic 6.5 to 7.0, no band below 6.0 to 6.5 |
| Top universities and some courses (law, medicine, education) | Academic 7.0 to 7.5, sometimes 8.0 in Speaking or Writing |
| Nursing and medical registration (UK, Australia, Canada) | Academic 7.0 in each skill is common (some boards accept 6.5 in Writing) |
| Canada Express Entry, Federal Skilled Worker | General Training, at least CLB 7 in every skill (6.0 in each) |
| Canada Express Entry, high points | General Training, CLB 9 or higher (L 8.0, R/W/S 7.0) |
| UK Skilled Worker visa | B2 level in each skill for new applications from January 2026 (about 5.5 in IELTS for UKVI); it was B1 before. Check gov.uk |
| Australia skilled migration | "Competent", "Proficient" or "Superior" English: roughly 6, 7 or 8 in each skill |

## 3. Canada: IELTS to CLB

Canada measures English with the **Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB)**. For Express Entry and many other programmes, IRCC accepts IELTS **General Training** and converts each skill separately with this chart (checked 22 November 2026):

| CLB | Listening | Reading | Writing | Speaking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 8.5 | 8.0 | 7.5 | 7.5 |
| 9 | 8.0 | 7.0 | 7.0 | 7.0 |
| 8 | 7.5 | 6.5 | 6.5 | 6.5 |
| 7 | 6.0 | 6.0 | 6.0 | 6.0 |
| 6 | 5.5 | 5.0 | 5.5 | 5.5 |
| 5 | 5.0 | 4.0 | 5.0 | 5.0 |
| 4 | 4.5 | 3.5 | 4.0 | 4.0 |

Look closely: **Listening needs a higher band** than the other skills for CLB 8 and above, and Reading needs a lower one. Express Entry gives many more points at CLB 9 and above, so going from CLB 8 to CLB 9 in all four skills can matter more than almost anything else in a profile.

## 4. Find your exact target in five minutes

1. Open the official page of the university course, licensing body or visa you want.
2. Search the page for "IELTS" and copy the requirement word for word into your notes.
3. Write your target as four numbers, for example: **L 8.0 · R 7.0 · W 7.0 · S 7.0**.
4. If you have a practice score, write it next to the target and find the biggest gap. That skill gets the most study time in your plan (Lesson 1.5).
5. Note the date your result must still be valid on (usually the application date, sometimes the course start date).

<div class="keypoints">

- Read the whole requirement: module, overall band, minimum per skill, and how old the result can be.
- Most universities ask for 6.0 to 7.0 Academic; nursing and medicine often 7.0 in each skill.
- Canada converts each IELTS General Training skill to a CLB level separately; CLB 7 is 6.0 in each skill, CLB 9 is L 8.0 and 7.0 in the others.
- Write your target as four numbers and plan around the biggest gap.
- Always confirm on the official page: requirements change.

</div>
