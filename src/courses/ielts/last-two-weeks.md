**Module 11 · about 10 minutes.** The last lesson of the course. By the end you will have a day-by-day plan for your final fortnight.

<div class="bigidea">The last two weeks are for sharpening, not learning everything. Two mocks show you where marks are leaking; the days between fix those leaks; the last day is for rest.</div>

## 1. The plan

| Day | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Full mock test ([Lesson 11.1](/learn/courses/ielts/mock-tests/)) |
| 2 | Review the mock: label every error, pick your two weakest question types |
| 3 to 6 | Each day: 30 to 45 minutes on weakness 1 or 2, one Task 2 essay or letter/chart, 15 minutes of speaking out loud |
| 7 | Rest or light review |
| 8 | Second full mock |
| 9 | Review: did the weak areas improve? Choose the final focus |
| 10 to 12 | Targeted practice, one timed essay a day, speaking every day |
| 13 | Light review: your error checklist, phrase lists, test-day logistics ([Lesson 11.2](/learn/courses/ielts/test-day/)) |
| 14 | Test day |

## 2. A daily session (about 90 minutes)

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><p><b>Speak out loud, 15 minutes:</b> one Part 2 card with the timer, recorded, then one Part 3 set.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Targeted practice, 30 to 45 minutes:</b> your weakest question type from the mock review.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Write, 20 to 40 minutes:</b> one task under time, then the checklist and your top-three error list.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Listen and review, 10 minutes:</b> something in English (a podcast while travelling counts) and the vocabulary you noted, not new lists.</p></div>

## 3. Look after yourself

Sleep, food and exercise affect concentration more than one more hour of study. The night before, stop by early evening.

<div class="myth">

**"I need to cover every lesson again in the last two weeks."** You need to fix the few things that cost you most marks. Revisit only the lessons your mock reviews point to.

</div>

## 4. You have finished the course

You have worked through every part of IELTS: the test, Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking, grammar, vocabulary and test day. Keep using the [practice room](/learn/courses/ielts/practice/) for as long as you like. Good luck.

<div class="keypoints">

- Two full mocks, a week apart, each followed by a careful review.
- Spend the days between on your two weakest question types.
- Speak and write a little every day under time.
- Rest the day before; check logistics.
- Revisit only the lessons your mistakes point to.

</div>
