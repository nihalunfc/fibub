**Module 11 · about 20 minutes to read, about 3 hours to take.** By the end you will have taken a complete practice IELTS from this course's question bank and know exactly what to study next.

<div class="bigidea">A mock test is two things: a rehearsal of test day, and a diagnosis. Take it under real conditions, then spend as long reviewing it as you spent taking it.</div>

## 1. Set up real conditions

- A quiet room, a clock, no phone, no dictionary, no pausing.
- Listening, Reading and Writing back to back, as on test day (2 hours 40 minutes).
- Speaking the same day or the next, recorded on your phone.
- Paper test? Practise writing answers on paper. Computer test? Type your essays.

## 2. Timer and band calculator

Use the timer for each paper, then enter your results at the end.

## 3. The mock test

Each practice block below draws fresh questions from the bank, so you can take several different mocks. In each block, answer everything first and only then check, as you would on test day.

## 4. Review: the most important hour

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><p><b>List every wrong answer</b> in Listening and Reading, with its question type (form completion, TFNG, headings...).</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Give each one a cause</b>: didn't understand, ran out of time, spelling, word limit, misread the question, distractor, NOT GIVEN confusion.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Count the causes.</b> The biggest two are your plan: go back to those lessons and do 30 targeted questions each this week.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Writing:</b> tick the checklist for each task, read the model, and mark your top three grammar errors from <a href="/learn/courses/ielts/common-errors/">the 30 most common errors</a>.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Speaking:</b> listen to your recording three times: for fluency, for grammar, for pronunciation.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="myth">

**"My mock score is my real score."** Home mocks are a guide. Raw-score conversions vary a little between tests, and your own Writing and Speaking marks are estimates. Use trends across several mocks, not one result.

</div>

<div class="keypoints">

- Real conditions: timed, no pausing, papers back to back, Speaking recorded.
- Use the timer and band calculator; treat the result as an estimate.
- Review every mistake and label its cause; the top two causes are next week's plan.
- Mark Writing with the checklists and models; review Speaking recordings three times.
- One full mock every week or two in the final month or two.

</div>
