**Module 8 · about 13 minutes.** By the end you will have practical ways to keep talking smoothly, even when you need time to think.

<div class="bigidea">Fluency is keeping the conversation moving: a steady pace, ideas that connect, and natural ways to think aloud instead of silence.</div>

## 1. Buying time, naturally

| Situation | Say |
|---|---|
| A hard question | That's an interesting question. I've never really thought about it, but... |
| You need a second | Let me think... / Well, I suppose... / How can I put this... |
| You want it repeated | Sorry, could you say that again? / Do you mean...? |
| You lost a word | What's the word... it's a kind of... (paraphrase it) |

Use each one occasionally. Repeating "That's a good question" before every answer sounds memorised.

## 2. Extending answers

When an answer feels too short, add one of: **because** (a reason), **for example** (an example), **but** (a contrast), **so** (a result), **which means** (a consequence), **when I was younger** (a comparison with the past).

## 3. Self-correction

"I go... I went there last year." Quick and smooth is fine, and can even show control. Avoid long apologies or restarting the whole answer.

## 4. A practice routine

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><p><b>Record</b> a 2-minute Part 2 answer.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Listen</b> and count long pauses (over 2 seconds) and "umm"s.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Repeat the same card</b> immediately. The second try is almost always smoother.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Do it daily</b> with a new card. Ten minutes a day beats one hour a week.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="myth">

**"Fillers like 'umm' always lose marks."** Everyone uses some. The problem is frequent, long hesitation that breaks the flow. Replace some of it with natural phrases like "well" and "let me think".

</div>

- Fluency means a steady pace and connected ideas, not speed.
- Buy time with natural phrases, not long silences; vary them.
- Extend with because, for example, but, so, which means.
- Self-correct quickly and move on.
- Record, listen, repeat the same task, daily.
