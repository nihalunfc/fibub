**Module 10 · about 13 minutes.** Read with: [Spellings and numbers](/learn/courses/ielts/spelling-and-numbers/) in Listening. By the end you will know the rules behind most spelling errors and the words learners misspell most.

<div class="bigidea">In Listening and Reading a misspelled answer scores zero. A few rules and a short personal list of trap words remove most of the risk.</div>

## 1. Five rules

| Rule | Examples |
|---|---|
| Double the final consonant (short vowel + one consonant, stressed) | stop → stopped, begin → beginning, but visit → visited |
| Drop silent e before a vowel suffix | make → making, hope → hoping, but agree → agreeing |
| y → i after a consonant | study → studies, happy → happiness, but play → played |
| i before e except after c (sound "ee") | believe, achieve, but receive, ceiling |
| -ful has one l | careful, useful, beautiful |

## 2. Words learners often misspell

accommodation, address, beginning, business, calendar, colleague, committee, environment, February, government, guarantee, library, necessary, occasionally, receive, recommend, restaurant, separate, temperature, Wednesday.

## 3. British or American?

Both are accepted. Choose one and be consistent: colour/color, centre/center, organise/organize, travelling/traveling, programme/program, licence/license.
