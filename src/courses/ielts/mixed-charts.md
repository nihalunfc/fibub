**Module 5 · about 14 minutes.** This is the last Task 1 lesson; read the earlier ones first. By the end you will handle two visuals in 150 to 200 words without losing the main features.

<div class="bigidea">Two charts, one report: an introduction for both, one overview with the key point of each, one paragraph per chart, and a link only where the data really connects.</div>

## 1. The shape

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><p><b>Introduction.</b> One sentence that names both: "The bar chart shows... and the table gives..."</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Overview.</b> The main feature of chart 1 and the main feature of chart 2, in one or two sentences.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Paragraph for chart 1.</b> Selected details.</p></div>
<div class="step"><p><b>Paragraph for chart 2.</b> Selected details, with a link back to chart 1 if one exists.</p></div>
</div>

## 2. Linking phrases

- *The table, meanwhile, shows that...*
- *Turning to the table, ...*
- *This pattern is also reflected in the table, which...*
- *In contrast to the growth shown in the bar chart, the table reveals...*

## 3. Worked example

*The bar chart shows the number of international students at one university from 2015 to 2025 (in thousands): 2015: 4.0, 2020: 5.5, 2025: 8.2. The table shows the top four subjects these students chose in 2025: Business 34%, Engineering 26%, Computing 22%, Health 9%.*

<div class="meaning">

**Model answer (about 170 words)**

The bar chart illustrates how many international students attended one university between 2015 and 2025, while the table shows the four most popular subjects among these students in 2025.

Overall, the number of international students roughly doubled over the decade, and in 2025 business was clearly the most popular field of study.

In 2015, the university had 4,000 international students. This number rose moderately to 5,500 in 2020 and then increased much more sharply over the following five years, reaching 8,200 in 2025, just over twice the original figure.

Turning to the table, just over a third of international students (34%) were studying business in 2025. Engineering and computing were the next most common choices, at about a quarter (26%) and just over a fifth (22%) respectively. Health attracted far fewer students, at only 9%, meaning that the four subjects together accounted for over 90% of all international students.

</div>

Notice the overview: one point per chart. And notice what is **not** there: no attempt to explain the growth by the subjects.

<div class="myth">

**"I need to describe both charts equally in detail."** You need the main features of both. If one chart is simple (three bars), its paragraph can be shorter.

</div>

## 5. Practise everything in Task 1

<div class="keypoints">

- One introduction naming both visuals, one overview with the key point of each.
- One body paragraph per visual; link them only where the data connects.
- Same 150-word minimum, so select even more strictly.
- Use linking phrases: meanwhile, turning to, in contrast to.
- Never invent a cause that joins the two charts.

</div>
