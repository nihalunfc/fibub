**Start here · about 14 minutes.** By the end you will know what each exam looks like, how it is marked, and how one preparation covers all of them.

<div class="bigidea">All five engineering entrance exams test the same Plus Two Physics, Chemistry and Maths. They differ in three things only: how deep the questions go, how fast you must answer, and how wrong answers are punished.</div>

## 1. Five exams, one syllabus

Every exam in this course is built on the NCERT Class 11 and Class 12 chapters. A student who truly understands those chapters, and has practised every way they can be asked, is ready for all five. That is why this course has one set of lessons and one question bank, with each question tagged for the exams it suits.

| Exam | Who runs it | What it opens |
|---|---|---|
| **KEAM** | Commissioner for Entrance Examinations, Kerala | Engineering seats in Kerala government, aided and self-financing colleges |
| **CUSAT CAT** | Cochin University of Science and Technology | B.Tech seats at CUSAT's engineering schools |
| **COMEDK UGET** | Consortium of private colleges in Karnataka | Engineering seats in private Karnataka colleges |
| **JEE Main** | National Testing Agency | NITs, IIITs, other centrally funded institutes, many state and private colleges, and the gate to JEE Advanced |
| **JEE Advanced** | One of the IITs, by turn | The IITs (and a few other institutes) |

Three things jump out:

- **Time per question ranges from under a minute to several minutes.** CUSAT CAT gives you about 48 seconds per question; JEE Main gives 2 minutes 24 seconds; a JEE Advanced paper gives several minutes per question because each one is long and layered.
- **Maths is heavy in the state exams.** KEAM's 75 Maths questions are worth 300 of its 600 marks. CUSAT CAT has 90 Maths questions out of 225.
- **Negative marking differs.** JEE Main, KEAM and CUSAT take marks away for a wrong answer; COMEDK does not.

<div class="myth">

**"JEE Main is just harder KEAM."** Not quite. JEE Main asks fewer questions but each one usually joins two ideas or hides a condition. KEAM and CUSAT ask more, simpler questions and reward speed and accuracy. Practise both styles: the course's level tags (1 to 5) do exactly this.

</div>

## 3. How negative marking changes your score

Your score is not the number of questions you got right. With negative marking,

```math
\text{score} = (\text{right}) \times (\text{marks for right}) - (\text{wrong}) \times (\text{marks for wrong})
```

<div class="meaning">
<dl>
<dt>right</dt><dd>questions you answered correctly</dd>
<dt>wrong</dt><dd>questions you answered and got wrong (blanks are neither)</dd>
<dt>Physical meaning</dt><dd>Every careless wrong answer cancels part of a right one. In JEE Main and KEAM, four careless mistakes wipe out one correct answer completely.</dd>
</dl>

## 4. What each exam rewards

<div class="steps">
<div class="step"><b>KEAM.</b> Speed in Maths and accuracy everywhere. 150 questions in 3 hours: 72 seconds each. Most questions are one or two steps. The Kerala rank list has traditionally combined your KEAM score with your Plus Two Physics, Chemistry and Maths marks, so your board exam matters too (check the current prospectus).</div>
<div class="step"><b>CUSAT CAT.</b> Pure speed. 225 questions in 3 hours, +3 and −1. Knowing shortcuts and standard results by heart is worth more here than anywhere else.</div>
<div class="step"><b>COMEDK UGET.</b> Coverage. 180 questions, +1 each, no negative marks. Attempt everything; a smart guess after crossing out options is always worth it.</div>
<div class="step"><b>JEE Main.</b> Understanding plus speed. 75 questions (20 single-answer and 5 numerical per subject), +4 and −1 for both kinds. Questions often mix two ideas. Your percentile also decides whether you may sit JEE Advanced.</div>
<div class="step"><b>JEE Advanced.</b> Depth. Two 3-hour papers whose formats change every year: single answer, one-or-more correct with partial marks, integer and decimal answers, matching lists and paragraphs. Questions join several chapters and punish half-understanding.</div>
</div>

## 5. One plan for all of them

Because the syllabus is shared, plan around the hardest exam you are aiming for and the fastest one:

1. **Learn each chapter to JEE Main depth** (levels 1 to 3 in the question bank). That already covers KEAM, CUSAT and COMEDK.
2. **Build speed** with timed sets of level 1 and 2 questions before KEAM, CUSAT and COMEDK.
3. **If you are aiming for the IITs**, add level 4 and 5 questions and the multi-correct, matching and paragraph formats.

<div class="keypoints">

- KEAM, CUSAT CAT, COMEDK, JEE Main and JEE Advanced all test NCERT Class 11 and 12 Physics, Chemistry and Maths.
- They differ in depth (JEE Advanced deepest), speed (CUSAT fastest, about 48 s per question) and marking (COMEDK has no negative marks).
- Score = right × marks for right − wrong × marks for wrong; four careless errors in JEE Main or KEAM cancel one correct answer.
- Prepare to JEE Main depth first, then add speed for the state exams and depth (levels 4 and 5) for JEE Advanced.
- Patterns change: check the official website every year.

</div>
