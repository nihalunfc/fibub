**Practice · Class 8 Science · Chapter 3.** Read the chapter lesson, [Health: The Ultimate Treasure](/learn/courses/ncert-class-8-science/health-the-ultimate-treasure/), first. Try each question before opening the answer.

<div class="bigidea">Health = body + mind + community. Prevention (clean water, soap, vaccines, balanced food) beats cure.</div>

## How to answer

1. For deficiency questions, name the nutrient, the disease and one food that supplies it.
2. For spread of disease, name the route, then say how to break it.
3. In longer answers, give short, numbered points.

## 1-mark questions

1. Give the three parts of health.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Physical, mental and social health.</details>

2. Which mineral does iodised salt provide?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Iodine (it prevents goitre).</details>

3. Is diabetes infectious or non-infectious?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Non-infectious.</details>

4. Name a disease spread by mosquitoes.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Malaria or dengue (also chikungunya).</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

5. Why is a balanced diet important? *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>It supplies energy, material for growth and repair, and vitamins and minerals that protect the body. Missing nutrients cause deficiency diseases and weaker immunity.</details>

6. Differentiate between infectious and non-infectious diseases with one example each. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>An infectious disease is caused by germs and spreads from person to person (measles). A non-infectious disease is not caused by germs and does not spread (diabetes).</details>

7. Name the deficiency disease and one source for each: vitamin D, iron, vitamin A. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Vitamin D: rickets (sunlight, milk, eggs). Iron: anaemia (spinach, jaggery, dates). Vitamin A: night blindness (carrot, papaya, spinach).</details>

8. Why should we wash our hands with soap before eating? *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Hands pick up germs from surfaces and the toilet. Soap and water remove or kill most of them, so they do not enter our mouth with food and cause infection.</details>

9. How does a vaccine give protection against a disease? *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A vaccine has a dead or weakened germ, or a harmless part of it. The body's white blood cells make antibodies against it and keep memory cells. If the real germ enters later, the body recognises it quickly and makes a fast, strong response, so we do not fall ill.</details>

10. A student is 1.5 m tall and has a mass of 45 kg. Find the BMI (mass ÷ height²). *(3 marks)*

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Height squared = 1.5 × 1.5 = 2.25 m².

BMI = 45 ÷ 2.25 = **20 kg/m²**.

</details>

## 5-mark questions

11. Describe four ways in which the body defends itself against germs. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>1. Skin is a barrier, and mucus and hairs in the nose trap germs. 2. Tears, saliva and stomach acid kill germs. 3. White blood cells swallow germs that enter the body. 4. Antibodies stick to a particular germ and mark it for destruction. 5. Memory cells remember the germ, so the response is quicker the next time (immunity).</details>

12. Explain how handwashing, safe water and clean surroundings prevent diseases spreading. Give an example of a disease for each. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Handwashing with soap removes germs from hands, preventing diarrhoea and cold. Boiled or filtered water kills the bacteria that cause cholera and typhoid. Clean surroundings with no standing water stop mosquitoes breeding, so malaria and dengue do not spread. Covering the mouth when coughing stops droplet spread of colds. Each one breaks a link in the chain from source to a new person.</details>

## Case-based question

13. In a village, many children fall ill with loose motions after the monsoon. The health worker finds that the village well is next to an open drain, and that most families do not wash hands with soap. She advises boiling water, building a cover for the well, and handwashing.

(a) Name the route by which the disease is probably spreading. *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Through contaminated water (and dirty hands).</details>

(b) Why does boiling the water help? *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Boiling kills the germs in the water.</details>

(c) Why must the well be covered and kept away from the drain? *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>So that germs from dirty water cannot get into the drinking water.</details>

(d) Is the disease infectious or non-infectious? *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Infectious, because it is caused by germs and spreads from person to person.</details>

## Assertion-reason

14. **Assertion (A):** Antibiotics should not be taken for a common cold. **Reason (R):** A cold is caused by a virus, and antibiotics do not kill viruses.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Both true, and R explains A.</details>

15. **Assertion (A):** A person with no disease is always fully healthy. **Reason (R):** Health also includes mental and social well-being.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A is false; R is true. Health is more than the absence of disease.</details>

16. **Assertion (A):** When most people in a community are vaccinated, even unvaccinated people are somewhat protected. **Reason (R):** The germ finds it hard to pass from person to person.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Both true, and R explains A (herd immunity).</details>

<div class="keypoints">

- Health = physical, mental and social well-being.
- Balanced diet: carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, fibre and water; deficiency causes anaemia, scurvy, rickets, goitre and night blindness.
- Infectious diseases spread by air, water, food, touch and vectors; break the chain by handwashing, safe water, clean surroundings and vaccines.
- Defences: skin, chemicals, white blood cells, antibodies, memory cells. Vaccines train immunity; herd immunity protects communities.
- Antibiotics work only on bacteria.
