**Practice · Class 8 Science · Chapter 8.** Read the chapter lesson, [Nature of Matter: Elements, Compounds, and Mixtures](/learn/courses/ncert-class-8-science/nature-of-matter-elements-compounds-and/), first. Try each question before opening the answer.

<div class="bigidea">Sort with two questions: how many kinds of atom? Are they chemically joined? Separate by a property that differs.</div>

## How to answer

1. For "classify" questions, give the reason (one kind of atom, fixed ratio, can be separated by physical means).
2. For separation, name the property of the mixture that the method uses.
3. Write formulas and symbols with capital and small letters exactly (Co is cobalt, CO is a compound).

## 1-mark questions

1. What is the symbol of gold?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Au.</details>

2. Name a liquid metal.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Mercury.</details>

3. Is sea water a pure substance or a mixture?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A mixture.</details>

4. Which method separates husk from grain using wind?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Winnowing.</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

5. Give two differences between a mixture and a compound. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A mixture has substances in any proportion and they keep their own properties; they can be separated by physical methods. A compound has elements joined in a fixed ratio, has new properties and can be split only by chemical methods.</details>

6. Classify as element, compound or mixture: (a) copper wire, (b) carbon dioxide, (c) air. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(a) Element: only copper atoms. (b) Compound: carbon and oxygen joined in a fixed ratio. (c) Mixture: nitrogen, oxygen and other gases mixed in no fixed ratio.</details>

7. Why is oxygen gas, O₂, an element and not a compound? *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Both atoms in an O₂ molecule are oxygen atoms, so only one kind of atom is present. A compound needs two or more different kinds.</details>

8. Explain how you would separate a mixture of sand and salt. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Add water and stir: salt dissolves, sand does not. Filter the mixture; the sand stays on the filter paper. Heat the filtrate (salt water) to evaporate the water and collect the salt that is left behind.</details>

9. In 30 g of a mixture, 6 g is salt. Find the percentage of salt. *(3 marks)*

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

`$\text{percentage} = \frac{6}{30} \times 100 = 20$` %. So the mixture is 20% salt.

</details>

10. Water has hydrogen and oxygen in the mass ratio 1 : 8. How much hydrogen is in 27 g of water? *(3 marks)*

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Total parts = 9, so one part = 27 ÷ 9 = 3 g. Hydrogen is 1 part = **3 g** (and oxygen is 24 g).

</details>

## 5-mark questions

11. Describe, with the iron and sulfur experiment, how a mixture differs from a compound. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>When iron filings and sulfur powder are only mixed, each keeps its properties: iron is grey and magnetic, sulfur is yellow. A magnet separates the iron, so the sample is a mixture. When the mixture is strongly heated, a black solid, iron sulfide, forms. It is a new substance with new properties and it is not attracted by a magnet. It is a compound because the iron and sulfur are chemically joined in a fixed ratio, and they cannot be separated by simple physical means.</details>

12. A mixture contains iron filings, sand, salt and water. Describe the steps to get each substance back. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>1) Use a magnet to remove the iron filings. 2) Filter the rest through filter paper: the sand stays on the paper and salt water passes through. 3) Heat the salt water to evaporate it: the salt is left behind. 4) For water too, condense the vapour (distillation). Each method uses a different property: magnetism, insolubility in water and difference in boiling point.</details>

## Case-based question

A salt farmer fills a shallow pan with sea water and lets the Sun heat it for several days. White crystals appear at the bottom. Some of the crystals are muddy, so the farmer dissolves them in clean water, lets the mud settle and filters.

13. Name the process that forms the crystals. *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Evaporation.</details>

14. What kind of mixture is sea water? *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A homogeneous mixture (a solution of salts in water).</details>

15. Which property lets the farmer remove the mud by filtering? *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The mud is insoluble in water and the particles are too big to pass through the filter, whereas the dissolved salt passes through.</details>

16. Is the salt obtained a compound or an element? *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A compound (sodium chloride, Na and Cl chemically joined).</details>

## Assertion and reason

For each, choose: (A) both true and R explains A, (B) both true but R does not explain A, (C) A true and R false, (D) A false and R true.

17. **Assertion:** Water is a compound. **Reason:** Water can be separated into hydrogen and oxygen by ordinary filtering.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(C). A is true. R is false: a compound can be split only by chemical methods such as electrolysis.</details>

18. **Assertion:** Brass is a mixture. **Reason:** Its copper and zinc can be present in different proportions.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(A). A variable ratio is a sign of a mixture.</details>
