**Practice · Class 8 Science · Chapter 6.** Read the chapter lesson, [Pressure, Winds, Storms, and Cyclones](/learn/courses/ncert-class-8-science/pressure-winds-storms-and-cyclones/), first. Try each question before opening the answer.

<div class="bigidea">One formula, p = F ÷ A, and one rule, air moves from high pressure to low pressure, explain most of this chapter.</div>

## How to answer

1. Write the formula, put in the numbers with units, then give the answer with its unit.
2. In "why" questions, name the quantity that changes (area, depth, height or temperature).
3. For storms, say what the danger is and what people should do.

## 1-mark questions

1. Name the unit of pressure.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The pascal (Pa).</details>

2. What happens to the pressure of a liquid as the depth increases?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>It increases.</details>

3. Which part of a cyclone is calm?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The eye.</details>

4. Between which two regions does wind blow?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>From a high-pressure region to a low-pressure region.</details>

## 2-mark and 3-mark questions

5. Why are the straps of a heavy school bag made wide? *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A wide strap spreads the weight over a larger area, so the pressure on the shoulder is smaller and the bag is more comfortable.</details>

6. A box exerts a force of 500 N on a floor over 0.25 m². Find the pressure. *(3 marks)*

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

`$p = F/A = 500 / 0.25 = 2000$` Pa.

</details>

7. Why does water from a lower hole in a tank come out faster than from an upper hole? *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The lower hole is deeper, so there is more water above it and the liquid pressure is greater there.</details>

8. Explain how a sea breeze blows in the daytime. *(3 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The land heats faster than the sea. The air over the land warms, expands and rises, leaving a low-pressure region near the ground. Cooler air over the sea has higher pressure and moves toward the land. This moving air is the sea breeze.</details>

9. A sealed, empty tin can is crushed when some of the air is pumped out. Why? *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>When air is removed, the pressure inside falls. The atmospheric pressure outside is now larger than the pressure inside, so it pushes the walls in.</details>

10. State two ways people can stay safe when a cyclone warning is issued. *(2 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Move to a cyclone shelter if told to do so, stay indoors away from windows, keep drinking water and a torch ready, and follow official alerts (any two).</details>

## 5-mark questions

11. Describe how a cyclone forms and name its main parts. List the dangers it brings. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A cyclone forms over warm oceans. Warm, moist air rises, leaving low pressure below. Air from the surroundings rushes in and, because Earth rotates, spirals around the centre. The rising air forms huge clouds and heavy rain. The main parts are the eye (calm, clear, lowest pressure) and the eye wall around it (strongest winds and rain), with spiral rain bands outside. Dangers: very strong winds, heavy rain and flooding, and a storm surge of sea water onto the coast.</details>

12. Explain why atmospheric pressure decreases with height and give two effects of air pressure in daily life. *(5 marks)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Pressure is caused by the weight of the air above. As we go higher there is less air above us, so the pressure falls: it is about half at 5.5 km. Two daily-life effects: a drink rises in a straw because the outside air pressure pushes it up when we reduce the pressure inside; a rubber suction hook sticks to a wall because the outside air presses it against the wall when the air under it has been squeezed out.</details>

## Case-based question

A coastal village hears that a cyclone is 240 km away, moving toward the coast at 20 km/h. The sea is calm, but the sky is overcast and the wind is rising. Fishermen are told to bring their boats in.

13. In how many hours might the cyclone reach the coast? *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>240 ÷ 20 = 12 hours.</details>

14. Why is the wind rising as the cyclone gets closer? *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The pressure difference between the village and the low-pressure centre becomes larger, so the wind gets stronger.</details>

15. Suddenly the wind drops and the sky clears for an hour. What part of the cyclone is overhead, and should people go out? *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The eye. They should not go out: the strong winds of the eye wall will return from the other side.</details>

16. Name one danger from the sea during a cyclone. *(1 mark)*
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A storm surge, a rush of sea water onto the land.</details>

## Assertion and reason

For each, choose: (A) both true and R explains A, (B) both true but R does not explain A, (C) A true and R false, (D) A false and R true.

17. **Assertion:** A person lying on a bed of nails is not hurt. **Reason:** The weight is spread over a large number of nails, so the pressure from each is small.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(A). Many nails mean a large total area.</details>

18. **Assertion:** The pressure at the bottom of a lake is more than at the top. **Reason:** Water presses only on the bottom of its container.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>(C). A is true because of the weight of water above. R is false: liquids press in all directions.</details>
