<div class="bigidea">Five facts solve almost every SAT angle and triangle question: a straight line is 180°, opposite angles are equal, parallel lines copy angles, a triangle's angles add to 180°, and similar triangles keep their side ratios.</div>

## 1. Angles on lines

- Angles on a straight line add to 180°.
- Vertical (opposite) angles where two lines cross are equal.
- A transversal across parallel lines makes equal corresponding and alternate angles; same-side interior angles add to 180°.

## 2. Triangles

<div class="steps">
<div class="step">Draw any triangle and tear off its three corners.</div>
<div class="step">Put the corners side by side along a straight line.</div>
<div class="step">They fill the straight line exactly: the three angles of every triangle add to <b>180°</b>.</div>
</div>

<div class="problem" data-problem="lt-g01"></div>

Equal sides face equal angles: an isosceles triangle has two equal base angles, an equilateral triangle has three 60° angles.

## 3. The Pythagorean theorem

In a right triangle, <i>a</i>² + <i>b</i>² = <i>c</i>², where <i>c</i> is the hypotenuse (the side opposite the right angle). Common whole-number sets: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25, and their multiples.

<div class="problem" data-problem="lt-g04"></div>

## 4. Similar triangles

Two triangles with the same three angles are similar: one is a scaled copy of the other, so matching sides share one ratio.

<div class="problem" data-problem="lt-g03"></div>

<div class="meaning">

<dl>
<dt>Similar</dt><dd>same shape, maybe a different size: equal angles, proportional sides</dd>
<dt>Congruent</dt><dd>same shape and same size</dd>
<dt>Physical meaning</dt><dd>Similar triangles let you measure a tree from its shadow: your height over your shadow equals the tree's height over its shadow, at the same time of day.</dd>
</dl>

</div>

<div class="myth">

**"Two triangles with two equal sides are similar."** Similarity is about angles (or all three side ratios). Two equal angles are enough, because the third then must match too.

</div>
