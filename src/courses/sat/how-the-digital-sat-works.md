**Module 1 · about 12 minutes.** By the end you will know exactly what happens on test day, how long each part lasts, and why the first module of each section matters most.

<div class="bigidea">The digital SAT is two sections, each split into two timed modules. Do well on the first module and the test gives you a harder second module, which is the only road to a top score.</div>

## 1. The test at a glance

The SAT is taken on a laptop or tablet in the College Board's **Bluebook** app, at a test centre or at your school. You can bring your own device or borrow one. It has two sections:

| Section | Modules | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reading and Writing | 2 modules of 27 questions | 54 | 64 minutes (32 + 32) |
| *Break* | | | 10 minutes |
| Math | 2 modules of 22 questions | 44 | 70 minutes (35 + 35) |
| **Total** | **4 modules** | **98** | **2 hours 14 minutes of testing** |

A few questions in each section are being tried out for future tests and do not count, but you cannot tell which ones, so treat every question as real.

**Try it:** Press Play to run the clock and set your own seconds per question.

## 2. Adaptive by module: what that means

Older tests gave everyone the same questions. The digital SAT is **multistage adaptive**: the first module of each section has a mix of easy, medium and hard questions. When it ends, the app checks how you did and picks your second module:

- did well → a **harder** second module, with more difficult questions that are worth more on the score scale;
- found it tough → an **easier** second module.

<div class="steps" data-fig="sat-map">
<div class="step">You start with Reading and Writing, Module 1: 27 questions in 32 minutes, a mix of every difficulty.</div>
<div class="step">When the timer ends, the app scores Module 1 and chooses which version of Module 2 you get.</div>
<div class="step">A strong Module 1 sends you to the <b>harder</b> Module 2. Harder questions are the path to scores in the high 600s and 700s.</div>
<div class="step">Then a 10-minute break. Stand up, drink water, eat a snack.</div>
<div class="step">Math works the same way: Module 1 (22 questions, 35 minutes) decides which Math Module 2 you see.</div>
<div class="step">Four modules, 98 questions, about 2 hours 14 minutes. Your score comes from both modules of each section together.</div>
</div>

<div class="myth">

**"If my second module feels hard, I must be failing."** It is the opposite. A hard Module 2 means you did well on Module 1. Students who get the harder module and miss several questions often still score higher than students who ace the easier module.

</div>

## 3. What each section tests

**Reading and Writing** gives you many short passages (from one sentence to about 150 words), each with **one** question. The topics are literature, history and social studies, the humanities and science. Questions come in four groups, in this order inside each module: Craft and Structure, Information and Ideas, Standard English Conventions, Expression of Ideas. Part C of this course has a lesson for every question type.

**Math** covers four areas: Algebra (about 35%), Advanced Math (about 35%), Problem-Solving and Data Analysis (about 15%) and Geometry and Trigonometry (about 15%). About three quarters of the questions are multiple choice with four options; the rest ask you to type your own answer. A graphing calculator (Desmos) is built into the app for the whole Math section, and you may also bring an approved calculator. A reference sheet of formulas is one click away.

## 4. Tools inside the app

- **Mark for review**: flag a question and jump back to it from the question list.
- **Answer eliminator**: cross out options you have ruled out. Use it on every hard question.
- **Timer**: can be hidden, and it warns you when 5 minutes are left.
- **Highlights and notes** in Reading and Writing.
- **Desmos calculator** and **reference sheet** in Math.

<div class="keypoints">

- Two sections (Reading and Writing, then Math), each in two modules: 98 questions, about 2 hours 14 minutes, plus a 10-minute break.
- Your first module in each section decides whether your second is harder or easier; the harder one leads to top scores.
- There is no penalty for wrong answers: answer every question.
- Desmos and a formula sheet are built in for all of Math; Reading and Writing has one short passage per question.
