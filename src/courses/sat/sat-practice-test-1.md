**Module 10 · about 80 minutes · read first: [1.4 A study plan](/learn/courses/sat/sat-study-plan/) and every lesson you have studied so far.** By the end you will know your pace in each section and which topics to review first.

<div class="bigidea">A practice test is a measurement, not a lesson. Take it under real conditions (timer on, no notes, Desmos allowed in Math), then spend as long reviewing as you spent testing. The review is where the score goes up.</div>

## 1. Before you start

<div class="steps">
<div class="step">Find 80 quiet minutes. Put your phone in another room.</div>
<div class="step">Have scrap paper and a pencil. For Math, open the <a href="/learn/courses/sat/desmos-calculator/">Desmos calculator</a> in another tab, as you would in the real test app.</div>
<div class="step">Pacing: about 70 seconds per Reading and Writing question, about 95 seconds per Math question. Check the clock at the halfway point.</div>
<div class="step">Answer every question. Mark the ones you guessed so you can review them even if you got them right.</div>
</div>

<div class="meaning">

<dl>
<dt>Module</dt><dd>one timed block of the digital SAT; each section has two, and the second adapts to how you did on the first</dd>
<dt>This test</dt><dd>gives you one module of each, not adaptive, drawn fresh from the fibub bank every time</dd>
<dt>Physical meaning</dt><dd>Your pace is a rate, like any other: questions per minute. If you finish with 8 minutes left, you can afford more checking; if you run out, you need faster first passes.</dd>

## 4. After the test

Write down, for each question you missed or guessed: the topic, why you missed it (did not know the rule, misread, ran out of time, careless slip), and the lesson to reread. Then go to [10.3 Mistake review](/learn/courses/sat/sat-mistake-review/).

<details class="try"><summary>How do I turn my score into an SAT score?</summary>

You cannot exactly: the real SAT scores each section from 200 to 800 using question difficulty and the adaptive second module. Use your percent right and your topic list to decide what to study, and use the official practice tests in the College Board's Bluebook app for score estimates.

</details>

<div class="keypoints">

- Test under real conditions; review for at least as long as you tested.
- R&W: 27 questions in 32 minutes. Math: 22 questions in 35 minutes.
- Answer every question; mark guesses for review.
- Sort misses by cause: knowledge, misreading, time or slip.
