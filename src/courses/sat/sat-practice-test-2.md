**Module 10 · about 2½ hours · read first: [10.1 Practice test 1](/learn/courses/sat/sat-practice-test-1/).** By the end you will have sat a whole SAT-length test and know how your focus holds up in the second hour.

<div class="bigidea">The full SAT is a stamina test as much as a knowledge test. Take this once you have studied most lessons, in the morning if you can, with the 10-minute break between sections and nothing else.</div>

## 1. The plan

<div class="steps">
<div class="step">Reading and Writing: 54 questions in 64 minutes (both modules together here). Check the clock at question 27: about 32 minutes should be gone.</div>
<div class="step">Break: 10 minutes. Stand up, drink water, eat a snack. Do not look at your answers.</div>
<div class="step">Math: 44 questions in 70 minutes. Desmos open. Check the clock at question 22: about 35 minutes gone.</div>
<div class="step">Afterwards, mark every miss and guess, and use [10.3 Mistake review](/learn/courses/sat/sat-mistake-review/).</div>
