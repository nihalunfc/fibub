**Module 8 · about 12 minutes · read first: [7.2 Text structure](/learn/courses/sat/text-structure-purpose/).** By the end you will name the relationship between two sentences and pick its word.

<div class="bigidea">A transition is a signpost for the relationship between two ideas. Decide the relationship first, without looking at the options, then choose the word from that family.</div>

## 1. The five families

| Relationship | Words |
|---|---|
| Contrast or correction | however, but, in contrast, nevertheless, in reality, instead |
| Cause and effect | therefore, as a result, consequently, thus |
| Example | for example, for instance, specifically |
| Addition | moreover, in addition, furthermore, similarly |
| Sequence | first, next, then, finally, meanwhile |

<div class="steps">
<div class="step">"Polar bears look white, and most people assume their fur is white." Then "each hair is clear and hollow."</div>
<div class="step">Relationship: the second sentence corrects the first. Family: contrast or correction.</div>
<div class="step">Options: "In reality", "Similarly", "For example", "As a result". Only "In reality" is in the family.</div>
</div>

<div class="meaning">

<dl>
<dt>Transition</dt><dd>a word or phrase that tells the reader how the next idea connects to the last</dd>
<dt>Physical meaning</dt><dd>Without transitions, readers have to guess the connection. With the wrong one, they are actively misled, which is why the SAT tests it.</dd>
</dl>

</div>

<details class="try"><summary>Try it: "The factory switched to solar power. ______ its energy bill fell by half."</summary>

Cause and effect: "As a result,".

</details>

<div class="myth">

**"However" sounds better, so use it.** It is right only for a contrast. When the two ideas agree, a contrast word is simply wrong.

</div>
