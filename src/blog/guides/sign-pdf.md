Printing a form just to sign it, then scanning it back in, wastes paper and time. The [[sign-pdf]] tool lets you make a signature on screen and stamp it onto any page of a PDF, along with your initials, a date or a short note, all inside your browser. It is handy for tenancy papers, school consent forms, job offer letters and supplier agreements. Type into the form boxes first with [[fill-pdf]], put the pages in the right order with [[organise-pdf]], and if the signed file is too large to email, shrink it with [[compress-file]].

## What it is

There are two very different things people call a "digital signature".

- A **simple electronic signature** is a mark that shows you agreed, such as a picture of your handwriting placed on the page. It is the on-screen version of signing a printout. This is what the tool makes.
- A **certificate-based signature** uses a digital ID issued by a trusted provider. It locks the file and records who signed and when. This tool does not make that kind; if a form asks for it, the sender will name the service to use.

The tool builds your signature as a picture with a see-through background, cut tightly around the ink. When you save, it is drawn into the page itself (this is called **flattening**), so it is no longer a loose layer that slides around when someone opens the file.

Sizes on the page are worked out as fractions of the page. A new signature starts at 30% of the page width and new initials at 10%, keeping the shape of what you made. You can then resize them by hand.

## How to use it

1. Press **Choose a PDF to sign**, or drop the file on the box. Each page appears below as a preview.
2. Make your signature on one of three tabs: **Draw** on the pad (press **Clear** to start over), **Type** your name and pick a writing style, or **Upload picture** of a signature on paper. On the upload tab, **Make the white background clear** removes the paper colour.
3. Check the **Your initials** box. When you type your name, it fills itself with the first letters; you can overwrite it.
4. Press **Add signature**, then tap the spot on the page. Drag the dashed box to move it, pull its square corner to change its size, or press its **x** to remove it.
5. Do the same with **Add initials**, **Add date** and **Add text**. The **Date text** box starts with today's date as year-month-day, and **Other text** takes anything short, such as "Approved". Tick **Put my initials on every page** to copy your first initials to the same spot on every other page.
6. Press **Download signed PDF**. The file keeps its name with "-signed" added. Open it and check every page before you send it.

## A worked example

Sam has a four-page A4 tenancy agreement. The landlord wants initials on every page and a full signature with the date on page 4.

**Make the marks.** Sam types "Sam K Okafor" on the Type tab and picks a style. The initials box fills itself with **SKO**.

**Size on the page.** A4 is 210 mm wide. The new signature starts at 30% of that: 0.30 × 210 mm = **63 mm** wide. The initials start at 0.10 × 210 mm = **21 mm** wide. Sam drags the signature corner until it fits the line.

**Place them.** Sam presses **Add signature** and taps the line on page 4, then **Add date** and taps beside it. The date reads 2026-11-10; Sam changes it to "10 November 2026". Then **Add initials**, a tap in the bottom corner of page 1, and a tick in **Put my initials on every page**.

**Count the marks.** Sam placed 3 marks: signature, date and one set of initials. The tick copies the initials onto the other 3 pages, so the file gets 3 + 3 = **6 marks on 4 pages**, and the message after saving says so.

## Where it is used

- **Renting and housing.** Leases, inventories and move-out forms often need initials on each page.
- **Schools.** Permission slips and trip forms can be signed on a phone in a minute.
- **Freelancers and small firms.** Quotes, purchase orders and simple contracts can be signed and returned the same day.
- **Human resources.** Offer letters and policy acknowledgements are often a signature and a date at the bottom.
- **Approvals.** The **Add text** button stamps a word such as "Approved" or "Received" with your initials on invoices and delivery notes.

## Common mistakes

- **Signing before filling in the form.** Fill the fields first, then sign, so nothing is added under your signature later.
- **Uploading a dark or shadowed photo.** The clear-background setting removes only pale paper. Sign in black ink on white paper, in good light, for a clean result.
- **Forgetting the every-page box copies only the first initials.** Place one set on page 1 where you want them on all pages, then tick the box.
- **Sending without checking.** Open the downloaded file and scroll through every page; a mark can end up on the wrong page with a stray tap.
- **Using it where a certificate is required.** Some government, bank and property forms ask for a certified digital ID. Check the official source for your document.

## Questions people ask

**Can I sign on a phone?** Yes. Draw with your finger on the pad, then tap the page to place it. A stylus gives a smoother line.

**Can I fix a mistake after I download?** Not in the saved file, because the marks are flattened. Open your original PDF again and redo the signing.

**Is a typed name really a signature?** It can be, for many everyday papers, because what counts is that you meant to agree. For important documents, check the official source for the rules where you live.
