A good week plan is one you can see at a glance. The [[planner]] is built for that: you type what you need to do, the page reads the day and the time from your words, and the week lays itself out in columns. For a team's shifts, a [[shift-scheduler]] is the better fit. For dates that come from money, such as rent or a card payment, put them on the [[bill-calendar]].

## What it is

The planner is a week-and-month calendar with one special feature: the quick-add box. Instead of clicking through a form, you type a short line and press Enter. The planner looks for two things in that line:

- **A time**, such as `7am`, `14:00`, or a range such as `9-9:15am`.
- **A day**, such as `mon`, `fri`, or words such as `today` and `tomorrow`.

Whatever is left becomes the name of the plan. So `Gym 7am tue` becomes "Gym" at 7:00 am on Tuesday of the week you are looking at.

Plans in a day are sorted by time. Plans without a time, such as "Pay rent", sit at the top of the day. Each plan can have a colour, which helps you spot work among personal plans at a glance.

The planner keeps your plans in the browser's own storage on the device you use, so there is no sign-in. The price of that is that another phone or laptop starts empty, and wiping the browser's site data erases the plans.

## How to use it

1. Click in the quick-add box and type a plan, such as `Team call 10-11am thu`. Press Enter.
2. Check that the plan landed on the right day. If it did not, click it and change the day, the time or the text.
3. Use **Week** for the seven-day view, or **Month** for the whole month. On a phone the week stacks one day under another.
4. Tap the small circle in front of a plan when you have done it. The plan stays on the page, but is marked as done.
5. Open **Settings** to set a title for the download, such as "Exam week", and choose whether weeks start on Monday, Sunday or Saturday.
6. Press **Download image** for a PNG, or **Download PDF** for an A4 landscape page.

## A worked example

Mei is a student with a heavy exam week. She types these lines one after another, pressing Enter after each:

- `Revision 4-6pm mon` (2 hours)
- `Revision 4-6pm tue` (2 hours)
- `Revision 4-6pm wed` (2 hours)
- `Study group 7-8:30pm wed` (1.5 hours)
- `Maths paper 9-12pm fri` (3 hours, read as 9 am to 12 pm)
- `Pay rent mon` (no time, so it sits at the top of Monday)

Adding up the timed plans gives 2 + 2 + 2 + 1.5 + 3 = 10.5 hours of study in the week. Mei colours the maths paper red so it stands out, sets the title to "Exam week", and starts the week on Monday. She presses **Download image** and sends the PNG to her study group. Later in the week she ticks off each revision session as she finishes it.

## Where it is used

- **Students** planning revision, practicals and deadlines around a timetable.
- **Families** sharing one printed week on the fridge, using the PDF.
- **Freelancers and small teams** who want a simple view of meetings and deliverables without a paid calendar.
- **Volunteers** and clubs that send a weekly list of sessions to members as a picture.
- **Anyone building a routine**, who can see how many gym or study sessions fit into a week before committing to them.

## Common mistakes

1. **Typing a plan without a day.** It may land on a day you did not mean. Check the column after each entry.
2. **Trusting one copy only.** Plans live in one browser. Download a PNG or PDF of anything you need to keep, especially before clearing browser data.
3. **Expecting it to sync.** A plan added on your laptop will not appear on your phone. Use the download to move it across.
4. **Using a shared computer.** Anyone who uses the same browser can see your plans. Open **Settings** and press **Clear this week** before you leave a shared device.
5. **Putting too much in one day.** Long lists are hard to read. Use the colours and the time sort to split a crowded day, or move a few plans to another day.

## Questions people ask

**What does a range like `11-1pm` mean?** The planner reads it as 11 am to 1 pm, because 11 pm to 1 pm would run backwards. If you want 9 pm to 11 pm, write `9-11pm`. Always type am or pm on a range, since a bare `9-12` is not read as a time.

**Can I put a plan on a day outside the week I am looking at?** Day words such as `fri` choose a day in the week on screen. To reach another week, use the arrows first, or click **+ Add** under a day to use the full form with its date field.

**How do I empty a week?** Open **Settings** and press **Clear this week**. Download the week first if you may want it back.
