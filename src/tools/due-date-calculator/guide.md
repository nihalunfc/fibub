## What it does

This calculator estimates when a baby is due. It works from the first day of your last period, from a known conception date, or from an IVF embryo transfer date. It also shows how many weeks and days pregnant you are on a chosen date, which trimester that is, and the dates when the main weeks begin, such as the usual scan weeks and full term.

It is an estimate, not medical advice. Your dating scan is more accurate, so go by the scan when your care team gives you a date. To plan earlier, the [[ovulation-calendar]] estimates fertile days, and the [[sleep-calculator]] helps with bedtimes.

## How to use it

1. Choose what you know in **Work it out from**.
2. Enter that date. For a period date, set your usual cycle length.
3. Read the due date, the week count and the table of key weeks.

## What each setting does

- **Work it out from**: last period (Naegele's rule, +280 days), conception (+266 days), or IVF transfer of a day-3 (+263) or day-5 (+261) embryo.
- **Usual cycle length**: only for a period date. Each day longer than 28 moves the due date one day later, each day shorter one day earlier. Lengths outside 20 to 45 days fall back to 28.
- **Today's date**: filled in from your device; change it to see the week count on another day.
- **Reset**: back to the example.

Weeks are always counted from two weeks before conception, the way doctors and midwives count them, whichever method you use.

## Worked example

The last period started on 1 January 2026 and cycles are 28 days. 280 days later is Thursday 8 October 2026. With a 35-day cycle, ovulation is about a week later, so the due date moves to 15 October 2026. For a day-5 embryo transferred on 15 January 2026, the due date is 15 January + 261 days = 3 October 2026. On 1 March 2026, the first example is 8 weeks 3 days, first trimester.

## FAQs

**How exact is the due date?** Only about 1 baby in 25 is born on it. Most arrive within two weeks either side.

**Why do weeks start before conception?** Pregnancy is counted from the last period, which is about two weeks before ovulation.

**My scan gave a different date. Which is right?** Use the scan date.
