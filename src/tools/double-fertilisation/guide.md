## What it does

Flowering plants do something no other living thing does: each pollen tube delivers **two** male gametes, and **both** fuse with something. One fuses with the egg to make the zygote (2n); the other fuses with two polar nuclei to make the primary endosperm nucleus (3n). This page walks through it in eleven steps inside one ovule:

1. The **megaspore mother cell** (2n).
2. **Meiosis** gives four megaspores (n).
3. Three break down; the **functional megaspore** remains.
4. to 6. **Three mitoses** without walls: 2, then 4, then 8 free nuclei.
7. Walls form: the **embryo sac**, with 7 cells and 8 nuclei (2 synergids, the egg, 3 antipodals and a central cell with 2 polar nuclei).
8. The **pollen tube** grows down the style and in through the micropyle.
9. It bursts into a synergid and releases **two male gametes**.
10. **Syngamy** and **triple fusion**: double fertilisation.
11. The endosperm and the embryo grow; the ovule becomes a seed.

Each step animates, and the **ploidy counter** lists every cell present with its ploidy and its chromosome number for the plant you choose. It fits **Class 12 Biology: sexual reproduction in flowering plants** (megasporogenesis, the female gametophyte, pollination, double fertilisation, endosperm and embryo). For meiosis itself see [[cell-division]], and to follow what the zygote's genes do in the next generation try [[mendel-peas]].

## Worked example

Choose **maize, 2n = 20**, so n = 10. The megaspore mother cell has 20 chromosomes; after meiosis each megaspore has 10. Three mitoses keep that number, so the egg, the synergids, the antipodals and each polar nucleus all have 10. At step 10 the table shows the **zygote: 10 + 10 = 20** and the **primary endosperm nucleus: 10 + 10 + 10 = 30**. The seed coat comes from the mother's integuments, so it has 20.

How many meiotic divisions make **100 maize grains**? Each grain needs one ovule, and each ovule's embryo sac comes from one meiosis: 100. Each also needs one pollen grain, and one meiosis makes four pollen grains: 100 ÷ 4 = 25. Total **125**.

## Tips

- "7 cells, 8 nuclei" is the most asked fact: the central cell is one cell with two nuclei.
- Synergids carry the filiform apparatus that guides the pollen tube in.
- In most plants the endosperm starts dividing before the zygote: the embryo needs food first.

## FAQ

**Is the ovule really straight?** Most are bent (anatropous), with the micropyle near the stalk. It is drawn straight here so the steps are easy to follow.

**Do all plants have this kind of embryo sac?** About 70 % of flowering plants do (the Polygonum type). Others differ in detail.

**Is this a botanical reference?** No. It is a teaching model of the main steps.
