## What it does

Type a title, a line of thanks and the names, pick one of three plain layouts and download a landscape PDF with one certificate on each page. It suits classes, clubs, courses, sports days and workplaces.

Your names, text and logo stay on your device. To change the wording per person from a spreadsheet, see [[mail-merge]]; to join certificates with other pages or pull a few out, use [[organise-pdf]]; for billing paperwork there is [[invoice-maker]].

## How to use it

1. Type the names, one per line, or open a CSV file. The first column is used and a heading called "Name" is skipped.
2. Write the title, the line above the name and the text underneath.
3. Pick a layout, a colour and the paper (A4 or Letter). Add a logo if you like.
4. Download the PDF. Print on plain or heavy paper.

## What each setting does

- **Classic**: a double border and a serif font.
- **Modern**: a coloured band at the top and a thin one at the bottom.
- **Simple**: two thin lines and a clean sans-serif font.
- **Second signature line**: adds a second place to sign. Leave it empty for one.
- **Logo**: placed at the top, scaled to fit.

## Worked example

Three names (Asha Rao, Ben Lee, Chidi Okafor) give a three-page PDF, one certificate per person. Long names are made smaller so they fit on one line.

## FAQs

**Can I leave the name blank to write by hand?** Yes. With no names you get one page with an empty name line.

**Why do some letters turn into question marks?** The PDF uses standard fonts, which cover Latin letters and accents but not other scripts.

**Is it stored anywhere?** No. Nothing is uploaded or kept.
