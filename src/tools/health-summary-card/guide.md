## What it does

A new doctor, a hospital desk or a paramedic often asks the same questions: what are you allergic to, what do you take, who should we call? This tool collects your answers in one place and turns them into three things you can keep: a one-page summary, a folding wallet card, and a picture for your phone's lock screen.

It only repeats what you type. It does not check medicines, suggest anything or say what a condition means. Keep your answers as they appear on your own records, and use [[medical-terms|the medical words explainer]] for abbreviations on your papers and [[health-log]] to keep readings for your doctor.

## How to use it

1. Fill in your details. Add a row with **+ Add a row** and remove one with the × button. Blank rows are ignored.
2. Check the preview that appears under the form.
3. Download the summary (image, PDF or Word), the wallet card PDF, or the lock-screen PNG.
4. Print the card on plain paper or card, cut along the outline, and fold on the dotted line.

## What each option does

- **Blood group** is optional. Leave it as "Not given" if you do not know it.
- **Medicines** has two boxes: the name as on your label, and how you take it in your own words.
- **Keep what I type on this device** is off by default. When ticked, the form is kept in this browser only. **Clear everything** empties the form and deletes the kept copy.
- Every output prints "Made on [date]. Check it is up to date." so a reader can see how old it is.

## Worked example

Asha Example (made up) types two allergies, two conditions, two medicines and two emergency contacts. The summary lists each in a table with her date of birth. The card shows allergies, conditions and name on the front, and contacts and medicines on the back. The phone picture shows allergies, conditions and contacts in large white text on black.

## FAQs

**Is my health information uploaded?** No. Everything is built in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

**Will the card hold everything?** The card is small. If a list is too long it ends with "more on the summary page", so print the summary too.

**How often should I update it?** Whenever something changes. The date on each output shows when it was made.
