## What it does

The hard part of a word problem is not the solving, it is turning the story into equations. This page takes one story at a time and shows the four steps:

1. **Name the unknowns**: what x and y stand for.
2. **Write two equations**: each sentence of the story that gives a fact becomes one equation, with a line saying how.
3. **Solve** by **elimination** or by **substitution**, every step with the story's numbers.
4. **Answer and check**: put the answer back into the story, not just into the equations.

There are five kinds of story: **ages** (now and in a few years), **tickets** (two groups paying for adult and child tickets), a **boat in a stream** (downstream the speeds add, upstream they subtract), a **rectangle** (length, width and perimeter) and **banknotes** (how many of each). Press **New numbers** for a fresh story of the same kind. The second card draws both equations as lines; once all the steps are shown, the point where they cross is marked: it is the answer.

It follows the Class 10 Maths chapter *pair of linear equations in two variables* (algebraic methods and word problems).

To solve any pair you type in yourself and see the ratio test, use [[linear-pair-solver]]. Stories whose equations have a square in them lead on to [[quadratic-explorer]].

## How to use it

1. **Pick a story** with the buttons and read it.
2. Try it on paper first: decide what x and y are and write two equations.
3. Choose **Elimination** or **Substitution**.
4. Press **Next step** to reveal one step at a time and compare with your working, or **Show all**. **Hide steps** starts again.
5. Look at the graph: the two lines and, at the end, the crossing point with its coordinates.

## Worked example

A boat goes 45 km downstream in 3 hours and 27 km upstream in 3 hours. Let x be the speed of the boat in still water and y the speed of the stream, in km/h.

- Downstream the stream helps: x + y = 45 ÷ 3 = 15. … (1)
- Upstream it holds the boat back: x − y = 27 ÷ 3 = 9. … (2)
- Elimination: add or subtract the equations. Subtracting (2) from (1) gives 2y = 6, so y = 3, and then x = 12.
- Check: downstream 12 + 3 = 15 km/h for 3 hours is 45 km; upstream 12 − 3 = 9 km/h for 3 hours is 27 km. ✓

## Tips

- Write what x and y stand for, with units, before anything else.
- Ages: "in n years" adds n to **both** ages. "n years ago" takes n from both.
- Boats and streams: downstream speed = boat + stream, upstream speed = boat − stream. The same works for planes and wind.
- Always check the answer in the words of the story. A negative age or price means an equation was set up wrongly.

## FAQ

**Which method should I use?** Either works. Substitution is quick when one equation already says x = … or y = …; elimination is quick when the x or y numbers already match.

**Why does my equation look different from the page's?** It may be the same equation rearranged or multiplied through. If both your equations give the same answer, they are fine.

**Is this from the textbook?** No. The stories and numbers are made up for practice and follow the syllabus topics; fibub is not part of NCERT.

**Is anything saved?** No. Everything is worked out in your browser and nothing is stored or sent.
