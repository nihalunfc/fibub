## What it does

Insurance wording is long, and the costly surprises sit in a few phrases: waiting periods, caps, co-payments, exclusions. This tool scans text you paste (or a .txt or text PDF you open), highlights those clauses in colour, and lists the numbers inside them, such as 36 months or 1%.

Your text stays on this page: it is never uploaded or stored. The tool finds words only and cannot tell you what a clause means for you. Look up unfamiliar words in [[insurance-terms]], test whether a price is fair with [[should-i-insure]].

## How to use it

1. Paste the wording into the box, or choose a file.
2. Read the highlighted text. The colour key shows each group.
3. Open the list of clauses found: each has why it matters and a question to ask the insurer.
4. Use **Copy summary** to take the checklist to a call or email.

## What it looks for

Waiting period, pre-existing conditions, moratorium, sub-limits, room rent, co-payment, deductible or excess, exclusions, proportionate deduction, average or underinsurance, depreciation, free look, grace period, cancellation and refund, claim notice deadlines, network or cashless rules, and renewal or age limits.

## Worked example

"Pre-existing diseases are covered after a waiting period of 36 months. Room rent is limited to 1% of the sum insured per day." The tool finds three groups (waiting period, pre-existing, room rent) and lists 36 months and 1%. On a sum insured of 500,000, 1% is 5,000 a day.

## FAQs

**Does it read PDFs that are scans?** No, only PDFs that contain real text.

**Does it find everything?** No. A clause may use different words. Read the whole policy.

**Is it advice?** No. Ask your insurer or an adviser if a clause is unclear.
