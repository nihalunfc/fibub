## What it does

Putting out music takes more steps than pressing upload. This page takes your release date and works backwards, giving each step a date: master ready, cover artwork, ISRC and UPC codes, metadata, split sheet, upload to a distributor, pitching, pre-save link, promotion, release day and the weeks after.

Tick items off, and watch the bar. Change any date if your distributor needs more or less time. Get the files ready with the [[loudness-normaliser]] and the [[music-tag-editor]], agree who owns what with the [[split-sheet-maker]], and see what you may earn with [[streaming-royalties]].

## How to use it

1. Pick the **release date** and, if you like, a name.
2. Read the list from the earliest step down. Tick items as you finish them.
3. Change a step's date with its date box.
4. Press **Download calendar (.ics)** to add every step to a calendar app, **Download CSV** for a spreadsheet, or **Print**.

## What each part does

- **Reset list**: puts back the starting dates and removes ticks.
- **Remember my checklist**: off by default. When ticked, the list is kept in this browser only. **Clear saved checklist** deletes it.
- Calendar events are all-day and use your release name as a prefix.

## Worked example

For a release on **2026-12-01**, the master is due **42 days before: 2026-10-20**. The editorial pitch is due 14 days before, **2026-11-17**, which is more than the 7-day minimum. The last item, a look at your stats, is 30 days after: **2026-12-31**.

## FAQs

**Are these dates rules?** No, only a plan. Check your distributor's lead time.

**What is an ISRC?** A code that identifies one recording. A UPC or EAN identifies the whole release.

**Is my plan uploaded?** No. Everything stays on your device.
