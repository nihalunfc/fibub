## What it does

Paste text and press a button to see it in another case. You can fix a heading typed in capitals by mistake, turn a title into Title Case, make sentences start with capital letters, or turn a phrase into the style programmers use for names: camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, CONSTANT_CASE and kebab-case. The result appears under the box and you copy it with one press.

Your text never leaves your device. If you need to count the result, open [[word-counter]], and to remove stray spaces and line breaks first use [[text-cleaner]].

## How to use it

1. Type or paste your text.
2. Press the case you want. The result shows below.
3. Press **Copy the result**, or **Use result as my text** to keep working on it, for example to try another case on top.

## What each case does

- **UPPER CASE** and **lower case**: every letter big or small.
- **Title Case**: each word starts with a capital, except small words (a, an, and, of, the, to ...) in the middle. The first and last word and the word after a colon always get a capital.
- **Sentence case**: the first letter of each sentence is a capital and the rest small. The word "I" is kept as a capital.
- **camelCase** and **PascalCase**: words joined with no spaces, with capitals marking each new word. camelCase starts small, PascalCase starts big.
- **snake_case**, **CONSTANT_CASE**, **kebab-case**: words joined by an underscore or hyphen.
- **aLtErNaTe**: letters switch between small and big, just for fun.

## Worked example

"the lord of the rings: a tale" becomes "The Lord of the Rings: A Tale" in Title Case, "the_lord_of_the_rings_a_tale" in snake_case, and "theLordOfTheRingsATale" in camelCase.

## FAQs

**Does it handle names like McDonald?** Title Case writes the rest of each word in small letters, so you may need to fix such names by hand.

**Does it work for other languages?** Yes for scripts that have capitals, such as accented European letters. Scripts without capitals, such as Hindi or Arabic, are left as they are.

**Is anything sent to a server?** No.
