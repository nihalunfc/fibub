## What it does

Add the people who share a home and the bills you share, and pick how each bill is split: equally, by a share you agreed, by room size, or by income. The table shows what each person owes on every bill and their total for the month, and you can download it as a CSV to send around.

Different bills can use different rules, for example rent by room size and internet equally. For a one-off meal or a holiday, try the [[tip-calculator]] or the [[trip-splitter]]; to plan the whole month, the [[budget-calculator|budget planner]] and the [[bill-calendar]] help.

## How to use it

1. Type each person's name. Fill in the column your rules need: **Share %**, **Room size** or **Income**.
2. Add each bill with its amount and choose how to split it.
3. Read the table. Tick **Remember** only if you want the list kept for next month.

## What each option does

- **Equally**: everyone pays the same.
- **By share %**: each person pays their share of the total of all shares, so the numbers need not add to 100.
- **By room size**: rent in proportion to the size of each person's room. Use any unit, as long as it is the same for everyone.
- **By income**: pays in proportion to monthly income, so the person who earns more pays more.

## Worked example

Rent is 2,000. Sam has a room of 12 square metres and Alex 8. Split by room size, Sam pays 1,200 and Alex pays 800. Internet of 60 split equally is 30 each, so Sam's month is 1,230 and Alex's is 830.

## FAQs

**What about odd cents?** 100 between three people is 33.34, 33.33 and 33.33. The shares always add up to the exact bill.

**Is my list saved?** Not unless you tick the box. If you do, it stays in this browser on this device and the Clear button removes it.

**Who paid the bill?** This tool only works out each person's share. To track who paid what on a trip and settle up, use the [[trip-splitter]]. For percentage sums, the [[percentage-calculator]] helps.
