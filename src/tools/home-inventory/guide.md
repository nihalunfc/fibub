## What it does

A written list of your belongings, with what each would cost to replace, makes a claim faster and shows whether your contents cover is big enough. Add items room by room, with the year bought, the price paid, today's replacement cost and an optional photo. The tool totals each room, shows the replacement total beside a worn-down (depreciated) value, and compares the total with your contents sum insured.

Photos stay on your device and are never uploaded. If you find a gap, work out what a claim would pay with [[underinsurance-check]]; for the words in your policy, see [[insurance-terms]].

## How to use it

1. Add a row for each item: room, item, quantity, year bought, price paid, replacement cost of one today.
2. Type the years of life you expect from the item if you want a depreciated value (leave 0 for none).
3. Add a photo if you like, then type your contents sum insured.
4. Download the CSV or print the page. The numbers shown are examples: change them to yours.

## What each setting does

- **Replacement cost**: buying the item new today. Policies paying this are "replacement cost" policies.
- **Years of life**: straight-line wear: an item of 10 years bought 5 years ago is valued at half.
- **Keep this list in this browser**: off by default; saves the text of your list on this device only, never photos.

## Worked example

Two items with replacement cost 1,000 and 500 give a total of 1,500. With a contents sum insured of 1,000 the shortfall is 500.

## FAQs

**Are my photos saved?** No. Photos are not saved to this browser's storage; they disappear when you leave or reload, so keep your own copies.

**Is the list uploaded?** No, nothing leaves this page. The saved copy is only there if you tick the box, and the Clear button removes it.

**Which value will my insurer pay?** It depends on your policy: replacement cost or actual cash value.

How it's worked out: replacement total = sum of quantity × replacement cost; depreciated value = replacement × (1 − age ÷ life), never below 0; shortfall = total − sum insured.
