## What it does

Watermark PDF puts words across your pages, such as DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL or COPY, or your own text. You choose the size, angle, colour and how see-through it is. Or choose a stamp (Received, Approved, Paid) with today's date, placed in a corner inside a box.

It works on all pages or only the ones you list, like `1-3,5`. It also works on JPG and PNG pictures, which suits a copy of an ID marked "Copy for bank use only". Nothing is uploaded or kept. When you are done, add numbers with [[page-numbers]] or reorder pages with [[organise-pdf]].

## How to use it

1. Choose a PDF or picture.
2. Pick **Text across the page** or **Stamp with date**.
3. Set the words, size, angle, opacity and colour. Pictures show a live preview.
4. For a PDF, type page ranges to mark only some pages, or leave it blank for all.
5. Press **Add watermark and download**.

## What each setting does

- **Opacity**: 100 is solid; 20 to 30 lets the page show through.
- **Angle**: 45 runs from bottom left to top right; 0 is flat.
- **Size**: for PDFs it is in points; on pictures it scales with the picture width.
- **Stamp corner**: where the dated box goes.

## Worked example

You are sending a 3-page quote for review. Choose Text, type DRAFT, size 90, angle 45, opacity 25. The saved PDF still has 3 pages and each shows a faint red DRAFT across the middle.

## FAQs

**Can someone remove the watermark?** On a PDF it is drawn on the page and is not a security feature; a determined person can edit it out. For ID copies, a visible mark with who and why discourages misuse.

**Which letters work?** Latin letters, numbers and common symbols.

**Does it keep my picture?** No. The picture is marked in your browser and saved to your device only.
