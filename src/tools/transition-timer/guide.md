## What it does

A transition is the moment one track hands over to the next. Like any skill it improves with repetition, and it helps to practise against the clock. This timer counts down a whole transition at your BPM and splits it into phrase-length steps, so you know when to bring in the bass, swap the EQ or cut. You do the mixing on your own gear; the timer is only the stopwatch.

After each try you rate it clean, ok or trainwreck, add a note if you like, and the tool keeps a session list with your average, best streak of clean runs and a chart. Know your phrase lengths first with the [[phrase-counter]], and when you play a real set log the crowd with the [[crowd-energy-log]].

## How to use it

1. Pick a **Drill** and type the **BPM** of the tracks you are mixing.
2. Press **Start** as the old track reaches a phrase start. The big number is the time left and the step.
3. When it ends, press **Clean**, **OK** or **Trainwreck**. Add a note first if you want to.
4. Press **Download CSV** to keep the list.

## What each setting does

- **Drill**: sets the number of bars. Beatmatch by ear 8, 16-bar blend 16, 32-bar EQ swap 32, quick cut 2, echo out 8. **Custom** uses your own count.
- **Bars** and **Step**: total length and the size of each step (4, 8 or 16 bars).
- **Remember this session on this device**: off by default. When ticked, the list is kept in this browser only. **Clear** wipes it.

## Worked example

32-bar EQ swap at 128 BPM: one bar is 1.875 s, so the drill lasts 32 x 1.875 = 60 s. With 8-bar steps there are four steps of 15 s. If your last three attempts are clean, clean, ok, the average is (2 + 2 + 1) / 3 = 1.67 of 2, shown as 83%, and the best streak is 2.

## FAQs

**Does it play music?** No. It only counts. Start it on a phrase start of your own track.

**What is the score?** Clean counts 2, ok 1, trainwreck 0, shown as a percentage of the best possible.

**Where is my data?** Only in this page unless you tick the remember box, and then only in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.
