## What it does

Walk around a used car, two-wheeler or truck with this list on your phone or on paper. Each item can be marked OK, Problem or Not checked, with a note. The tool shows a score and a list of red flags, and you can print the list or save it as a PDF.

Check the number with [[vin-check]] first, work out what you can afford with [[car-loan]], and see what it may lose in value with [[depreciation]].

## How to use it

1. Choose Car, Two-wheeler or Truck. The list changes to fit.
2. Go through the items in order. Mark each one and add a note where something looks wrong.
3. Read the score and the red flags at the bottom.
4. Print, or download the PDF to keep with the seller's quote.

## What each setting does

- **OK / Problem / Not checked**: only OK and Problem count in the score. Not checked is left out, so you can skip what you cannot test.
- **Red flag items**: marked on the list. A Problem on one of them (odometer doubts, flood signs, frame damage, papers mismatch) shows in the red flag list whatever the score is.
- **Clear all**: wipes every mark and note. Nothing is saved on your device or sent anywhere.

## Worked example

You mark 1 item OK and 3 Problem, one of them a damp smell under the carpet. The score is 25% OK, and "Flood signs" appears as a red flag. Even with a decent score, one red flag is a reason to walk away or get an expert opinion.

## FAQs

**Is a high score a green light?** No. It only shows what you noticed. Pay a mechanic to inspect any vehicle you plan to buy, especially if there is a red flag.

**Why are the red flags separate?** Odometer tampering, flood damage, frame damage and mismatched papers can make a vehicle unsafe or hard to register, so they matter more than worn wipers.

**Can I use it on a phone?** Yes, it is built for it. Print works from a computer.
