## What it does

Type your yearly salary and any deductions you claim, and the calculator works out your income tax for FY 2026-27 under both regimes at once. Each column shows the taxable income, the tax on the slabs, the Section 87A rebate, any surcharge, the 4% cess and the money left after tax. The cheaper regime is marked in green, with how much it saves you a year and a month. Everything is worked out in your browser.

Once you know your tax, plan where the rest goes with the [[budget-calculator|budget planner]], or compare with the [[canada-income-tax|Canada]] and [[uk-income-tax|UK tax calculators]].

## How to use it

1. Pick your **age**: the old regime gives seniors a higher tax-free amount.
2. Type your **gross salary** for the year, and any **other income** such as bank interest or rent.
3. Open **Old regime deductions** and type what you claim. The new regime ignores them, apart from its own Rs 75,000 standard deduction.

## What each part means

- **New regime**: the default. Slabs of 0% up to Rs 4 lakh, then 5%, 10%, 15%, 20% and 25% in Rs 4 lakh steps, and 30% above Rs 24 lakh.
- **87A rebate**: no tax if taxable income is up to Rs 12 lakh (new) or Rs 5 lakh (old). Just above Rs 12 lakh, marginal relief keeps the tax no higher than the income above Rs 12 lakh.
- **Surcharge**: 10% above Rs 50 lakh, 15% above Rs 1 crore, 25% above Rs 2 crore (old regime: 37% above Rs 5 crore), with marginal relief.
- **Cess**: 4% on tax plus surcharge.
- **Marginal rate**: the tax on your next Rs 1,000.

## Worked example

Asha earns Rs 15,00,000 and claims Rs 1,50,000 under 80C and Rs 25,000 under 80D. New regime: taxable Rs 14,25,000, tax Rs 93,750 plus Rs 3,750 cess = **Rs 97,500**. Old regime: taxable Rs 12,75,000, tax Rs 1,95,000 plus cess = **Rs 2,02,800**. The new regime saves her Rs 1,05,300.

A salary of Rs 12,75,000 pays no tax in the new regime: Rs 75,000 standard deduction leaves Rs 12 lakh, fully covered by the rebate.

## FAQs

**Which regime should I choose?** The one with less tax. The old regime only wins with large deductions, such as a big home loan and full 80C and 80D.

**Does the new Income-tax Act change my tax?** Not the slabs for 2026-27. Section numbers change, so your Form 16 may use new ones.

**Is anything saved?** No. Nothing you type leaves the page. Not financial or tax advice; check the official source.
