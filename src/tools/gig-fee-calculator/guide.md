## What it does

Most people undercharge for gigs because they count only the set. The real job also includes preparing the music, loading in, travelling and wear on your gear. This tool adds all of that up and tells you the smallest fee that still pays you your own hourly rate, plus a quote with a margin on top, the deposit to ask for, the balance due and what you actually keep.

It does not know what gigs pay in your area, so it never suggests a rate. You type the rate you want to keep. Compare gig income with the [[streaming-royalties]] estimator.

## How to use it

1. Pick your currency symbol, then enter set length, prep hours, travel hours and the hourly rate you want to keep.
2. Open **More options** to enter distance and cost per kilometre or mile, equipment hire, gear wear, agent cut, tax set-aside, margin and deposit.
3. Read the quote and the table. The effective hourly rate shows what you keep per hour.

## What each option does

- **Minimum fee**: the lowest fee where you still keep your rate for every hour. Do not go below it.
- **Margin**: extra on top of the minimum for the quote, so there is room to negotiate.
- **Agent or booking cut**: taken as a share of the whole fee.
- **Tax set-aside**: a share of what is left after the agent and your costs, to put away. It is not tax advice.
- **Deposit and balance**: the part to ask for upfront and the rest due on the night.

## Worked example

A 2-hour set with 3 hours of prep and 1 hour of travel is 6 hours. At a rate of 30 to keep, that is 180. Costs are 50 (a 50 km trip at 0.50 per km is 25, hire 20, wear 5). With a 20% tax set-aside and a 10% agent, the minimum fee is (180 / 0.8 + 50) / 0.9 = 305.56. With a 10% margin the quote is 336.11, a 30% deposit is 100.83, the balance is 235.28 and you keep 202.00, which is 33.67 per hour.

## FAQs

**How do I choose my rate?** Think about what you need per hour and what your skill and demand are worth, then ask people in your scene. The page has a short guide.

**Why is the effective rate higher than mine?** The margin adds extra on top.

**Are my numbers saved?** No. Nothing is stored or sent.
