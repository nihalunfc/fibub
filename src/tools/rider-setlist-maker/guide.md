## What it does

A rider is the page you send a venue or promoter before a gig. It says what you need in the booth, what power and hospitality you expect, how you travel and how you get paid. This page builds one from a short form, adds a setlist with title, artist, BPM, key and notes, and shows a clean page that you can print or download as PDF, Word or an image.

Gear is described in plain words, such as "2 media players" or "booth monitor", never by brand, so it works for any venue. Work out your price first with the [[gig-fee-calculator]], plan the night with the [[dj-set-planner]], and run through the room on the day with the [[soundcheck-list]].

## How to use it

1. Fill in the gig details, then tick the gear you need and edit any line.
2. Fill in power, hospitality, travel and payment terms.
3. Add tracks to the setlist. Use **Up** and **Down** to reorder. The running time updates.
4. Check the preview, then press **Print** or a download button.

## What each part does

- **Length** is `m:ss` or a number of minutes. It gives the running time and each track's start.
- **Remember my rider**: off by default. When ticked, the form is kept in this browser only. **Clear saved rider** deletes it.
- Empty sections are left out of the page.

## Worked example

Three tracks of 4:30, 5:00 and 3:45 give a running time of **13:15**, and they start at 0:00, 4:30 and 9:30. Only filled tracks are counted.

## FAQs

**Is a rider a contract?** No. It is a request. Anything you must have should also be in the agreement.

**Why are the gear lines generic?** So any venue can read them. Add a model name yourself if you need an exact one.

**Is it uploaded?** No. Everything is made on your device.
