## What it does

A split sheet is a short record of who made a song and what share each person owns. Fill in the song, add everyone who helped, and give each person a share of the **writing** (the song itself: melody, lyrics, chords) and of the **master** (the finished recording). Each column must add up to exactly 100, and the page tells you in plain words if it does not.

It is a template, not legal advice, and it is not a contract until everyone signs it. Agree the numbers before the song is released, then work out the money with the [[streaming-royalties]] estimator.

## How to use it

1. Type the song title, check the date and add the ISRC if you already have one.
2. Press **Add person** for each writer or producer. Enter a name, a role, the society they are registered with (if any) and an IPI number if they know it.
3. Type the writing % and master %, or press **Split equally** to share both columns evenly. With three people that gives 33.34, 33.33 and 33.33 so the total is still 100.
4. When the total line turns green, download the **PDF**, **Word** or **Image**, print it and sign on the lines. **Download CSV** gives a spreadsheet of the same table.

## What each field does

- **Writing %**: the share of the song as written. Writing is often split between writers and publishers by the collecting society.
- **Master %**: the share of the recording. A producer or a performer may have a master share without a writing share.
- **PRO or society, IPI**: where each person is registered, so money can be matched to them later. Optional.
- **Remember this sheet** (under More options): off by default. When ticked, the sheet stays in this browser only, and **Clear saved sheet** removes it.

## Worked example

Three people made a track: Ana wrote it, Ben produced it, Cy co-wrote. Press **Split equally**: writing 33.34, 33.33, 33.33 and master 33.34, 33.33, 33.33. Then change Ben to writing 0 and master 40, Ana to writing 50 master 30, and Cy to writing 50 master 30. Both columns now read 100 and the line turns green.

## FAQs

**Does a split sheet replace a contract?** No. It records what people agreed. For anything involving a label, publisher or large sums, get legal advice.

**Why two columns?** The song and the recording are owned and paid separately, so they are usually agreed separately.

**Does it need to be 100 exactly?** Yes for each column, so nothing is left unclaimed or claimed twice.

**Is my data uploaded?** No. Everything stays on your device.
