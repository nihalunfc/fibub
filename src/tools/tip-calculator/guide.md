## What it does

Type the bill, pick a tip and see the total and what each person pays. It handles the usual awkward bits: tax, a tip worked out before or after tax, a service charge that is already on the bill, and a group that wants to pay the same amount each.

It works in any currency, because only the numbers matter. For other percentage sums, use the [[percentage-calculator]]; to plan the month's spending, the [[budget-calculator|budget planner]].

## How to use it

1. Type the **bill before tax** and the **tax rate** (use 0 if the tax is already hidden in the price).
2. Tap a tip button (10, 15, 18 or 20%) or type your own in **Custom**.
3. Enter how many people are splitting.
4. Read what each person pays, with the full breakdown below.

## What each option does

- **Tip on the bill before tax / with tax**: many people tip on the pre-tax amount; some tip on the final total. The tip is bigger on the second choice.
- **Service charge already included**: if the bill already has a service charge, the tip is left out so you do not pay twice.
- **Round up what each person pays**: each person pays a whole number. The few pennies over simply become a slightly bigger tip, and the page tells you how much.

## Worked example

Four friends have a bill of 100, with no tax. At 15% the tip is 15, the total is 115, and each person pays 28.75. With a 10% tax on the bill and the tip worked out on the bill with tax, the 20% tip is 22, the total is 132, and each pays 33.

## FAQs

**How much should I tip?** It depends on the country, and on the kind of place. In some places 15 to 20% is normal; in others a service charge is built in and tipping is a small extra or not expected at all.

**Should I tip before or after tax?** Either is accepted in most places. Before tax is the stricter reading of "a percentage of the food".

**Can I split unevenly?** Not here: it splits equally. For uneven splits, work out each person's share first.
