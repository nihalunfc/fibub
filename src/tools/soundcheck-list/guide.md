## What it does

A sound check is the short slot before doors open when you set up, test the gear and agree the levels with the venue. This page gives you a tick list for it, grouped in the order you do things: arrive and load in, power and cables, mixer and players, booth monitor, main PA, recording and after the set.

Every item can be edited, removed or added to, so the list fits your own setup. A bar shows how much is done. Plan your night with the [[dj-set-planner]], check what you will be exposed to with the [[hearing-timer]], and send the venue your needs with the [[rider-setlist-maker]].

## How to use it

1. Type the venue name and the date (both optional).
2. Tick items as you go. Click an item's text to reword it, or **Remove** to drop it.
3. Press **+ Add an item** in any group for your own item.
4. Use **Print** for paper, or **Copy as text** to paste it into a message or notes app.

## What each button does

- **Untick all**: clears the ticks but keeps your edits, ready for the next gig.
- **Reset list**: goes back to the starting items and removes your edits.
- **Remember my list on this device**: off by default. When ticked, the list is kept in this browser only. **Clear saved list** deletes it.

## Worked example

You arrive at a bar with a list of 28 items and tick 8 of them while setting up. The page shows **8 of 28 done (29%)** and the bar fills about 29%. After the set you press **Untick all**, and the list is ready for the next night.

## FAQs

**What does "keep peaks out of the red" mean?** Each channel and the master have a level meter. The top lights are red. If they light up regularly the sound can distort, so turn the gain or the level down.

**Why keep the booth monitor low?** Loud monitors tire your ears and make it harder to judge the room. Set it only as loud as you need to hear the beat. See the [[hearing-timer]] for how listening time and loudness relate.

**Is my list uploaded?** No. Nothing leaves your device.
