## Everything for one night, together

On a clear night you might need four or five tools: when it gets dark, where the planets are, when your targets are high, where Polaris goes in the polar scope, which eyepiece to use, and a sheet to write on. This page puts the ones you choose together, for the same place and night. You can download them as **one** image, PDF or Word file to print or keep on your phone, or download **any part on its own**.

## How to use it

1. **Set your place and the night** at the top. Your location stays on your device.
2. **Tick what you want** in your session:
   - **Sky tonight:** sunset, dark hours, the Moon, a chart of the planets and the best deep-sky objects for what you observe with.
   - **Target heights:** a chart and table for your own list, such as `M31, M45, M42, Saturn`, with meridian times and airmass.
   - **Polar alignment:** where Polaris (or Sigma Octantis) sits in the polar scope at the time you plan to set up.
   - **Telescope, eyepieces and camera:** power, true field and exit pupil for each eyepiece, plus camera scale and field.
   - **Sky events this month:** Moon phases, meteor showers, eclipses and planet events.
   - **Blank observing log sheet:** a printable table to fill in at the telescope.
3. Fill in the **details** for the parts you picked. Only the fields you need are shown.
4. Download **All together** as one file, or use **Just this part** under any section. Each section also links to its full tool, where you can do more.

## Tips

- Print the PDF on paper, or keep the image on your phone with the **Red light** mode of fibub's astronomy tools for the screen.
- The page remembers your choices for this tab, so you can switch to a full tool and come back.
- Making a plan for a club night? The Word file is easy to edit and add your own notes to before you share it.

## Worked example

For a Saturday in November from Toronto, tick Sky tonight, Target heights and the log sheet. Type `M31, M45, M42, Saturn` and choose **Small telescope**. The page shows dark from about 18:40, Saturn best in the evening, M31 almost overhead at 21:52, and M42 highest at about 02:45. Press **PDF** under All together for a three-page plan with a 12-row log sheet at the end.

## Questions

**Is anything uploaded?** No. Every part is worked out in your browser, and the page keeps working offline once it has loaded.

**Can I add my own targets by coordinates?** On this page, targets come from the catalogue (Messier, bright NGC and IC objects, planets and the Moon). For anything else, use the target altitude planner, which accepts `name, RA, Dec`.
