## What it does

Pick the kind of paper you want, set the gap between lines, and download a PDF ready to print. It makes lined, graph, dot grid, isometric, hexagon and music staff paper, on A4, Letter or A5, in portrait or landscape.

Tick more than one kind and each gets its own page. Everything runs on your device and nothing is uploaded. If you are unsure which paper size to use, look at [[paper-sizes]]; to add page numbers afterwards use [[page-numbers]]; and to combine pages into a pad use [[organise-pdf]].

## How to use it

1. Tick the kinds of paper you want and set the spacing for each.
2. Choose the page size, direction, colour and margin.
3. Check the preview, then download the PDF and print at 100%.

## What each setting does

- **Spacing**: the gap between lines or dots (or the side of a hexagon), in millimetres or inches.
- **Darker line every**: for graph paper, every Nth line is drawn heavier, like the 1 cm lines on 5 mm paper.
- **Pages of each**: how many pages of every ticked kind.
- **Colour**: light grey and light blue use less ink and let your writing stand out.
- **Margin**: the blank edge where nothing is drawn.

## Worked example

Tick Lined and Graph, keep 1 page each, and the PDF has 2 pages. Graph paper with 5 mm squares and a darker line every 5 has a heavier line each 25 mm.

## FAQs

**Why does the grid not measure right?** Print at 100% or "actual size", not "fit to page".

**Can I change the unit?** Yes. Switch to inches and the numbers convert. A common graph size is 0.25 in.

**Does it work offline?** Yes, once the page is open.
